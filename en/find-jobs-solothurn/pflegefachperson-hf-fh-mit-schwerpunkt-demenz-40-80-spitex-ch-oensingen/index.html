Full description

Care professional HF with focus on dementia 60-100 % On behalf of the municipalities, Spitex Obwalden provides comprehensive Spitex services throughout the Canton of Obwalden. We offer professional support in the areas of care, psychiatric care, housekeeping and maternal and paternal counselling – competent, reliable and with heart. Spitex Obwalden is also an attractive training company and invests in the specialists of tomorrow. Do you like working independently and enjoying outpatient care? Then you are exactly right with us. Your tasks

  • Contact person for customers, relatives and employees in all questions regarding dementia
  • Ensuring a high quality of care for our customers at home
  • Clarification of needs after interRAI until completion of the care process as a case-leading nurse
  • Taking on nursing and administrative tasks
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with employees, specialist bodies and doctors
  • Project work and assumption of additional tasks

Your profile

  • Training as a nurse HF
  • Ideally with further education in the field of dementia or willingness to further education in this field
  • Outpatient professional experience is beneficial
  • Appreciative communication culture and mastery of the German language in words and writing
  • interRai HC course or willingness to complete it

We offer

  • A versatile, interesting and responsible activity
  • Progressive conditions of employment
  • Internal training opportunities

Are you interested? For information, Michaela Bissig, Head of Care (041 662 90 90), is at your disposal. We look forward to your application! Please send your complete application documents by post or mail to Spitex Obwalden, Personaladministration, Kägiswilerstrasse 29, 6060 Sarnen or personal@spitexow.ch.
