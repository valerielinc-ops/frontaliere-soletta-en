How about having time for your patients again in geriatrics – and still making a career? As a senior physician or senior physician, respectively, Senior physician Internal Medicine with destination LA, you lead your own department with permanent assistance in a manageable rehabilitation clinic, set up a consultation hour and usually have work at 5 pm. Her new place of work is an established rehabilitation clinic in the canton of Aargau, which has consciously remained manageable with around 400 employees. It is small enough that you know each other quickly, and large enough for a wide range of geriatric, musculoskeletal and internistic-oncological rehabilitation. X-ray, laboratory, an in-house pharmacy, psychology and nutritional advice are in the house. The clinic is a SIWF-certified training institution and focuses on short decision-making processes instead of hierarchical thinking. Because the stays are planned and last several weeks, there is time for medicine instead of throughput. You really get to know your patients and accompany them back to an independent life. The clinic is close to nature on the Aare, in the middle of a local recreation area, and yet central. From the nearest regional centre, Interregio trains travel half-hourly to Zurich and Basel. Who comes from southern Germany, has a short wire to the old homeland. The job is suitable for everyone who wants to progress professionally without having to live in the big city.

  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and leading young colleagues
