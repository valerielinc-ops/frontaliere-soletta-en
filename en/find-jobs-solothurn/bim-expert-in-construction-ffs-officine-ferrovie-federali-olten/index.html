  • Move Switzerland with us. BIM expert: in construction Olten / Work Smart, September 1st, 2026 or by appointment, 60-100% Apply now ## You can look forward to this. We are driving forward the digital transformation in railway construction and are looking for an experienced, implementation-oriented personality who, as a BIM expert in construction, will take on technical responsibility for BIM topics in the area of ​​roadways. You ensure that BIM tools, processes and data provide real benefits – economically, practically and sustainably. You can move that.
  • As an experienced construction manager, civil engineer or construction project manager, you will use your BIM expertise specifically to increase the added value of our BIM and development projects.
  • You support the creation of introduction strategies for the various BIM development steps and their introduction to employees in the area. At the same time, you will help set up the required technical operational and support organizations.
  • You will be responsible for communication between the roadway department and coordinate the implementation of your development projects with the other departments. You regularly inform your stakeholders and management about the current status.
  • You identify technical, procedural and data technology requirements and ensure their implementation in appropriate BIM applications, processes, component libraries and data.
  • You strengthen the BIM skills of the teams along the process chain, ensure the broad transfer of knowledge and, together with the BIM organization, drive efficient digital project management for punctual, customer-centric rail operations. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are an initiative, solution-oriented doer personality who takes responsibility and actively promotes change management in our organization. The ability to work in a team, interdisciplinary work and economic thinking are a given for you. You are well connected in the construction industry.
  • You translate complex requirements into understandable, implementable concepts and confidently present them to stakeholders at various levels.
  • Higher education (FH/University) in engineering or construction, in-depth construction know-how and knowledge of the normative requirements are required.
  • You have a good understanding of processes and experience in the application and configuration of Common Data Environments (CDE) and BIM planning tools. You also have experience in using AI to digitize business processes.
  • You speak French or German (min. C1), but you can communicate well in the other language orally/in writing (B2). ## Your salary and your benefits.
