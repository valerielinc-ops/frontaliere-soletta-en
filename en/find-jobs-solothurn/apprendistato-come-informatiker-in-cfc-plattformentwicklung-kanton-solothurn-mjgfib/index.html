The Office of Informatics and Organization (AIO) is the central point of contact for all computer science issues and is responsible for the implementation of computer science projects as well as consulting and support of the departments. With us at the Solothurn site you have the possibility from 01.08.2027 of a versatile and varied training for the computer science engineer/in EFZ platform development.

Your responsibility

  • As a platform developer, you talk to the users and understand their needs. You implement them technically, creatively.
  • You'll be implementing server and network systems, monitoring and waiting for them, and you'll be working on support requests.
  • You'll be dealing with the current cloud solutions.
  • You'll take on users' problems and find a suitable solution with them.
  • In your work, you'll make preliminary explanations, elaborate detailed concepts and find solutions for innovations.
