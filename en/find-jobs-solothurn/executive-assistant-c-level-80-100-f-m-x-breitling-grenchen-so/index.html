Full description

We are a dedicated Squad on a mission to redefine Swiss watchmaking, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision we create timepieces that combine modern-retro design with casual, approachable, and sustainable luxury — powered by passionate teams around the world. Join our vibrant team as an Executive Assistant C-level (80-100%) (f/m/x) to be part of something extraordinary! You will provide high-level executive support to several members of the Executive Committee, with a primary focus on supporting the Chief Operations Officer (80%) and the Chief Marketing Officer (20%). You will play a key role in ensuring the smooth coordination of executive activities across the Operations and Marketing departments by managing schedules, organizing travel and events, coordinating meetings, and serving as a trusted administrative partner. This position is based between La Chaux-de-Fonds and Grenchen, with one day per week in Zurich .
