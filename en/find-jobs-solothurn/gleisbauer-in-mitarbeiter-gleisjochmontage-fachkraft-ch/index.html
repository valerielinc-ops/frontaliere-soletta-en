Full description

Activities

  • Manufacture and assembly of railway technology components according to technical drawings and working specifications
  • Reading and understanding of turnout drawings, track pull drawings, installation plans and type plans
  • Working according to installation instructions for drives of railway crossovers
  • Distributing and precise positioning of thresholds using a crane
  • Marking of rail classification according to technical specifications
  • Provision and mounting of various fastening materials
  • Professional tightening of fixtures with a torque machine
  • Ensure the required quality and quantity

Requirements

  • Experience in assembly, metal processing or in track construction
  • Good understanding of technical drawings and assembly plans
  • Handicraft skills and technical understanding
  • Practiced and safe handling of hand tools
  • Experience in handling cranes
  • Physical resilience and pleasure in practical work
  • Efficient, reliable and responsible working methods

Offer

  • A varied and responsible activity in the production of railway technology components. You work according to clear technical specifications and make an important contribution to the quality and safety of railway traffic with your careful work.
