You have acquired your specialist degree in gynecology and obstetrics and are looking for the next step? Here you will assume direct medical responsibility in the operating room, in the bearroom and in the ambulatorium, in a collegial team and with a place of residence between Jura, Aare and Alps. The women’s clinic of a modern center hospital in the Swiss Mittelland accompanies women through all phases of life. The spectrum ranges from desire to have children, lowering, incontinence and bladder diseases, fibroids and endometriosis to gynecological oncology. A certified, interdisciplinary breast center is part of the clinic. In maternity care, the team relies on family-oriented care, is certified as a baby-friendly hospital according to UNICEF and WHO and offers, among other things, the window caesarean section. They work in a new hospital building with modern infrastructure. Bern, Basel and Zurich can be reached easily by train, and Aare and Jura are right on the doorstep.

Their tasks

  • Care of patients across the entire spectrum, from endometriosis and urogynecology to gynecological oncology and obstetrics
  • Medical practice in the operating room, on the bed station and in the ambulatory
  • Care of gynecological and obstetric emergencies
  • Guidance and promotion of assistant doctors: inside
  • Co-design of the processes in the Women's Clinic

Your Competences

  • Specialist title in gynecology and obstetrics, also freshly acquired
  • Enjoyment across the spectrum, including oncology, uromynecology and senology
  • Structured, responsible working methods and team spirit
  • Very good knowledge of German (at least C1) and openness to Swiss German
  • For foreign diploma: MEBEKO recognition or advanced procedure. Medimeo supports you with advice and financial support.

Their advantages

  • Upper medical function from the specialist title, with real responsibility from the beginning
  • Wage in Swiss francs with 13 monthly wages, possible benefit bonus and pension provision via the pension fund
  • Permanent employment, entry by agreement
  • Pensum between 50 and 100%, with room for career as for later family plans
  • Own nurseries and possible childcare allowance if the time has come
  • Collegial team with cooperation at eye level
  • Internal training, health promotion and employee benefits
