Vehicle electricians Commercial vehicles (m/f/d | 100%) HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 500 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to an extensive bus model program, HESS projects, builds and sells commercial vehicle bodies and conversions of all kinds. To complement our commercial vehicles division in Subingen, we are looking for a motivated and experienced vehicle electrician for the production team. Are you fascinated by the world of vehicles and are you familiar with vehicle electronics? Then you are exactly right with us! With us you take the reins yourself – They implement ambitious projects independently and professionally. Your exciting tasks include

  • Focus on the latest vehicle technology: From the new installation of vehicle-specific cabling to the integration of complex components in transporters, commercial vehicles and trailers You are our expert!
  • Innovation in security and information: Installing and cabling state-of-the-art security and information systems to make vehicles even smarter
  • Specialist for comfort and performance: installation of air suspension systems and implementation of loads for optimal performance and driving comfort
  • Detective work in diagnostics: Precise error analysis and diagnostics to overcome complex challenges and find solutions
  • Your plus point: Electroschema expert (no MUST): Ideally, you have knowledge in drawing electroschemas – but this is not an exclusion criterion, but a welcome bonus.
  • Organizational talent in demand: Careful documentation and storage of the work carried out for a smooth workflow
  • Co-creation and innovation: Bring your fresh ideas and actively shape the future of our company! Our requirements
  • Completed basic vocational training as an automotive mechatronic engineer (vehicle electrician), automotive specialist (car or commercial vehicle), agricultural machinery or construction machinery mechanic
  • Professional experience in vehicle electrics
  • Willingness to learn something new
  • Self-employed and reliable working methods
  • Flexibility and teamwork We offer them
  • Responsible and varied permanent position with a lot of self-responsibility
  • Interesting, varied and challenging tasks
  • Modern working environment
