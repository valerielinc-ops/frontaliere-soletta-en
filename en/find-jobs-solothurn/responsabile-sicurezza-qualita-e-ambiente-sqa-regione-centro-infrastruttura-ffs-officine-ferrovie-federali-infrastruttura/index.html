Head of Security, Quality and Environment (SQA) - Central Region, Infrastructure Candidates now » Date: 16 Nov 2026

Place

Olten, CH

Company

SBB What we offer you. In this management function you will contribute significantly to defining safety, quality and environment (SQA), in collaboration with the operational organizations of the Infrastructure divisions, with a practical and appropriate approach to the different recipients. In collaboration with management teams, SQA projects and specialists, you will support the implementation of our security and quality objectives in the Central Region. You will work in a varied context, between operational yards, issues related to the exercise, conceptual and strategic projects and directives. What you can move.

  • Together with your team, you will provide advice, training and introduction of directives, tools and processes in the field of safety, quality and environment for infrastructure sectors (maintenance, projects, plant management and exercise) in the Central Region.
  • You will be responsible for analysis of events, analyses and reporting on safety indicators, as well as training and examination of operational safety functions. You will also post line managers during security checks and audits.
  • In close collaboration with the bodies responsible for the elaboration of directives and with the other three regional units Safety, quality and environment Infrastructure, you will develop regional directives and provide support in the projects and concrete issues related to the security of the partners of the region.
  • Take advantage of your solid contacts with the region's Infrastructure division management teams to provide proven solutions and quickly identify opportunities and risks.
  • You will develop and implement practical tools: from field safety campaigns to environmental control lists and Kaizen initiatives.
  • You will take care of third-party subcontractors and yards and contribute to the development of the SQA regional unit, taking into account the corporate management principles.

What you bring with you.

  • Thanks to your strong communication skills, to your great empathy, to the ability to adopt different perspectives and to your security, you will be able to involve the different interest groups in a manner that suits their needs.
  • You have a first experience in results-oriented management and development in a technical context or in specialized safety management.
  • In your previous functions you have gained experience in designing and conducting trainings and workshops as well as introducing standards and consulting.
  • You have excellent knowledge of the standards and safety standards in the rail sector, in particular the safety of shipyards and workplaces (RTE 20100).
  • You have a university degree or a professional university school (SUP) in business economy or in a technical field, or equivalent training, and have practical experience in managing processes and projects.
  • For this function excellent oral and written knowledge of German are required, at least at level C1. Knowledge of other national languages is an advantage.
