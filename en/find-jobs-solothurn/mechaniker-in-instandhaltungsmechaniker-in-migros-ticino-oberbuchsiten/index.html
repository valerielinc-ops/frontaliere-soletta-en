Full description

With our new production site in Oberbuchsiten, we are creating a modern production environment for the future. For this we are looking for a committed personality who, with technical understanding, craftsmanship and reliable working methods, actively participates in the construction, commissioning and sustainable operation of our mechanical plants. Mansioni

  • You will be responsible for the assembly, maintenance and maintenance of mechanical plants and systems.
  • You carry out repairs and preventive maintenance work independently and professionally.
  • Analyze malfunctions on mechanical assemblies, correct errors and support smooth production operations.
  • You exchange mechanical components, align them and adjust them precisely.
  • You safely read technical drawings, parts lists and assembly plans and implement them in practice.
  • You carefully document work, maintenance and repairs and ensure compliance with safety and quality guidelines.
  • You are actively involved in the commissioning of a new production building and support in the assembly, alignment and adjustment of production plants, machines and conveyor technology.
  • Requisiti
  • You ideally bring several years of professional experience: in an industrial or production environment.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): as a mechanic, polymechanic, production mechanic or a comparable qualification.
  • You have experience or knowledge in the commissioning of industrial plants or new buildings as well as in dealing with production and conveyor systems.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of mechanics, mechanical engineering and maintenance as well as basic knowledge of pneumatics and hydraulics.
  • You are familiar with the relevant Swiss regulations and guidelines, in particular SUVA, EKAS and the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC (CH-compliant).
  • You work independently, structured and reliable, bring a high level of security awareness and communicate clearly in the team.
  • Ideally, you have the category B driver's license and you are ready for pikettdienst.
