Commercial vehicle sales clerk (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 6,000 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our modular system “Co-Bolt”. We are the only Swiss bus manufacturer to go far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The culture of innovation is the fuel of our company.

To complement our team in the commercial vehicle sector, we are looking for a motivated and experienced clerk for internal sales. Do you have experience in vehicle construction and would you like to use your practical knowledge in an exciting sales job? A challenging job awaits you in our commercial vehicles division, in which you, as the person responsible for order processing, are an important link to our customers. Your tasks

  • Sales of spare parts for vehicle bodies from the HESS Group
  • Receiving and reviewing customer concerns as well as assessing feasibility in collaboration with production management
  • Creation of offers as well as preliminary and final calculations
  • Technical and administrative order processing
  • Execution, scheduling and support of orders in collaboration with the technical project manager and the production manager
  • Assisting in the creation of customer documents, brochures and price lists
  • Assisting in the organization of trade fairs and exhibitions

Our requirements

  • Completed basic training as a designer, vehicle fitter or automotive mechatronics engineer
  • Professional experience in vehicle construction
  • Technical training
  • Several years of experience in a comparable position
  • Experienced use of ERP systems and MS Office (ideally also knowledge of 3D CAD)
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German (French is an advantage)
  • Strong sales flair
  • Strong organizational skills, assertiveness and a results-oriented way of working

We offer you

  • Varied and responsible work
  • Exciting challenge in vehicle construction
  • Flexible working hours
  • Performance-based employment conditions
