Working Student Asset Servicing 40-60

Olten, 40-60%, reference 8384

You want to gain real insights into an international financial environment and actively shape it instead of just running along with your studies? Then you are exactly right with us!

Asset Servicing is a highly diverse department with specialized teams at the locations of Switzerland and Spain. Together, we ensure that our services in the areas of Corporate Actions, Alaska Processing and Proxy Ling are provided reliably and efficiently. As a working student, you work directly in the daily operational business – and you also get the opportunity to analyze processes, identify optimization potential and bring your own ideas.

As a modern, dynamic and innovative company, we offer a variety of opportunities to develop your potential in the exciting world of technology and finance. We support you in your career entry, invest in your development and expand your personal skills. As a "Working Student" until you are part of our Young Talents community and are part of a network of dedicated career starters. This job is limited to two years.

Tasks

  • Work in the daily business in various areas of asset servicing (91 Actions, Hawaii, Proxy Voting)
  • Support for the high-quality and timely processing of customer orders
  • Analysis of processes and cooperation in the further development of Proezssen
  • To bring in own suggestions for improvement and support in their implementation
  • Cooperation with several teams and international interfaces

Requirements

  • Current Bachelor's or Master's degree (e.g. Economics, Finance, Banking, Data/Analytics or comparable)
  • A completed banking apprenticeship, experience in the field of securities services are beneficial
  • Analytic thinking and joy in questioning and improving things
  • Above-average customer and service orientation
  • Self-employed, structured and reliable working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

We offer

  • Part of the Young Talents Community
  • Part of a network of dedicated career starters
  • Support for career entry
  • Investing in your development and expanding your personal skills
