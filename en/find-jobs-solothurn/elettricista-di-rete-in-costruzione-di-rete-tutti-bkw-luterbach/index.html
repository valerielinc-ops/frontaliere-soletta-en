Full description

Your tasks

  • You build, operate and maintain together with your colleagues: the distribution network of BKW
  • Assembly work is just as much a part of your tasks as the maintenance and troubleshooting of our plants in the low- and medium-voltage distribution network with a focus on 132-0.4kV and long-range and regulated overhead lines.
  • You lay cable lines, maintain overhead lines and are also there when it is necessary to repair damage (picking and disruption service)

You bring that with you

  • Training as a grid electrician:in EFZ, electrical installer:in EFZ or assembly electrician:in EFZ is an advantage. Also with a craftsmanship background and the willingness to familiarize yourself with the area, you are welcome with us.
  • Experience in electrical installations or network construction
  • Independent, reliable working method and enjoyment of teamwork
  • Category B or C driving licence
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
