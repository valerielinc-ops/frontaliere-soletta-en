Development Engineer Vehicle Construction Bus & Trolley (m/f/d | 100%) HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 500 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company. In order to meet the growing demand for electrically driven buses, we are strengthening our technical and development teams. We are looking for a dynamic and motivated development engineer for the Design Bodywork group. Do you have a flair for electromobility and see yourself as a generalist? Take this opportunity to experience your future work results holistically with the only vehicle OEM in Switzerland and to be able to drive! Let’s create vehicles of tomorrow together. With your know-how, you can move thousands of people safely, comfortably and sustainably with electric mobility in public transport every day. The implementation of your ideas significantly influences the execution of the upcoming vehicles and are noticeable for every passenger as well as driver. For this, a wide range of technologies, requirements and possibilities have to be mastered. Are you ready? Their tasks

  • Constructing Mech. Structural elements for the bus body: welding assemblies, aluminum profiles, laser bent parts, screwed assemblies, thermoforming e.g. ABS deep-drawn parts, die casting, GRP
  • Designing the vehicle fairing for the exterior and interior
  • Integration and packaging of roof structures
  • Creating modular/scalable constructions for our entire bus portfolio from 10m to 25m
  • Selection and design of HVAC/cooling systems in close cooperation with our suppliers
  • FEM construction accompanying
  • Perform design reviews using development and change processes
  • Technical compliance and implementation of vehicle homologation requirements
  • Optimization of production and assembly processes from the point of view of development and construction
  • Support and instruct the internal interfaces with focus on production as well as AVOR, procurement and quality assurance Our requirements
  • Basic technical training
  • Training as an engineer FH Fahrzeugbau/Maschinenbau (or equivalent training)
  • Several years of professional experience in vehicle construction are advantageous
  • Experience with CAD (Solidworks 3D), FEM, PDM and ERP
  • German and English in word and writing
  • Self-employed and structured working methods
  • Team-capable and solution-oriented personality We offer them
  • Responsible, varied and challenging work in a growing environment
  • Large design possibilities
  • Support for training
  • Fast and short decision paths
  • Smooth, flexible working hours and possibility for home office
  • Performance-based conditions of employment If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including letters of motivation via link (Apply now).
