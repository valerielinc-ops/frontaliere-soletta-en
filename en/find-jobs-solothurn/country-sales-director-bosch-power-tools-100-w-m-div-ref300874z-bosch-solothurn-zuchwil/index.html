Full description

Your wich

To strengthen our sales team in Switzerland, we are looking for an experienced and entrepreneurial personality as a country sales manager for DIY and online business for power tools, garden tools, measurement technology and accessories. You are a sales expert with proven successes, who not only loves to recognize potential but also to quickly realize it with strategic skill and power? Then we offer you the opportunity to develop a strong team and important trading partners and actively shape growth.

Your main tasks

  • Customer development & support: You develop and establish sustainable business relationships with defined key customers with an experienced team of Key Account Manager.
  • Overall responsibility & leadership: You bear full responsibility for sales, costs and margins of the defined area and actively control the product life cycle strategically and operationally. As a role model and coach, you motivate your team to perform top performance and develop it continuously.
  • Negotiation: You conduct annual discussions and negotiations at decision-making level with your team.

The design and maintenance of the corresponding contracts is also part of your field of responsibility. - Market analysis & strategy: Through precise market and customer analyses, you identify the relevant growth segments. You balance them with customer needs in order to derive and implement strategies that are appropriate to potential. - Customer activities & POS: You plan and implement customer-specific activities with your team, from product range design to advertising, training, and effective point-of-sale measures and marketing campaigns.

  • Product launches: You and your team ensure the successful introduction of new products and campaigns and position our innovative overall range of products in the Swiss construction and online market. - Interface management: As a member of the Swiss management team, you act as an important interface and ensure close, coordinated cooperation with international sales managers and key account managers, local and international marketing and business units.
  • Project responsibility: You take over the management of projects such as services, alternative sales channels, logistics optimization or Category Management.

Your profile

  • Training: You have a solid basic commercial or technical training which you have supplemented with a higher level of further education (e.g. HF/FH in business administration, sales management). A university degree is a plus, but not a requirement. - Experience & know-how: You have a minimum of 5-10 years of professional experience in sales and marketing and first management (as sales manager or key account manager), ideally in an industry-related environment. The experienced handling of common IT applications, especially MS Excel and PowerPoint, is a matter of course for you.
  • Personality and working methods: You are a convincing, winning personality with entrepreneurial and proactive action. You can identify growth opportunities, build strong relationships and take responsibility for results from the first day to translate impulses into measurable successes. - Languages: Swiss German or German you control in the first half of the year. You also bring very good English skills with you, good French is an advantage.
