Full description

The Department of Education, Culture and Sport (DBKS) of the Canton of Solothurn comprises the Primary School Office, the Office for Vocational Education, Central and Higher Education, the Office for Culture and Sports and the Departmental Secretariat. The departmental secretariat is the hub and interface between the departmental management and the offices. It includes legal service, controlling, scholarship, communication and administration. For the administration department, we are looking for a case handler with a focus on HR, 50% at the Solothurn location on 01.11.2026 or by agreement.

Your responsibility

  • They support the Departmental Secretary in fulfilling her administrative and organisational tasks, especially in the area of HR. They also represent them in relevant working groups.
  • For internal and external HR issues, you are the contact person.
  • In the administration team, you and your colleagues are responsible for handling the business of the Government Council, business control, coordination and monitoring of all administrative tasks and processes as well as organizational tasks for the Department Secretariat.
  • They organise DBKS events with the administration team.

Your profile

  • They successfully completed a commercial training EFZ or equivalent training. You have already completed further training as an HR assistant and have professional experience, preferably in public administration and human resources.
  • Style-confident German in word and writing as well as very good MS-Office user knowledge distinguish you, ideally complemented by experience with CMI Axioma.
  • You are a service-oriented person, orderly, with organizational skill, exact, efficient and structured work.
  • A well-groomed appearance and a loyal attitude are just as much a matter of course for you as teamwork and discretion.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
