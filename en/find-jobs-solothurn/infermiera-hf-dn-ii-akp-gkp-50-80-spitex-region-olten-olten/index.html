Full description

Do you want to experience new care? Then you are exactly right with us. The Spitex Region Olten is no ordinary employer: We work in a team-organized manner – with flat hierarchies, a lot of personal responsibility and a strong cooperation. In a service area with 25,000 inhabitants, we provide around 24,000 hours per year in the areas of counselling, treatment care, basic care and home help. We enable our customers of all ages to live a self-determined life at home. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an Nurse HF (DN II/AKP/GKP) 50-80 %

What to expect

As a nurse, you take on the technically demanding care, care and preventive support of our customers in their usual environment. You carry out needs assessments (RAI-HC), coordinate with doctors and hospitals and advise relatives. You document the care process professionally and – if desired – contribute to the training and support of learners.

This is what you bring with you

  • Diploma in Care HF or equivalent education (DN II, AKP, GKP)
  • Practice trainer/in SVEB 1 or willingness to acquire
  • Outpatient professional experience is beneficial
  • Joy of self-responsibility and independent work
  • Motivation to help shape the new
  • Driving licence Cat. B

Your team

You work in a team-organized and interdisciplinary manner with 7-12 colleagues, who:

  • independently plans, organizes and decides
  • Flexible working hours (e.g. focus on evening or weekend services)
  • Select new team members independently
  • focuses on personal responsibility, trust and cooperation

What we offer you

  • A modern, future-oriented working environment

  • Attractive wages

What else we want from you

  • Reflection and a conscious professional approach to closeness and distance in cooperation with your clients
  • Willingness to change in the process with the team, this also includes conflict ability for us.
