Full description

Logistics specialist with knowledge of electrics (m/f/d | 100%) HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 500 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company. For our team in the Bus & Trolley division, we are looking for a motivated logistics specialist with knowledge in electrics for the Electric 24V group. In this exciting and versatile position, you are a central part of the production process for the electrification of our modern electric buses. They ensure that our electrical departments are always supplied with the right material, coordinate logistical processes and additionally support the professional cabling of the vehicles. The combination of logistics and assembly makes this task particularly varied – Boredom is not guaranteed here! Their tasks

  • Responsibility for KANBAN management and material logistics in production
  • Retrieve material wagons with the electrical material from the main warehouse and pre-sort them for the various electrical workstations
  • Build and prepare material for assembling TFT displays
  • Management of electrical defects at our production site in Portugal
  • Replace Defective Material and Write Complaints
  • Providing jobs and employees with consumables and tools
  • Coordination of the remaining material (agreement with main warehouse and independent booking back)
  • Procurement requests for missing material in consultation with AVOR
  • If necessary and suitable assumption of additional tasks in the AVOR
  • Assistance in the wiring and assembly of various electrical components in and on electric buses according to diagram
  • Preparation and prefabrication of small installation groups for assembly bus
  • In case of suitability and driving license C: assumption of additional tasks when maneuvering the buses possible Our requirements
  • Completed basic vocational training as a logistician or completed apprenticeship in an electrotechnical discipline (assembly electrician, automatic installer or other training in an electrical environment)
  • Broad expertise in the field of electrical components and their application
  • Professional experience in logistics
  • Self-employed and reliable working methods
  • Teamwork and flexibility
  • Interested in learning new things We offer them
  • Interesting and very varied activity for the production of innovative electric buses
  • Internal education and training
  • Permanent employment in a successful traditional company
  • Moving working time
  • Performance-based conditions of employment If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including letters of motivation via link (Apply now).
