Full description

Your tasks

  • As part of the team, you will get to know the business processes in controlling, especially in reporting and profit and loss accounting, and actively participate in the creation and analysis of monthly key figures.
  • You will support the preparation and development of decision and planning templates with your own ideas and proposed solutions.
  • You will be involved in the sales forecasting and tracking of our global accessories business, working closely with stakeholders from other functions.
  • You will use various systems (e.g. SAP BW, Oracle HFM) to create analyses and have the opportunity to help build new controlling tools.

Your profile

  • Education: Degree or recent degree in finance, controlling or comparable
  • Enthusiasm: Enjoyment and interest in dealing with numbers and working in a team
  • Personality and way of working: committed, proactive, flexible, communicative, precise and detailed way of working
  • Experience and know-how: very good MS Office skills, especially Excel, PowerPoint; analytical way of thinking, understanding of complex contexts
  • Languages: very good knowledge of German and English, both written and spoken

Contact & additional information

  • Start: Ideally from February 1, 2027 or by arrangement
  • Duration: 6.5 months or by arrangement
  • 40 hours/week
  • The prerequisite for the internship is enrollment at a university or a university degree that was not more than 6 months ago.
  • Please include your CV and a current certificate of enrollment with your application. Your benefits

  • In addition to your studies, gain your first experiences in professional life and develop personally with us.
