Role overview

The Department of Education, Culture and Sport (DBKS) of the Canton of Solothurn comprises the Primary School Office, the Office for Vocational Education, Central and Higher Education, the Office for Culture and Sports and the Departmental Secretariat.

The departmental secretariat is home to the central competence and focal point for communication, which advises and supports the department head, the department secretary and the office heads in all communication tasks.
