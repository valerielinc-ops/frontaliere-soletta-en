Full description

The activity includes the precise manufacture and assembly of railway engineering components. The company expects a careful working method and the safe handling of technical drawings. You will find here a responsible task with a clear working framework. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Manufacture and assembly of railway components according to technical drawings and work specifications
  • Reading and understanding of switch drawings, track yoke drawings, laying plans and type plans
  • Work according to installation instructions for drives of switches
  • Distributing and precisely positioning the sleepers using a crane
  • Marking of the rail division according to technical specifications
  • Provision and mounting of various fastening materials
  • Technical tightening of the attachments with a torque machine
  • Ensuring the required quality and quantity

Requirements

  • Experience in assembly, metal processing or track construction is advantageous
  • Good understanding of technical drawings and assembly plans
  • Craftsmanship and technical understanding
  • Practical and safe handling of hand tools
  • Experience in handling cranes is beneficial
  • Physical resilience and pleasure in practical work
  • Efficient, reliable and responsible working methods

Offer

  • A varied and responsible activity in the production of railway technology components. You work according to clear technical specifications and make an important contribution to quality and safety in rail transport with your careful work.
