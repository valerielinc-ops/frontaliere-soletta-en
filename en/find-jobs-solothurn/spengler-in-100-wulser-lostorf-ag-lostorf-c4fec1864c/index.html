Full description

Do you know us? We are a Swiss company with expertise in sanitary, heating and refrigeration technology as well as roofing and spenglerei. With our location in Lostorf and around 30 employees, we operate throughout the canton of Solothurn. We belong to the Burkhalter Group, the leading provider of cross-trade building technology in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein.
