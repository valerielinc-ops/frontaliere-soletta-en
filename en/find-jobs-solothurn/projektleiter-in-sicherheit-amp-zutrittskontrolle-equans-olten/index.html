Project manager*in Security & Access Control 100%

For our team in Olten, we are looking for a dedicated project manager in the field of security and access control who is passionate about developing innovative solutions and taking responsibility.

You can move that with us.

  • Develop customized customer solutions: You create convincing offers and thoughtful technical concepts.
  • Assume responsibility: You are responsible for the execution planning and commissioning of the most modern access and video systems.
  • Project management with foresight: You coordinate assignments, organize resources and ensure a smooth transfer to the customer.
  • Team leadership: You take over the assignment planning of the assigned employees.
  • Customer satisfaction as a top priority: You inspire our customers with professional work and first-class service.

That's what makes you.

  • Further education: Project manager SES or a comparable qualification with a federal certificate of advantage.
  • Practical experience: Solid experience in project management and the implementation of complex technical systems.
  • Digital Affinity: Very good MS Office knowledge and the ability to quickly familiarize yourself with new IT systems.
  • Personal strengths: independence, personal responsibility, flexibility and a strong organizational talent.
  • Linguistic competence: Knowledge of a second national language is an advantage.
  • Mobility: willingness to travel and driving licence Cat. B.
  • Customer and service orientation: You think and act in the interests of our customers and bring a safe, professional appearance.

That's what we offer you.

  • Diverse challenges: Exciting projects with renowned companies, authorities and private customers.
  • Design leeway: High personal responsibility and the opportunity to develop innovative solutions.
  • A strong team: A competent, collegial and motivated environment that supports you.
  • Technological progress: A modern IT environment with high-quality work equipment.
  • Attractive benefits: Above-average social benefits, an excellent education and training offer as well as a company vehicle (also for private use).
  • Structured familiarization: You will be professionally introduced to your new role and receive the support you need to succeed.

Take the step into an exciting and dynamic environment and send us your complete application documents via CareerCenter on our homepage. We look forward to seeing you!
