Full description

The Staff Office is responsible as a transversal office for all personnel matters of the cantonal administration. The Legal Service Department is the contact point for all personnel law issues in the cantonal administration. For the Legal Service department, we are looking for a lawyer, 80%, immediately or by agreement at the Solothurn location.

Your responsibility

  • In this position, you will be advised and supported by the personnel office and managers of the individual departments in all labour and personnel law issues.
  • As an internal sparring partner, you accompany personnel law processes and measures from employment to dismissal.
  • They take over the legal examination and processing of a wide range of topics in the field of personnel law and participate in legislative work.
  • The Staff Office represents you in administrative procedures, vis-à-vis authorities, social partners and in labour court proceedings.

Your profile

  • They have a university degree in law (lic.iur. or MLaw) and have several years of professional experience in the legal environment, ideally in public personnel and administrative law.
  • For challenging questions, you can draw on your broad expertise and your in-depth knowledge of human resources / employment law.
  • A self-responsible and precise working method, supplemented by a high level of social competence, distinguishes you and counts among your strengths.
  • You see yourself as an internal service provider with a high willingness to perform and work solution- and team-oriented. Negotiation skills as well as a strong communication and consulting competence in German round off your profile.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
