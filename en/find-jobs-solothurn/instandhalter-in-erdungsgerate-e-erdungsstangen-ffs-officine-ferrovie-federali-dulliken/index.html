Maintenance worker: in grounding devices and grounding rods 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Maintainer:in grounding devices and grounding rods Dulliken, November 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. Would you like to take on a responsible role in an innovative and safety-relevant environment? Then you've come to the right place. In this role, you will make a significant contribution to the safety of employees when working on electrified railway systems. You will develop step by step into a specialist in grounding devices and grounding rods and work with modern infrastructure and professional work equipment. You can expect a thorough introduction to grounding and protection devices, targeted further development and a motivated, collegial team. With your work, you ensure that colleagues can work safely on the railway infrastructure every day. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • also for the family
  • on our website under Benefits. You can move that.
  • You will independently undertake the demanding maintenance, servicing, repair and revision of ground rods, ground fittings, voltage testers and other safety-relevant equipment.
  • You check the function, carry out safety-related inspections in accordance with regulations and, in the event of malfunctions, take appropriate corrective measures so that the equipment remains ready for use.
  • By documenting all work, test results and technical findings in the appropriate systems, you ensure traceability and support the team with instructions, training and active knowledge transfer.
  • You are responsible for quality and safety and ensure compliance with all applicable safety, quality and environmental regulations.
  • You actively promote the further development of the department and create and optimize processes, work instructions and technical documentation.
  • If necessary, you will support other areas of the Regional Railway Technology Center and contribute your technical know-how flexibly. This is what you bring with you.
  • Team-oriented work is important to you and you are committed to constructive collaboration across departmental boundaries.
  • With your independent, reliable and structured way of working, you take responsibility and consistently pay attention to safety and quality.
  • You have a strong technical understanding and enjoy precise, careful and quality-conscious work.
  • You have completed an apprenticeship in a craft or mechanical field or have several years of experience in the maintenance and repair of electrical devices, motor devices, machines, two-wheelers or cars. Experience in maintaining, testing or repairing equipment is an advantage and knowledge of the electrical field is welcome.
  • You have good user knowledge of Microsoft 365, ideally supplemented with experience in SAP or another ERP system.
  • You have mastered spoken and written German at least at level B2.
