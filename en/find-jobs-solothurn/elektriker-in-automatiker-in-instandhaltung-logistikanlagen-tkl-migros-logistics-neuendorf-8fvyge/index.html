Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient. Start with us into the future of logistics and assume responsibility in the function as an electrician.

Mansioni

  • Independent performance of maintenance and servicing work on the fully automated order-picking systems
  • Ensuring a trouble-free production operation in cooperation with internal and external bodies
  • Support for conversions, new installations and commissioning of new plants
  • Repair of our machines and surrounding trades (e.g. storage and retrieval devices, gates, lifters and moving cars)

Requirements

  • 1-3 years of experience: maintenance
  • Apprenticeship as an electrician, electromechanic, automatic technician or equivalent training in the electrical sector with professional experience, preferably in maintenance
  • Basic knowledge of control technology and experience in dealing with sensors and actuators
  • Understanding troubleshooting in the automated work environment
  • Interest in new technologies, high willingness to learn, used to working independently and in a team
  • Readiness for shift work at minus temperatures (4-shift operation)
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ)
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension provision: Migros Pension Fund benefits are clearly above the law. All are retired at age 65
  • Food: In our staff restaurants, employees can cater at preferential prices
  • Employee offers: We offer our employees many special services and discounts (e.g. fuel, mobile subscriptions)
  • Sports and fitness offers: We support regular, health-promoting and health-preserving activities
  • Training and further education: We support our employees through targeted training and further education offers and support them generously
  • Social and personnel consultancy services: We offer our employees contact persons for private and professional challenges
