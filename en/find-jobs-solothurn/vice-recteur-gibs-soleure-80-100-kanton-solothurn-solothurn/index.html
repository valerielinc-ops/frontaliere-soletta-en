Full description

The Vocational Training Centre BBZ Solothurn-Grenchen is the regional competence centre for basic vocational training and further education and comprises five service areas (EBZ Solothurn-Grenchen, GIBS Grenchen, GIBS Solothurn, KBS Solothurn, ZeitZentrum Grenchen). The industrial vocational college GIBS Solothurn provides training for more than 1,500 learners in basic vocational training. For the Commercial-Industrial Vocational School GIBS Solothurn we are looking for a Vice-Rector, 80-100% as of 01.04.2027 or by agreement.

Your responsibility

  • As part of the BBZ Solothurn-Grenchen, you support the Rector in the performance areas of GIBS Solothurn with the professions, among others, in the areas of automotive technology, mechanical engineering, gastronomy, computer science/mediatics and room equipment. In particular, you support the pedagogical management and ensure the personnel management of a department.
  • They check and support that the teaching provided and the services provided meet the current requirements of the educational partners.
  • The assumption of school- and cross-divisional tasks of the BBZ Solothurn-Grenchen is also part of your sphere of activity.
  • They teach 6 to 9 lessons.
  • As a member of the school management of GIBS Solothurn, you work actively with the school management on overarching topics, tasks and projects.

Your profile

  • they hold a technical or university degree or equivalent education
  • Leadership experience is beneficial.
  • Several years of teaching experience at the Sek II level, in-depth knowledge of the Swiss educational landscape and active contacts to the working world distinguish you. Ideally, you have experience in business and industry.
  • Experience as a project manager / in various educational projects round off your profile ideally.
  • Working with young people and teaching gives you pleasure, you lead cooperatively and are a good motivator.
  • Furthermore, you are business-oriented, open to change, innovative, capable of compromise, loyal and experienced in dealing with new technologies.
