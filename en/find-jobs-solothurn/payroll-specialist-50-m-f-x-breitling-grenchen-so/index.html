Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities:
  • Ensure monthly payroll preparation, processing and monitoring in collaboration with internal stakeholders and external providers.
  • Ensure the accuracy of payroll calculations, payments and associated administrative activities.
  • Manage legal obligations related to pay (social insurance, source tax, family allowances, pension fund and other compulsory contributions).
  • Coordinate pay data, monitor results and participate in pay reconciliations.
  • Ensure the quality and maintenance of payroll data in HR and payroll systems.
  • Manage employee life cycle pay aspects (entry, modification, departure, absence, international missions, benefits, etc.).
  • Participate in legal statements, audits, annual closures and payroll reporting.
  • Answer questions from employees, managers and external partners about payroll.
  • Ensure the replacement and continuity of payroll operations in Switzerland in case of absence.

Key requirements

  • Business training (CTC) or equivalent training;
  • HR or payroll specialization is an asset.
  • Minimum 3 years of experience in payroll, ideally in Switzerland.
  • Good knowledge of Swiss legislation on pay, social insurance and compliance.
  • Experience with HR and payroll systems (SuccessFactors, SAP or equivalent).
