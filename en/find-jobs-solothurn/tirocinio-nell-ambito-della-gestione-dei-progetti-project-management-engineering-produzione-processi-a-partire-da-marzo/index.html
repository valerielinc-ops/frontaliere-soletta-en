Work Flexibility: Onsite

Our offer to you

  • You support our engineers in the Project Management department in the development or Optimization of manufacturing processes and thus gain comprehensive insights into our various divisions.
  • You will independently process supplier change requests and support the team in monitoring and maintaining the database.
  • You are jointly responsible for the product and process change requests as well as for corrective measures and work closely with our development and process-related engineering teams and production.
  • You will get the opportunity to actively participate in an international medical technology company with your practical solutions.

This sets you apart

  • You are an enrolled student of engineering with a specialization in mechanical engineering, medical engineering, production engineering, industrial engineering, mechatronics, electrical engineering or are enrolled in a similar degree program.
  • Your strong analytical skills and your structured, independent and precise way of working enable you to operate successfully in our dynamic team.
  • Your strong interest in manufacturing issues motivates you to deal with complex issues in the area of optimization processes.
  • Ideally, you could already gain practical experience in the field of production technology.
  • Your safe handling of MS Office and your very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing enable you to communicate easily in our international environment.
