Full description

Management Solothurn | Pensum: 50-100 %

Are you open-minded, ambitious and value a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? At Livit, you are committed to the concerns of owners and tenants with an exemplary customer orientation and thus contribute significantly to a trusting, long-lasting customer relationship and a positive corporate image. Are you ready to tackle the challenges of today and tomorrow and make a difference in the real estate world? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Your tasks

  • You support specialists in all aspects of daily business and are responsible for general data maintenance
  • You advise tenants, housekeepers, craftsmen and authorities by phone and provide information
  • You perform administrative tasks and process tenant correspondence
  • You are handling incoming notices
  • You process damage reports, issue and coordinate repair orders
  • You control and record creditors' bills
  • You make further and final statements

Your profile

  • You bring a completed CV apprenticeship EFZ or an equivalent education (e.g. building or civil engineering drawing:in)
  • You have in-depth business experience, benefit from our industry and are ready to learn new things
  • Your mother tongue is German and you can ideally communicate verbally and in writing in other languages
  • You have a high level of social and self-competence with the ability to self-control
  • You live the values of customer orientation, communicate professionally and enjoy dealing with people
  • You bring an affinity for technology and can apply new technologies
  • You have self-learning skills and it gives you pleasure to share your knowledge

For this vacancy, only direct applications are considered and we do not accept dossiers from recruitment agencies.
