The cantonal child and adult protection authorities (KESB) ensure the welfare and protection of people in need. For KESB Thal-Gäu/Dorneck-Thierstein we are looking for a clerk at KESB, 40% by 01.11.2026 or by agreement at the Breitenbach location.

Your responsibility

  • As an employee of the KESB law firm, you will perform all administrative tasks.
  • They manage dossiers and ensure their completeness, support the firm management in the planning of meetings of the KESB, independently prepare business and orders according to the instructions of the firm management and handle correspondences.
