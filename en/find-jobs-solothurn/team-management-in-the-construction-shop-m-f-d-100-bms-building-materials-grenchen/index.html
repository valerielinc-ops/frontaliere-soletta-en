Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of building materials for shell and interior construction. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at around 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent.

Your passion for shell construction meets leadership competence?

Then you are right with us! For our building supplies shop in Grenchen, we are looking for a motivated and experienced team leader by agreement.

We are all about high-quality materials, strong partnerships and excellent service. If you not only have a knack for shell construction products, but also for team leadership and love the daily challenge in a dynamic environment, then we look forward to you!

Task area

  • Technical and sales advice to customers
  • Professional and organizational leadership of a team of 2 as well as responsibility for their development
  • Co-responsibility for the Handwerkerzentrum and its business development
  • Co-responsibility of the sales and margin targets of the location
  • Responsibility for storage management and stocks
  • Implementation of various sales promotion measures, promotions and promotions
  • Preparation and post-processing of offers, collection, delivery and returns notes
  • Entry and exit handling and control of delivery notes
  • Direct contact person for our pick-up customers

Profile

If your heart beats faster in the area of responsibility described above and you have the following experiences and competencies, you are our desired candidate:

  • Great passion for sales and consulting
  • Completed vocational training, experience in the construction industry beneficial
  • In-depth knowledge in structural engineering
  • First leadership experience in a comparable position
  • Ideally existing network in the region
  • High motivation and drive to advance the business
  • Self-employed and structured working methods
  • Reliable gum
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
