As a track construction machine companion: you are responsible for preparing the work preparation for grinding, milling and tamping work in track construction. You can also do this in consultation from the home office. On this shift, you take on the role of work manager and security chief and ensure that the work runs smoothly and safely. Attractive employment conditions are a matter of course for us – even for irregular working hours. For night and weekend work you will receive attractive supplements, e.g. CHF 16.- per hour for Sunday work. In addition, you benefit from other benefits such as child allowances, a GA 2 class and discounted travel abroad for you and your family. You can find more about this online under “ Benefits”.   • You prepare the work on the construction site independently and accompany track construction machines in grinding and milling campaigns and tamping work.
  • The function of the work management and the security manager:in on the construction site is taken over by you.
  • As a shunting companion: in Ai you apply for shunting routes at the train line and coordinate the shunting movements with the machine operators:inside.
  • You are mainly in your assigned area of Brugg – but if necessary, you also support colleagues within the areas of Basel, Bern, Lucerne and Olten.

  • Training as a shunting companion: in Ai you have already completed or are ready to complete it.
