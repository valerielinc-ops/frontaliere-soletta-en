Working independently as a family doctor without having to do without the professional exchange and the support of experienced colleagues? Do you appreciate a well-established practice team that supports each other and do you also want to make a financial difference with your work performance? In this established group practice in the Canton of Aargau, you can expect exactly this combination: collegial cooperation, a lot of personal responsibility and the opportunity to benefit directly from your personal commitment after a training phase with a purely sales-based remuneration model. The established group practice is located in an attractive, rural region of Baden. Several specialists in general internal medicine work together here and ensure personal, continuous family care. Specialists in orthopaedics and urology also work in the centre on a daily basis. They take over an existing patient base and become part of a well-established team. Instead of having to cope with all medical and organizational challenges alone, you benefit from regular professional exchange, mutual support and well-organized practice structures. Outside of work, the region is also convincing: Natural recreation areas, good schools and good connections to Baden and Zurich offer attractive conditions for a long-term professional and private perspective.

Their tasks

  • Independent care of a broad range of family patients
  • Diagnosis, treatment and long-term support of patients with acute and chronic diseases
  • Self-responsible management of your office hours and patient base
  • Professional exchange and cooperation with medical colleagues
  • Active participation in medical and organisational practice processes

Your Competences

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine or equivalent recognised qualification
  • Practical doctors are also welcome
  • Broad clinical experience and enjoyment of independent family medicine
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and interest in defined benefit compensation
  • Collegial personality who values professional exchange and cooperation
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Experience in the Swiss healthcare system is an advantage

Their advantages

  • Working together instead of alone: Working in an established group practice with several medical colleagues
  • Attractive income potential: After a training phase, the opportunity to switch to a purely sales-based compensation model
  • Performance worthwhile: Your income develops according to your personal work performance and your earned turnover. On request, however, a fixed wage is also possible
  • High independence: You design your office hours independently and at the same time benefit from existing practice structures
  • Long-term perspective: permanent employment in a well-established practice with an existing patient base
  • High level of effort possible: ideally 80-100%
  • High quality of life: Rural residential and recreational area with good schools and attractive connections to Baden and Zurich
