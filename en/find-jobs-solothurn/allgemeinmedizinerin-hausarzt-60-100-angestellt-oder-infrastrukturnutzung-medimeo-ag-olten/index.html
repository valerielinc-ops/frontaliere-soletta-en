Would you like to become part of a well-established practice team that lives medicine with passion? Do you want a family environment, constant patients and long-term cooperation at eye level – without overloaded processes or design facades? Then this owner-managed family practice in the canton of Aargau could suit you exactly. This functionally set up, bright GP practice with modernized IT infrastructure (Axenita, AI telephony, dictation support, automated TARDOC billing) and strong MPA base has been deeply rooted in the region for many years. The MPA team is small but fine: consistent and well-rehearsed – There is hardly any change, as with the long-term patient population. The owner currently leads the practice alone – with growing patient pressure: two modern practice rooms are available, one workplace is fixed. Requests for new patients: women must currently be rejected daily. Now a medical colleague should join – with the opportunity to gradually familiarize themselves and grow in the medium term. I would like to tell you more about this exciting option.
