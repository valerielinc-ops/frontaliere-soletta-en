Full description

Bachtelen is involved in the canton of Solothurn with a wide range of offers for the promotion, support and integration of young people. This includes special schools, various housing offers, individually coordinated therapy offers as well as consultations and daycare centers. Bachtelen employs around 550 people. The school and vocational training sector offers resource-oriented teaching with individualised support. Our students: inside bring a special educational need. Individual education, personal development and preparation for the profession are the focus. In order to support and shape our field of school and vocational education and training, we are looking for an initiative and committed leader as Deputy Head of Schools 50-80 %

Your work includes

  • Support and deputy head of department in all management and organizational issues
  • Accompanying and supporting school leaders: inside our various school offers
  • Active participation in strategic, pedagogical and organizational school development
  • Self-responsible management of cross-cutting projects
  • Further develop school quality and promote inter-school cooperation

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training at a pedagogical university, in school curative education (SHP) and as school management
  • Several years of experience in school management
  • Experience in school development and project management
  • In-depth knowledge of the educational landscape and the current challenges of regular and special schools
  • Networked and strategic thinking, innovation and implementation strength
  • Strong communication and social skills

This is what we offer you

  • A versatile, demanding management task with a great deal of room for manoeuvre, short decision-making paths and the opportunity to significantly shape the future of our schools
  • An attractive workplace with plenty of room for initiative and participation
  • Flat hierarchies and an open, appreciative cooperation
  • Contemporary employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • A meaningful work environment where your work makes a real difference
  • Generous support (money and/or time) for further training

You have questions? Alexandra Pauli, Head of Schools at 032 654 85 22 or alexandra.pauli@bachtelen.ch will gladly provide you with information. We look forward to your application!
