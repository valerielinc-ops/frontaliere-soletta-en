The private clinic Obach is something very special in the Swiss hospital landscape: a small house that has been committed to medical care at the highest level for 100 years. An institution that, thanks to its ideal location, is simultaneously located in both close-to-city and relaxing, green surroundings. A small gem that fulfills its cantonal service mandate with personal care in a private atmosphere for patients from all insurance classes. The Private Clinic Obach offers a wide range of services with a special focus on orthopaedics and urology.
