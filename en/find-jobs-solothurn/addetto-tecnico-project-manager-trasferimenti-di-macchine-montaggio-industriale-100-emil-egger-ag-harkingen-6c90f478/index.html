Full description

Emil Egger AG is one of the leading Swiss logistics companies. With around 800 employees at 11 locations, we offer our customers comprehensive solutions in general cargo, heavy cargo and warehouse logistics. For our location in Härkingen we are looking for a committed personality who loves technical challenges, likes to organize and accompanies demanding projects from planning to implementation. Your tasks – versatile and responsible

  • You plan, organize and coordinate challenging machine and industrial moves as well as industrial assembly.
  • You will support and advise our existing and potential customers – from the first request to the successful completion of the project.
  • You create offers, calculate projects and develop suitable solutions for individual customer requirements.
  • You coordinate staff, vehicles and other resources and ensure a smooth process.
  • You accompany and monitor the work on site and keep an eye on appointments, quality and safety.

Your profile – tech-savvy and well-organized

  • You have completed basic technical training, ideally supplemented by further commercial training (e.g. technical merchant).
  • You bring experience in the field of machine and industrial moving.
  • You convince with technical understanding, organizational talent and negotiating skills.
  • You work independently, reliably and solution-oriented.
  • You enjoy customer contact and appreciate the cooperation in the team.
  • You can communicate verbally and in writing in English.

This is what awaits you with us

  • Exciting projects: Varied and demanding assignments in the industrial environment.
  • Plenty of creative leeway: Self-responsible work with short decision paths.
  • Flexibility: Possibility of independent and flexible working time allocation.
  • Modern infrastructure: A modern company building with contemporary equipment.
  • Attractive extras: Own business vehicle and company phone.
  • Good framework conditions: progressive employment conditions and social benefits.
  • Strong team: A family working environment where cohesion and mutual support count.

Ready for a new challenge? You want to take on responsibility, implement exciting projects and become part of a well-established team? Then we look forward to meeting you!
