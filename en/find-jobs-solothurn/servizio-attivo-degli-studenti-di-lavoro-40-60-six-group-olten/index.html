Working Student Asset Servicing 40-60 %

Olten | 40-60% | Reference 8384

In addition to your studies, do you want to gain real insights into an international financial environment and actively shape it instead of just running? Then you are exactly right with us!

Asset servicing is a technically versatile department with specialized teams in Switzerland and Spain. Together we ensure that our services in the areas of corporate actions, income processing and proxy voting are provided reliably and efficiently. As a working student, you work directly in day-to-day operations – and you also get the opportunity to analyze processes, identify optimization potential and contribute your own ideas.

As a modern, dynamic and innovative company, we offer a variety of opportunities to unleash your potential in the exciting world of technology and finance. We support you in your career, invest in your development and the expansion of your personal skills. As a “Working Student” you are part of our Young Talents community and part of a network of committed career starters. This employment is limited to two years.

These are your tasks

  • Participation in day-to-day business in various areas of asset servicing (corporate actions, income, proxy voting)
  • Support in the high-quality and timely processing of customer orders
  • Analysis of processes and cooperation in the further development of Proezssen
  • Submitting own suggestions for improvement and supporting them in their implementation
  • Cooperation with several teams and international interfaces

This you bring with you

  • Ongoing Bachelor's or Master's degree (e.g. Economics, Finance, Banking, Data/Analytics or similar)
  • A completed banking apprenticeship, experience in the field of securities services are an advantage
  • Analytical thinking and joy in questioning and improving things
  • Above-average customer and service orientation
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

If you have any questions, visit our FAQ page or contact Maria Teresa Buchanan on +41 58 399 8083.
