HR consultant:in, 70-90% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. HR consultant:in, 70-90 % Olten / Work Smart, by agreement, Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Join us in moving Switzerland and advising SBB executives on varied and exciting HR topics. A collaborative and helpful team, in which cooperation but also self-responsibility are given priority, is waiting for you. You can move that.

  • You will advise our managers personally, digitally and by telephone in all questions relating to the terms and conditions of employment within the framework of our collective employment contract (GAV).
  • You will work on various challenging questions along the HR processes and consistently live the outside-in thought.
  • As a sparring partner of the executives, you act in the implementation of labour law measures and the application of the guidelines.
  • Together with executives and HR business partners, you participate in the implementation of reorganizations.
  • You specialize in HR subjects, work in specialist teams and actively exchange your knowledge with colleagues. Furthermore, you contribute to the active improvement of our service.

You bring that with you.

  • A high solution orientation, conflict ability and pragmatism are your strengths.
  • You already have experience in HR consulting and are fit for employment law issues.
  • Commitment, integrity and ambition for continuous improvement are your flag.
  • We require a degree in business administration with a specialization in HRM or a training as HR specialist with additional training at tertiary level.
  • We presuppose stylish knowledge of German at level C1. Very good knowledge in French or Italian (level B2) we welcome.

Your reward and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. For this reason, further and detailed information on the wage system can be viewed transparently at SBB. This post is at requirement level J, regional allowance level 0. More about wages and benefits.

This is the starting point.

The fact that you want to move Switzerland with us is the greatest motivation for us. Therefore, we refrain from a letter of motivation at this point. If you still want to tell us your motivation, you still have the opportunity to do so in our application tool. Making big is easier for you. This starts with your application.

That's how it goes.
