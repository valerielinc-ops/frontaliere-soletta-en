Full description

The public prosecutor’s office conducts criminal investigations against adults in the canton of Solothurn. Engage with us for effective and efficient law enforcement. For the management / department of business control and order buses, we are looking for an 80-100% clerk at the Solothurn location as of 01.02.2027 or by agreement.

Your responsibility

  • They shall be responsible for recording and allocating all transactions.
  • They participate in the preparation of criminal orders and administratively supervise these procedures independently.
  • They help with daily post processing.
  • In numerous administrative matters, you support the public prosecutor's office.
  • Parties to the proceedings provide you on the telephone and reception friendly and competent information of all kinds.

Your profile

  • They have a commercial apprenticeship degree or equivalent training and have very good IT skills.
  • They also work accurately and reliably in a large number of shops.
  • A customer- and service-oriented way of working is a matter of course for you.
  • They like to work independently and in a team.
  • Stylistic German in word and writing and good knowledge of French round off your profile.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
