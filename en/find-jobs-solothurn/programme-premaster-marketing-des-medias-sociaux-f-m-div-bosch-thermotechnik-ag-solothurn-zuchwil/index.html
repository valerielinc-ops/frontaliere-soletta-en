Your wich

Contact & additional information

The Bosch PreMaster Program is a two-stage program for committed Bachelor graduates who want to join a Master's degree. In the first phase, the so-called Company Phase consists of 12 – Max 18 months of practical experience for induction into technical and business contexts. The second phase includes the Master's degree program, which is continued in a part-time program. This phase includes additional events and seminars as well as personal support on the way to a successful completion.
