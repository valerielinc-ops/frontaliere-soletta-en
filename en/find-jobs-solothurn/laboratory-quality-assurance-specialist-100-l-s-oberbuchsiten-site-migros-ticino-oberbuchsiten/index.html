Full description

A new location, a special opportunity: help shape quality assurance at L+S in Oberbuchsiten right from the start! You ensure that quality and hygiene are right in the laboratory, production and logistics. Your start and training will be in Kloten.

Luogo di lavoro: Lüchinger+Schmid, Halmackerstrasse 351, 4625 Oberbuchsiten

Important information

Only complete application documents will be considered. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You perform microbiological sampling including environmental monitoring in production.
  • You administer laboratory samples and organize their shipment.
  • Among other things, you analyze pigment contents and carry out in-process controls.
  • You control the quality and check the hygiene in production and logistics.
  • You support internal and external audits as well as the creation of reports on quality issues.
  • You participate in quality assurance processes, implement improvement measures and sensitize employees for quality and hygiene.

Requirements

  • You bring at least two years of experience in the laboratory, in quality assurance or in food production.: You have practical knowledge of quality assurance and hygiene.
  • Completed Vocational Apprenticeship (EFZ): You have an education in food technology, food chemistry or a comparable field.
  • You proceed analytically and find solutions for technical questions.
  • You communicate understandably and can carry out training.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (advantageous)
  • English (advantageous)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Reference information: When obtaining references, we ask for impressions, strengths and weaknesses again and have them verified by former supervisors or employees. References can be obtained after the first or second round and are only made with consent. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Final discussion before issuing the contract: Before the contract is issued, we hold a final discussion in order to clarify open questions of both sides and to regulate contractual details. If all points are clarified, the written offer follows. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
