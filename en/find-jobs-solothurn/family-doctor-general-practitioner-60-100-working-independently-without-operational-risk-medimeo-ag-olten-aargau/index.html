Are you looking for a long-term perspective in family care with a high level of independence, but without entrepreneurial risk? Do you want to become part of a modern care model that offers you a reliable practice infrastructure and complete relief from administrative tasks? For an established family practice in the canton of Aargau, a successor solution is sought in the course of the retirement of the previous owner. They work independently with a great deal of room for manoeuvre and can concentrate on medical activity thanks to central support. A growing patient base, modern infrastructure and an experienced MPA team ensure efficient everyday practice. The entry is accompanied professionally. A permanent job or a variable participation is possible.

Their tasks

  • Independent care of a broad range of family patients
  • Continuity and long-term relationship building with patients in the region
  • Collaboration with a constant local MPA team as well as interprofessional exchange
  • Participation in internal training and quality formats

Your Competences

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine or Practical Doctor (with MEBEKO recognition or in preparation)
  • Enjoy independent, high-quality work in a stable environment
  • Openness to modern tools, digital documentation and efficient practical processes
  • Interest in a long-term activity – ideally over several years

Their advantages

  • Attractive succession solution with regulated transfer into a well-established practice
  • Independent medical work – without business risk
  • Complete administrative discharge (wages, accounting, IT, billing)
  • Modern infrastructure, digitally managed with amétiq simed
  • Flexible employment models with fair fixum and/or revenue share
  • Family atmosphere with a constant MPA team and a high degree of creative freedom
  • Region with high quality of life, good accessibility and affordable housing
