Full description

If you like to start your day early, you love being in the fresh air and enjoy conjuring people a smile in your face? Then a training course in the distribution field is just right for you! During the three-year basic training course, you will acquire the tools to successfully enter the diverse world of logistics.

Your training

  • In the early morning you will complete your packages and load your yellow delivery vehicle for the following tour.
  • After that, you will be on your own, bringing and getting shipments of all kinds and taking care of the concerns of our customers.
  • Before you start your well-deserved evening off, you will complete various follow-up work at your apprenticeship.
  • During your training, you will be supervised by professionals, introduced to practice and continuously developed.
  • In addition, with the financial support of the post office, you will obtain the driving licence for category B motor vehicles and learn to drive a forklift.
  • With various internal stations, a deployment in a warehouse operation, as well as individual support measures according to your wishes, we complete your broad logistics training.

You bring this with you

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy and motivation who want to make a difference. You should fulfill the following points:

  • Working in the fresh air and physical activity are what inspires you (robustness is a prerequisite).
  • Furthermore, you enjoy dealing with people, be reliable, honest and keep secret.
  • You're at least 17 years old when you start your apprenticeship.
  • You are ready to receive the study driving pass for category B motor vehicles until the start of your apprenticeship.
  • You've completed compulsory schooling.
  • At the beginning of the course you have the language level B2 in German.
