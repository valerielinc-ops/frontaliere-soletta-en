Full description

Raiffeisenbank am Eppenberg is a successful, modern and regionally strongly anchored bank that moves a lot.

We are personal, maintain a trusting cooperation and create solutions for our customers that are profitable and individual for both sides. Our employees benefit from flexible working models, training opportunities and home office. Tasks: In this function, you are responsible for the management of the organizational unit "Services", which comprises the following areas: regulatory system, risk control, master data, payment transactions, accounting, controlling, compliance, VSB and money laundering, IKS, quality control and computer science. They shall ensure the needs-based support of the bank’s management and departments and the efficient handling of the transactions involved. In cooperation with the bank management, you prepare the bank accounts and the annual budget. You are responsible for asset and liability management, develop necessary measures and implement them. As a member of the bank management, you are also jointly responsible for the strategic, entrepreneurial and business management of the bank and contribute together with your team to the successful development of our bank. Requirements In this demanding and responsible position we see a personality with a higher education and proven practice in a comparable function. We offer: We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility.
