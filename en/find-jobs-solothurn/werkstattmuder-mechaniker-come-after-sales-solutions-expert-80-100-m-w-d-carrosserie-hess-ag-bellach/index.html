Workshop-weary mechanic as After Sales Solutions Expert (m/f/d |80% - 100%) HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 500 people in Bellach (SO).
