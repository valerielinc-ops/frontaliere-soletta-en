Team leader control room in logistics (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company.

In order to complement our team in materials management in the field of operations, we are looking for a motivated and qualified manager as team leader.

You appreciate the varied environment in logistics and you are a professional in the coordination of logistics processes then we have the ideal position for you! As team manager with around 9 employees, you have a significant influence on the quality of our logistics processes and are the hub within logistics. Help with your expertise to perfect the logistics around the production of our high-quality electric buses!

Their tasks

  • Organizational and personnel management of the control room with around 9 employees
  • Active assistance in day-to-day business as well as ensuring and monitoring day-to-day business
  • Monitoring of daily bookings
  • Timely preparation and processing of order picking orders
  • Close cooperation with internal departments to ensure optimal material disposition for spare parts and production
  • Participation in project groups and implementation of process optimization
  • Prepare data from the ERP in the EXCEL and format for further processing
  • Active support for inventory work
  • Ordering of consumables and tools

Our requirements

  • Basic technical training in an industrial establishment
  • Further education in the field of process specialist or logistics specialist as well as experience in the field of warehouse logistics
  • Organizational talent with hands-on mentality and leadership experience
  • Experienced MS Office knowledge (especially advanced user knowledge in Excel)
  • Very good user experience in an ERP system
  • Independent operation and high load capacity
  • High level of teamwork and a strong sense of duty
  • Very good knowledge of German (English of advantage)

We offer them

  • Interesting and varied work in a successful traditional company
  • Challenging Leadership
  • Moving working time
  • Conditions of performance
  • Opportunity for further training and development
