As a Flying Physio you are well equipped: A packed car and mobile phone are waiting for you. Nothing stops you from starting! You can also use our flying speedsters privately.

Your employer OMNIA Health Services AG OMNIA Physio & Flying Physios stands for a modern, patient-centered physiotherapy that combines high therapeutic quality with efficient organization. Our aim is to raise physiotherapeutic care in Switzerland to a new level – through individual, scientifically based treatments, close interdisciplinary cooperation and an infrastructure that offers our therapists the best possible working conditions. We combine practice-based and mobile physiotherapy in a well-thought-out concept: In our specialized practices, we create optimal conditions for targeted therapy, while our Flying Physios model ensures structured and professional domicile care.
