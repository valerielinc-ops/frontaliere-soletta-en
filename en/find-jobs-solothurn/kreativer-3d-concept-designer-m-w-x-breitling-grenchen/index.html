Full description

We are a dedicated Squad on a mission to redefine Swiss watchmaking, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision we create timepieces that combine modern-retro design with casual, approachable, and sustainable luxury — powered by passionate teams around the world. For our Creative Hub, we are looking for a Creative 3D Concept Designer to join our #Squad. Working within a multidisciplinary team, you will help bring creative ideas to life across retail environments, visual merchandising, point of sale, pop-ups, partnerships, and other brand experiences. Beyond creating high-quality visualisations, you will actively contribute to concept development, shaping spatial experiences and translating creative direction into compelling visual stories.

Your contribution

  • You will create high-quality 3D visualisations and imagery that reflect Breitling’s creative vision and brand standards.
  • You will translate creative briefs, sketches, and design directions into compelling spatial concepts, bringing ideas to life from the earliest stages.
  • You will visualise retail spaces, pop-ups, POS elements, furniture, and brand installations with accuracy, attention to scale, and a strong sense of composition.
  • You will contribute to projects from initial concept exploration through creative approval and implementation handover.
  • You will explore and test different design options, materials, lighting scenarios, and customer experiences to strengthen creative concepts.
  • You will lead the 3D visual output for assigned projects, ensuring clarity, quality, and consistency throughout the design process.
  • You will collaborate closely with Creatives, Senior and Expert Creatives, Graphic Designers, Trade Marketing, and other project partners to develop ideas and deliver cohesive brand experiences.
  • You will prepare visual materials for internal reviews, stakeholder presentations, and creative approval stages.
  • You will support implementation teams by providing visual clarification and creative guidance when needed.
  • You will contribute to special projects and new creative developments across our House of Brands.
  • You will manage several projects simultaneously, adapting to changing priorities while maintaining high standards and meeting deadlines.
  • You will maintain organised project files, efficient working methods, and consistent visualisation standards. You will fit well to our Squad if:
  • You have at least 2 years of relevant experience in 3D visualisation, spatial design, retail design, or a related field.
  • You have advanced proficiency in 3ds Max and strong knowledge of V-Ray or an equivalent rendering engine.
  • You have a degree in architecture, interior design, spatial design, or a related discipline, or equivalent professional experience.
  • You have a strong understanding of scale, proportion, materials, lighting, spatial composition, technical drawing, and spatial planning.
  • You have experience with Archicad, Cinema 4D, and Adobe Creative Suite, which would be an advantage.
  • You are proficient in using AI tools to support and enhance your creative and visualisation workflow.
  • You have experience in retail, luxury, hospitality, exhibitions, or branded environments, which would be an advantage.
  • You have a portfolio showcasing high-quality visualisation work across different project stages, from early concept development to refined final imagery.
  • You have a strong eye for detail and are committed to delivering high-quality, consistent visual output.
  • You are proactive, organised, and comfortable managing multiple assignments and shifting priorities.
  • You are a collaborative team player with strong communication and problem-solving skills, open to feedback and different perspectives.
  • You can interpret creative direction while bringing your own ideas, recommendations, and solutions to the table.
  • You are curious, adaptable, and solution-oriented, with the ability to remain focused and organised when working under pressure.
  • You value trust, transparency, and consistency, and understand how 3D visualisation can help a team explore ideas, align on creative direction, and make informed decisions. Do you think there is even more to discover in this role? You’re right — apply today and learn more!
