Acting as a strategic partner (Sparring Partner) for managers on all issues related to workforce and succession planning, talent development, disciplinary management and leadership Follow-up of long-term absences and support the reintegration of employees Actively participate in various HR projects and contribute to continuous improvement and optimization of HR processes.
