Full description

The Office for Spatial Planning (ARP) is committed to a differentiated and sustainable development of the municipalities and regions as well as of the canton as a whole - the aim is to deal with the non-renewable soil in a household. We are looking for the Office for Spatial Planning as per 01.11.2026 or by agreement at the Solothurn site a director/in office for spatial planning/canton planner/in, 100%.

Your Responsibility

  • You have an office with over 30 employees in the departments of spatial planning, including construction outside the building zone as well as nature and home protection.
  • In your function you are responsible for technical, personnel and financial aspects of the office and are responsible for cantonal key projects and the cantonal guidance plan.
  • The departmental head supports and advises you in all areas of the office.
  • You represent the canton in working groups, parliamentary committees as well as in relation to the neighbouring cantons and the federal government.

Your profile

  • You have a university degree in geography, law or architecture with a master's degree. This is complemented by additional training or proven experience in spatial planning; - High leadership and social competence, design will as well as a strong communication ability and teaching competence distinguish you. - You have a high level of commitment and assertiveness as well as a deeper understanding of political processes. Strategic and networked thinking as well as accurate and stylistically safe formulation characterize you.
  • You will have many years of experience in negotiating with local authorities and public and private bodies of large projects with a spatial impact. You will also be well versed in weighing conflicting interests and enjoying decision-making.
