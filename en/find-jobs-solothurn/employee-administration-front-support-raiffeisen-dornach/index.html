17.09.2026 Dornach

Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Dornach, a successful bank with strong local roots, is looking for a dedicated and service-oriented personality.

What awaits you?

  • Receiving calls with the independent completion of various customer orders or passing them on to customer consultants
  • Control of all new customer and product openings and mutations
  • Securities processing
  • Processing of payment orders and management of different payment systems
  • Management of e-banking credits and processing of general e-mails
  • Co-responsibility for due diligence, money laundering and compliance issues (VSB, etc.)
  • Scanning of documents
  • Responsible for processing customer orders for the Precautionary/Free Movement Foundation
  • Maintenance of statistics and lists
  • Management of deaths and cases of the child and adult protection authority
  • Share responsibility for relationship with contact & newslessness

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking education or equivalent with banking experience
  • Pronounced pleasure in internal and external customer contact with high service willingness and good communication skills
  • Exciting and versatile personality with a winning appearance
  • Ability to keep track even in hectic situations
  • Independent, precise and responsible as well as structured working methods with rapid comprehension
  • Great flexibility, teamwork and readiness for new things
  • Preferably experience in money laundering and compliance issues or willingness to complete the internal training course in the field of money laundering
  • Practiced in dealing with digital media
