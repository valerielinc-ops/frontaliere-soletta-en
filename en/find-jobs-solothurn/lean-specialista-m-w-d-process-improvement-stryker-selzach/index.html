Work Flexibility: Hybrid or Onsite

Description

Actively shape the future of production: In this role, you will drive the systematic advancement of Lean Management and embed sustainable improvements throughout the plant. You will create measurable value through efficient processes, clear management systems, and a strong culture of continuous improvement.

What we offer you

  • Lead and implement Lean initiatives and Kaizen workshops in production and support areas and ensure measurable improvements in lead times, quality, and productivity
  • Standardize and stabilize shopfloor management and KPI structures to transparently manage performance
  • Coach managers and teams in problem-solving methods and ensure the consistent application of structured improvement processes
  • Analyze value streams and conduct workshops to identify and reduce waste
  • Integrate Leader Standard Work into daily management and establish regular reviews to ensure implementation
  • Systematically follow up on improvement measures and ensure sustainable results based on clear metrics
  • Support the digitization of Lean elements by introducing visual and data-based solutions (e.g., digital dashboards)
  • Actively promote a culture of continuous improvement through training, instruction, and practical implementation in the workplace
  • Lead the implementation of the Stryker Production System (SPS) and ensure the sustainable application of Lean tools and methods through training, coaching, and support.
  • Act as deputy to the Lean Manager and, when needed, take on technical leadership and coordination of site-wide Lean and Continuous Improvement activities.

What sets you apart

Required qualifications

  • HF or FH degree (Bachelor's or Master's) in mechanical engineering, industrial engineering and management, Industrial Engineering, manufacturing engineering, Supply Chain Management, process management, or business informatics
  • Minimum 3 years' experience in planning and implementing Lean projects in a production environment
  • Demonstrable knowledge of Lean methods (e.g., value stream mapping, Kaizen, 5S, SMED, TPM, PDCA)
  • Experience using MS365, Power Automate, and Power BI
  • German at native-speaker level
  • English at a business-fluent level

Preferred qualifications

  • Certification in Lean or process management (e.g., Lean Management, Lean Six Sigma Green Belt, or Yellow Belt)
  • Experience with digital visualization and collaboration tools (e.g., Miro)
  • Knowledge of layout design and material flow analysis

Additional Information

  • Work location: Production environment at the plant
  • Close collaboration with cross-functional teams (Production, Quality, Engineering, Logistics)
  • Occasional cross-functional assignments and participation in audits possible

This position offers the opportunity to shape sustainable changes and measurably drive operational excellence – with a direct impact on the performance of the entire site.
