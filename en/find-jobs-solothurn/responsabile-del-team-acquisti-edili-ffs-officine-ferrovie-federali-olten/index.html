Team Leader: in Building Purchasing 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team leader: in construction purchasing Olten / Work Smart, by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

If you want to make a difference – then you are exactly right with us. There is an unusual task waiting for you in a dynamic environment. Together with your team, you design the purchase of planning and works services so that construction projects can progress efficiently and travellers can travel barrier-free. You can expect a diverse project portfolio from new and renovations to renovations of railway stations, tunnels, bridges and entire railway sections. You work closely with project managers, technology, law and other interfaces and take on responsibility with a noticeable effect. You can move that.

  • You are responsible for the project purchase construction of the Infrastructure Division in the Mitte region in terms of personnel and expertise.
  • Your eight or so project buyers: you advise on procurement and contract issues under public procurement law and agree on individual development measures.
  • The regional procurement business is conducted according to commercial principles and subscribes up to CHF 10 million.
  • As a member of the management team Baueinkauf Infrastruktur, you participate in regional and national committees and actively maintain contacts with internal and external stakeholders.
  • In specialist groups, you bring in approaches for the continuous development of processes and methods.
  • You are ready to travel within the region and perform regular assignments in Olten, Lucerne and Bellinzona.

You bring that with you.

  • In this leadership position, strong leadership skills such as results-oriented leadership and development-oriented leadership are of great importance.
  • You will quickly familiarize yourself with new and complex problems and act in a result-oriented and quality-conscious manner. In the management of escalations and critical project situations at management and management level, you have already gained experience.
  • Your strengths include entrepreneurial and cost-conscious thinking as well as strong communication and moderation skills.
  • You bring experience in project procurement and/or in the construction industry.
  • You have a university degree (in the field of engineering, business administration or comparable) or have a higher specialist examination (Federal Dipl.) Purchasing manager: in or an equivalent education.
  • In German you are safe to negotiate (C2). You speak French or Italian with very good knowledge (B2) of the other national languages.

Your reward and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This employment takes place in accordance with bond law, taking into account a comparison with the team, the market and the industry.
