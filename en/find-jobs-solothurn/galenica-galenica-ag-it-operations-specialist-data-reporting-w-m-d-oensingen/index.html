Full description

Location: Medifilm AG, Ostringstrasse 10, 4702 Oensingen

Do you want to not only operate IT operations, data platforms and analytics solutions, but actively develop them? In this versatile role, you will ensure stable and GxP-compliant systems while actively developing our reporting, analytics and data landscape. You ensure that data is reliably integrated, processed and usable for operational and business decisions. Through close cooperation with specialist areas, quality management and external partners, you make a direct contribution to important production and business processes.

You can do that.

  • You ensure the stable, secure and GxP-compliant operation of production-oriented IT systems, applications and data services.
  • You work on incidents, changes and requests and analyze disruptions along applications, databases, interfaces and data processes.
  • You develop our data platform and data models further, integrate new data sources and automate data flows.
  • You operate and optimize our reporting and analytics solutions and independently implement reports, dashboards and key figures.
  • You analyze data requirements, develop suitable data models and ensure traceable data flows from source to reporting.
  • You support validation, compliance and documentation activities and ensure regulatory requirements for data and systems.

You bring that with you

  • Completed training or further education in computer science (HF, FH or comparable) as well as experience in the operation of applications and production-related IT systems.
  • Very good SQL knowledge and practical experience with databases, data integration, data modeling and practical scripting knowledge, e.g. with PowerShell
  • Practical experience with reporting, data analysis and visualization, ideally with Power BI, Qlik Sense or QlikView
  • Experience in analyzing and fixing errors in data flows, databases and applications.
  • Analytical and solution-oriented working methods as well as pleasure in solving technical challenges sustainably.
  • High quality awareness, personal responsibility and a pronounced service and team orientation.
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Expand competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-defining healthcare provider in our country. The Galenica network offers a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
