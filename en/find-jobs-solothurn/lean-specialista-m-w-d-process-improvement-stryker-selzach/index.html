Full description

Work flexibility: Hybrid or Onsite

Actively shape the future of production: In this role, you drive forward the systematic development of lean management and anchor sustainable improvements throughout the plant. They create measurable added value through efficient processes, clear management systems and a strong culture of continuous improvement.

Our offer to you

  • Guide and implement lean initiatives and Kaizen workshops in production and support areas and ensure measurable improvements in throughput times, quality and productivity
  • Standardize and stabilize shop floor-level management and KPI structures for transparent control of performance
  • Coaching managers and teams in problem solving methods and ensure the consistent application of structured improvement processes
  • Analyse value streams and conduct workshops on identification and reduction of waste
  • Integrate Leader Standard Work into daily leadership and establish regular reviews to ensure implementation
  • Track improvement measures systematically and ensure sustainable results using clear indicators
  • Support the digitalization of lean elements by introducing visual and data-based solutions (e.g. digital Dashboards)
  • Actively promote a culture of continuous improvement through training, training and practical implementation at the workplace
  • Perform the implementation of the Stryker Production System (SPS) and ensure the sustainable application of lean tools and methods through training, coaching and support.
  • Act as a representative of the Lean Manager and take over the professional management and coordination of lean and Continual improvement activities if necessary.

That's what makes you feel Required qualifications

  • Degree in Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Supply Chain Management, Process Management and Business Informatics (HF) or FH (Bachelor or Master)
  • Minimum 3 years of experience in planning and implementing lean projects in the production environment
  • Detection of lean methods (e.g. value stream analysis, Kaizen, 5S, SMED, TPM, PDCA)
  • Experience in the use of MS65, Power Automate and Power BI
  • German at a native level
  • English at a negotiating level

Preferred qualifications

  • Lean or process management certification (e.g. Lean Management, Lean Six Sigium Green Belt or Yellow Belt)
  • Experience with digital visualization and collaboration tools (e.g. Miro)
  • Knowledge in layout design and material flow analysis

Additional information

  • Place of work: Production environment in the factory
  • Close cooperation with cross-functional teams (production, quality, engineering, logistics)
  • Occasional cross-cutting operations and participation in audits possible

This position offers the opportunity to shape sustainable changes and to drive operational excellence in a measurable way – with a direct impact on the performance of the entire location.
