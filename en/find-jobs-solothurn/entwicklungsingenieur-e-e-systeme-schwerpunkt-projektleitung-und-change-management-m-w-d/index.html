Development Engineer E/E Systems Focus on Project Management & Change Management (m/f/d | 80 - 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our modular system “Co-Bolt”. We are the only Swiss bus manufacturer to go far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The culture of innovation is the fuel of our company.

In order to meet the growing demand for electrically powered buses, we are looking for a motivated and experienced E/E Systems development engineer in the Operations department for the R&D E/E Systems team.

Do you have a flair for vehicles and are fascinated by the interaction of mechanics and electronics? The design of the on-board network with the latest information and camera systems, networks, infotainment, HMI and M2M interfaces as well as the programming of the individual components have a major influence on the functions of the vehicle. In this challenge, you will use your know-how to move thousands of people safely, comfortably and sustainably every day with electric mobility in public transport. Take advantage of this opportunity to contribute your future work results to the only vehicle OEM in Switzerland.

Your tasks

  • Partial project management in the area of ​​E/E systems for new vehicles and retrofits
  • Coordination and technical support of change management (change management)
  • Integration of customer systems: networks, control systems, video surveillance systems, etc.
  • Creation and changes of electrical circuit diagrams and parts lists with ECAD
  • Support and commissioning of customer systems in production including documentation
  • Contact person for the production site in Portugal for technical topics in the area of ​​E/E

Our requirements

  • Basic technical training in the field of electrics/electronics
  • Further training HF or FH in the field of automotive technology / mechanical engineering / electrical engineering or in a similar field
  • Knowledge of creating electrical circuit diagrams using ECAD
  • Fluent knowledge of spoken and written German and English, knowledge of French is an advantage
  • Passion for vehicles and interest in electromobility

We offer you

  • Varied and challenging work in an innovative and growing environment
  • Designing sustainable mobility solutions with a motivated team
  • Opportunity to specialize in the field of vehicle electrics/electronics
  • Fast and short decision-making processes
  • Sliding, flexible working hours (including the possibility of working from home)
  • Performance-based employment conditions
