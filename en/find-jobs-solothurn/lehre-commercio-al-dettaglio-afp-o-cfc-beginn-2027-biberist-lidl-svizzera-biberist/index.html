Full description

Do you already have ideas for the time after finishing school? Start your future with us and take advantage of the development opportunities at Lidl Schweiz. Our stores are at the heart of everything we do. As an apprentice in retail (specializing in creating shopping experiences), whether EBA or EFZ, you contribute to the success of your Lidl store. Start/end EBA: 1 August 2027 - 31 July 2029 Start/end EFZ: 1 August 2027 - 31 July 2030 Your tasks

  • Operating cash registers and preparing cash register reports
  • Ensuring freshness, quality and cleanliness
  • Stocking goods and presenting merchandise
  • Checking incoming goods in the warehouse and supporting your team during inventories
  • Supporting the planning and implementation of promotions and seasonal displays
  • Determining merchandise requirements and ordering goods in the 3rd year of training (EFZ)
  • Taking on initial shift management responsibilities in the 3rd year of training (EFZ)

Your profile

  • An average to very good secondary school-leaving qualification
  • A foreign language at A1 level for EBA and at least A2 for EFZ
  • Enjoying interacting with people
  • An independent working style and the ability to grasp things quickly
  • Teamwork skills, reliability and a sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to learn and commitment
  • Physical stamina
  • Very good German language skills

An apprenticeship with many paths open to you.
