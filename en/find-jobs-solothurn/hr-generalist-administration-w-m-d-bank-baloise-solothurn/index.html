Full description

Baloise Bank is part of Helvetia Baloise Switzerland – an innovative financial services provider that intelligently combines banking and insurance. We offer solutions that offer security and at the same time open up new paths. We are characterized by a unique culture in which the human being is at the center. We believe in the value of trust, respect and integrity

Tasks

To support our HR team, we are looking for a dedicated, reliable and service-oriented personality. Whether you already have HR experience or you are a student or student. Students with great interest in Human Resources would like to develop further: With us you will get the opportunity to gain valuable practical experience in an exciting and dynamic environment.

In this role, you support our HR team in the challenging and varied phase of the integration of Helvetia and Baloise. With your contribution, you ensure that our HR processes are implemented efficiently, professionally and customer-oriented.

  • Support of the HR team throughout the entire employee lifecycle, e.g. drawing up employment contracts, confirmations, certificates and other documents
  • Assumption of tasks related to the merger of Helvetia and Baloise
  • Maintenance and management of personnel data in HR systems, including mutations
  • Support in the recruitment process as well as for entry and exit
  • Processing of mutations and HR requests from employees, managers and external bodies
  • Your assignment in HR is temporary until 31.12.2027

Requirements

  • Ongoing studies or completed training with an interest in human resources as well as practical experience in the administrative environment (ideally in the HR area)
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Knowledge of French and/or Italian is a plus
  • Careful, structured and reliable workflow
  • High discretion and sense of responsibility in dealing with confidential information
  • Strong service orientation and pleasure in contact with people
  • Very good user knowledge of MS-Office programs; SAP is an advantage

We believe in cooperation on equal terms, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
