Full description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Planzer Paket.

Planzer Paket stands for reliable and fast parcel deliveries in Switzerland. As part of our team, you actively contribute to further developing our high quality standards and service. We offer you a varied job in a future-oriented company that focuses on sustainable solutions. Become part of our team and shape the future of the package world!

Tasks

  • You look after the drivers before and during delivery as well as on return
  • You communicate with internal and external bodies to ensure a smooth process
  • If it's not so smooth, you work on the deviations of our customers with a lot of grace and high service ideas
  • You promote a close cooperation with superiors and work colleagues, according to the motto "one for all, all for one"
  • Maintaining and updating the shipment data in the system does not bother you
  • You support our disposition in various administrative tasks

Profile

  • Ideally, you will bring a completed vocational training in the transport or logistics environment
  • You have professional experience in the commercial or administrative sector
  • Shift work between 5:00 and 20:00? No problem for you, because you appreciate the variety
  • PC, phone, chat – there you are nimble like a weasel and serve all means of communication with links
  • Oral and written you can surely express yourself in German language stylishly
  • You work reliably, accurately, are on Zack and quickly adapt to new challenges
  • Even if it gets stressful, you stay cool and keep track.

Benefits

  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • With our annual card you can go to the Tierpark Goldau for free!

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
