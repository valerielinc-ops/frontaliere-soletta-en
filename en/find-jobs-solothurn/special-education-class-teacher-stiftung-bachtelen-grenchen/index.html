Full description

In the canton of Solothurn, BACHTELEN is committed to the individual promotion and integration of children, young people and young adults.

In order to achieve our goals, we run daycare centers, day schools, housing offers, offers of individual support as well as therapeutic and advisory offers.

We are looking for the school year 2026/2027 for the day school Grenchen as of now

Healing class teachers

for cycle 1 and 2 (40 – 60 %)

or a teacher with readiness,

start training within the next two years

Tasks

  • Teaching and supporting normally gifted students with special educational needs in the areas of behavior, learning and communication
  • Class leadership of an age-mixed class of 8 – 9 students in cycle 1 and 2
  • Close cooperation in a multi-professional team consisting of the specialist disciplines of curative pedagogy, social pedagogy, therapy & counselling

We are happy about the personality that

  • Enjoying to also pursue new pedagogical paths, for example through learning in nature or action-oriented teaching
  • Bring enthusiasm for the profession and promote and challenge children at their individual development level
  • are interested in developing further in the areas of trauma pedagogy, bond-led pedagogy and New Authority
  • enjoy working in a multi-professional team and taking on responsibility

Among other things, we offer

  • Great room for manoeuvre in teaching
  • Working in a dedicated and supportive team
  • Support from team members and leadership
  • Various training offers as well as opportunities for reflection and intervision
  • Conditions of employment according to cantonal guidelines
