Full description

Your escort vehicle. Your responsibility. Every day a different special transport. Do you have ATB, C/E and experience in heavy or special transport? Then we have a job where you don’t just do miles. At EMIL EGGER, you are deployed where normal transport stops: for special and exceptional transports, large machines, overspread loads and challenging routes across Switzerland. And best of all: Your ATB escort vehicle can go home with you. Your tasks

  • 🚛 Accompanying exceptional transports throughout Switzerland – No day is like the other
  • 🛣 Secure transport routes and ensure that even demanding transports reach their destination safely
  • Assuming responsibility and recognizing traffic situations early and assessing them correctly
  • 🤝 Working closely with chauffeurs, dispatchers, police and other stakeholders
  • 🔪 If necessary, also be on the road as a chauffeur in heavy and special transport.
  • 🏨 Overnight stays are included depending on the use
  • ⚡ Every day new challenges instead of monotonous tours
  • 🚘 Your ATB escort vehicle may be at your home

You bring with you

  • ATB training is mandatory. You should:
  • hold the driving licence Cat C/E
  • bring several years of experience as a chauffeur in heavy or special transport
  • remain calm and sovereign even in demanding situations
  • Working independently and taking responsibility
  • Be flexible and enjoy being on the road
  • You can rely on your team – and your team can rely on you

We offer

Here you will not get an 08/15 chauffeur job. At EMIL EGGER you work with modern equipment and state-of-the-art vehicles and are part of a team that knows what it means to put demanding transports on the road. In addition,

  • 🚘 ATB accompanying vehicle for private use or Take home
  • 🏗 Exciting special and heavy transport throughout Switzerland
  • Modern vehicles and professional equipment
  • 💰 Fair and attractive employment conditions
  • 🛡 Attractive social benefits
  • Free parking
  • 🤝 Collegial team and real you-culture
  • 📅 Flexible entry by arrangement
  • 💪 An employer with many years of experience and over 230 employees in the field of heavy goods logistics

Why Emil EGGER? Because we don't just bring transport from A to B. We move things that others cannot move. Whether industrial plant, large machine or oversized load: You are in the middle of it and ensure with your know-how that the transport comes safely through Switzerland. If you are an ATB professional, do not shy away from responsibility and prefer challenging missions than boring standard tours, you are at the right place. Ready for the next mission? Then join Emil EGGER. We look forward to your application and getting to know you personally.
