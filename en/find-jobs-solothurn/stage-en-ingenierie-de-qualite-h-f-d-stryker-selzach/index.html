Our offer to you

  • You will support our Quality Engineers in quality-relevant tasks and gain exciting insights into the areas of Quality Engineering and Quality Assurance.

This sets you apart

  • You have a good technical understanding and are interested in quality management, process connections and continuous improvements.
  • Ideally, you already have initial knowledge or interest in problem-solving methods, risk management, change control, lean management or regulated production environments.
  • Your pronounced analytical skills and your structured, independent and reliable way of working enable you to systematically deal with complex questions.
  • You convince through your proactivity, teamwork and communication strength and enjoy working with different contacts.
  • Your secure use of MS Office as well as your very good knowledge of German and English allow you to communicate easily in our international environment.
