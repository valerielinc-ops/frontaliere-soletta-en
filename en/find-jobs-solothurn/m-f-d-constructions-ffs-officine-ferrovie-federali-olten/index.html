Head of construction (m/f/d) 60-100% | FFS

Move Switzerland with us. (m/f/d) constructions Olten / Work Smart, date to be convented, 60-100% Apply here

What we offer you.

If you want to make a difference, you're in the right place. An unusual assignment awaits you in a dynamic context. Together with your team, arrange performance and design purchases for building projects to proceed efficiently and people travel without barriers. A varied portfolio of projects is waiting for you, ranging from new constructions and transformations to stations, tunnels, bridges and entire railway routes. Work closely with project managers, engineering, legal area and other interfaces and assume responsibility with a tangible impact. What you can move.

  • Take care of technical and personnel management in the construction industry for the Infrastructure Division in your region
  • Provide about eight project purchasers with advice on procurement and contracts, in compliance with public procurement regulations, and agreed individual development measures.
  • Manage regional purchase operations based on commercial principles and sign contracts up to CHF 10 million.
  • As a member of the management team of Infrastructure Building Purchases, you collaborate in regional and national committees and actively take care of contacts with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Within working groups proponi ideas for the continuous development of processes and methods.
  • You are ready to travel within the region and regularly perform tasks in Olten, Lucerne and Bellinzona.

What you bring with you.

  • For this management position, management skills, such as results-oriented management and development are key.
  • Quickly acquire familiarity with new and complex issues, act in a result-oriented way and with a quality awareness. You have already gained some experience in escalation management and critical management and management situations.
  • Your strengths include an entrepreneurial and cost-conscious mindset as well as strong communication and moderation skills.
  • You have experience in the process of acquiring projects and/or in the construction industry.
  • You have a professional university degree (in engineering, business or equivalent) or you have a higher professional exam (Federal Chief Diploma or equivalent training).
  • You have an excellent command of the German (C2). You speak French or Italian with excellent knowledge (B2) of other national languages.

Salary and benefits.
