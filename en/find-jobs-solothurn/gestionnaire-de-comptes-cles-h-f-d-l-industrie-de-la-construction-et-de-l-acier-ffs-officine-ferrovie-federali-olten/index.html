Key Account Manager (m/f/d) Construction and Steel Industry 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Key Account Manager (m/f/d) Construction and Steel Industry Olten / Work Smart, October 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. At SBB Cargo, you have your finger on the pulse of the Swiss economy and get more goods on the rails in a climate-friendly way. SBB Cargo transports 185,000 tons every day, relieves road traffic by 15,000 truck journeys per day and saves the environment 460,000 tons of CO2 every year. In the industry focus on construction and steel, you will help shape the freight transport of the future and contribute directly to the success of our customers. You can move that.

  • By proactively managing your portfolio, you identify additional needs and develop tailor-made rail logistics solutions.
  • You will win new customers in the construction and steel industries and further develop the business with your existing major customer portfolio so that more goods are transported by rail in a climate-friendly manner.
  • In offer and tender processes, you negotiate framework agreements, prices and services independently and ensure that the deals are achieved safely.
  • Together with internal partners, you coordinate the operational implementation and accompany new projects from the idea to implementation.
  • Based on analyzes of traffic flows, customer needs and market trends, you derive measures to retain customers and acquire new customers.
  • By looking at the bigger picture, you will contribute to the further development of our service portfolio for construction and steel and ensure close integration with production and sales. This is what you bring with you.
  • You communicate clearly, bring together different interests and achieve viable solutions.
  • In demanding customer situations, you demonstrate negotiation skills and appropriate assertiveness.
  • With an analytical and strategic way of thinking, you recognize potential and translate it into marketable offers.
  • You have several years of experience in key account management, ideally in freight transport, logistics, construction or the steel industry and you understand rail transport such as wagonload transport, construction site logistics and intermodal transport.
  • You have completed higher education at a technical college, technical college or university in business administration, logistics or transport or an equivalent qualification and you are ready to travel within Switzerland.
  • You are fluent in German at level C2 and ideally you speak another national language and English at level B2.
