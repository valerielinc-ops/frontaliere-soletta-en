Full description

Shape living spaces for the future with us and become part of the nearly 90-year success story of Frey+Gnehm Ingenieure AG. We offer you a responsible and extraordinary challenge with excellent opportunities for further development.

Your Responsibilities

  • Independent processing and (co-)management of projects focusing on fire protection and related structural engineering topics
  • Creation and further development of fire protection concepts in accordance with VKF guidelines
  • Collaboration in interdisciplinary projects across various project phases (from planning to execution)
  • Coordination with architects, specialist planners, and authorities
  • Carrying out fire protection inspections on construction sites and supervising implementation
  • Advising clients and planners on fire protection and general structural engineering issues
  • Ensuring compliance with legal regulations and quality standards
  • Documentation and verification

Your Expertise

  • Training in the construction sector, e.g.: Architect, Civil Engineer, HF Construction Planning Technician, Construction Manager, or EFZ Draftsman specializing in Architecture/Civil Engineering with corresponding practical experience
  • Further training as a VKF fire protection specialist (mandatory)
  • Further training as a VKF fire protection expert is an advantage
  • Interest in a versatile activity with components outside of pure fire protection
  • Independent, structured, and solution-oriented way of working
  • Strong communication skills and a confident demeanor toward various stakeholder groups
  • Business-fluent German language skills in word and writing

We do not accept inquiries from personnel service providers.
