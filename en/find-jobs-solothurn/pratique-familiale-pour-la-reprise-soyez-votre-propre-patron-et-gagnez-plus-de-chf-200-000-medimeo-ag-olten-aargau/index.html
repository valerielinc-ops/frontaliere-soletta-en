You want to lead your own family practice, make your own decisions and work independently of large practice groups? There is a rare opportunity in the Canton of Aargau: an economically established individual practice with a loyal patient base, a long-standing MPA and exceptionally accommodating terms and conditions. Here you can shape your professional future yourself without having to start from scratch. In a growing community in the district of Bremgarten, a family practice that has been established for many years is under consideration. The current owner wants to retire after a long medical activity and is looking for a long-term succession solution. The practice is firmly anchored in the community, has a large patient base and is well connected locally. The need for primary care is high and many patients expressly want the practice to continue. Particularly valuable is the support of a medical practice assistant who has been working in practice for about 30 years. She knows the processes, the patients and the local conditions very well. The premises comprise around 150 m2 with two offices, a therapy room, laboratory and other practice rooms. The electronic medical history and the computer were constantly modernized. Individual medical devices can be renewed according to your own ideas.

Their tasks

  • Taking over an established individual practice with an existing patient base
  • Independent design of your office hours, working hours and medical orientation
  • Broad family practice with diagnosis, treatment and long-term patient care
  • Continuation of existing regional networking with other healthcare providers
  • Gradual modernization and further development of the practice according to your own ideas
  • If necessary, also examination of part-time models or joint practice management

Your Competences

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine or equivalent qualification recognised in Switzerland
  • Enjoyment of a broad and independent family doctor work
  • Desire to take on corporate responsibility and make your own decisions
  • Interested in long-term activity in a manageable community
  • Enjoying personal contact with a long-standing patient base and networking on site

Their advantages

  • More than CHF 200,000 income potential: The long-established practice offers an attractive economic perspective if utilization rates are comparable
  • Exceptionally fair takeover conditions: The previous owner attaches great importance to a successful succession and is very accommodating in terms of purchase price, payment methods and handover.
  • Attractive rental conditions: Significantly reduced rental rate and a landlord who is also willing to invest in the premises
  • Existing patient base: You take on a well-established practice with continuous demand instead of having to laboriously build up your own patient base first
  • Long-term support: An experienced MPA that has known the company and patients for decades
  • Full independence: No higher-level corporate structures or externally determined medical decisions
  • Flexible delivery: The previous owner is ready to personally accompany the successor during a transitional period if necessary
  • room for development: possibilities for modernization and, if necessary, spatial expansion
  • Good accessibility: Attractive residential region in the Aargau with proximity to Zurich, Zug and Baden
  • Little emergency service burden: The regular regional emergency service is largely covered by a central structure
