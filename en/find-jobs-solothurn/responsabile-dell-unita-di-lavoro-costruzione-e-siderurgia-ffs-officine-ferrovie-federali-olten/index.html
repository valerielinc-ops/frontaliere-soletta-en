Head of Business Unit Construction & Siderurgy 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Head of Business Unit Construction & Siderurgy Olten / Work Smart, October 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. At SBB Güterverkehr you have your finger on the pulse of the Swiss economy. As head of the Construction & Siderurgy business unit, you are responsible for an important business area with strategically important customers and actively shape its further development. You can expect a demanding market environment, an experienced team and a task with a lot of responsibility and creative freedom

  • close to our customers and the business. You can move that.
  • Together with your team of 8 employees, you are responsible for strategically important key accounts and a business volume of around CHF 180 million.
  • You will further develop existing customer relationships in the long term, identify new business opportunities and win customers for suitable transport solutions.
  • With a good feel for the market, customers and economic relationships, you will continuously develop the business unit and its market development.
  • During important customer and contract negotiations, you bring together different interests and find economically viable solutions.
  • As a manager, you provide orientation, set clear priorities and create an environment in which personal responsibility and collaboration are practiced.
  • Internally and externally, you maintain an open and constructive exchange and bring the relevant partners together. This is what you bring with you.
  • With your winning and authentic manner, you create trust and meet customers, employees and partners on an equal footing.
  • You enjoy taking on responsibility and are reliable. Even in challenging situations, you maintain an overview and find pragmatic solutions.
  • Several years of management and/or sales experience in a comparable position as well as a convincing B2B background form the basis for this task.
  • Ideally, you are already at home in the freight transport, transport, logistics, construction or siderurgical environment and know the corresponding market mechanisms and customer needs.
  • Your in-depth understanding of the market and business enables you to quickly grasp connections and represent decisions in a comprehensible manner.
  • A university degree (University/FH) as well as business fluent German and good knowledge of English and French (B1) complete your profile.
