Full description

The veterinary service in the Office for Agriculture is responsible for the implementation of animal welfare, animal diseases and veterinary medicine legislation as well as food legislation in the field of animal husbandry and slaughter in the canton of Solothurn. In the area of food safety, we are looking for an official assistant in meat, 80-100%, as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement at the Oensingen location.

Your responsibility

  • Together with other official assistants, they carry out the meat inspection on the slaughter belt of Switzerland’s largest and most modern cattle slaughterhouse.
  • Your responsibilities include other tasks such as hygiene controls, sampling, ante-mortem and meat inspection on a small farm, etc.

Your profile

  • You have completed a vocational apprenticeship in the food sector, preferably as a butcher or meat specialist and ideally have the certificate of competence as an official assistant or are ready to acquire it.
  • High resilience, flexibility and teamwork are among your strengths and you communicate with customers in a friendly and clear manner.
  • Craftsmanship and good physical condition are basic requirements for this activity.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity for mobile work
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
