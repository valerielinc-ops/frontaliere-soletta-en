Full description

Vehicle and maintenance specialist - - - - The SBB Historical Heritage Foundation (SBB Historic) maintains the rich and successful history of Switzerland as a railway country. On the one hand, our tasks include collecting, preserving, preserving, documenting and archiving contemporary witnesses to Swiss railway history. On the other hand, we make these valuable witnesses of Swiss cultural and economic history accessible to the public - among other things, by offering adventure trips and rail adventures with our extensive historic vehicle fleet. Vehicle and maintenance specialist: in 80-100% Olten, as of February 1, 2027

Your tasks

  • Carrying out demanding maintenance and repair work on our historic vehicles through to overhaul work on vehicles or components
  • Troubleshooting and troubleshooting
  • Planning and carrying out preventative maintenance measures on the vehicles
  • Support in the development and maintenance of the necessary technical. Maintenance documents
  • Accompanying trips as an on-board mechanic

Your profile

  • Basic technical training as a diagnostician, mechanic, electrician or similar
  • Good technical railway know-how
  • Good knowledge of rail vehicle maintenance processes
  • Knowledge or willingness to familiarize yourself with the ECM topic for the maintenance of rail vehicles
  • Independent way of working with a balanced awareness of quality and safety
  • Ability to work in a team and willingness to undertake occasional assignments across Switzerland
  • Affinity for carpentry work is another plus
