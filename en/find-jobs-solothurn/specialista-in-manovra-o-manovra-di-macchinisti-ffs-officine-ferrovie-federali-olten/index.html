Train driver: in shunting or shunting specialist: in 60-100% | SBB - - - - -

  • Move Switzerland with us. Train driver: in shunting or shunting specialist: in Olten, from now on or by appointment, 60-100% Apply now ## You can look forward to this. An exciting, innovative working environment awaits you in which you can drive big changes and thus contribute to the digitalization of SBB. At UHR (maintenance rolling stock) you will have a key position in the Olten plant, with many tasks and responsibilities in order to support our customers with your full commitment. Together with your teammates, you will develop solutions to current challenges and implement them as a team. Further training is also not neglected for individual professional development. At the Olten plant you are currently working in two shifts from Monday to Friday between 4:45 a.m. and 5:45 p.m. You can move that.
  • As a shunting specialist, you move passenger and freight train cars under the guidance of the maneuver coordinator.
  • Your area of ​​activity includes, among other things, attaching and detaching the various wagons and operating the manual switches and the signal box. You work in accordance with the transport service regulations and the prescribed standards of the SBB.
  • Commissioning and driving multiple units and articulated trains are also part of your area of ​​responsibility.
  • You drive shunting locomotives, road-rail vehicles, carry out brake tests and ensure that they are ready to drive. You are also responsible for the small maintenance of the vehicles.
  • Customer satisfaction is one of our top goals. You work for our customers every day. You live our safety culture and constantly apply the continuous improvement process (CIP). This is what you bring with you.
  • In order for you to be able to carry out this responsible task successfully, you must be mentally and physically fit and in good shape.
  • You are characterized by your networked thinking and actions, your resilience, your independence and your willingness to take responsibility.
  • Strong team play as well as an above-average customer and quality orientation are further attributes that describe your character well.
  • For the demanding position, you need to have completed basic EFZ training, ideally as a logistics specialist in transport or similar training in a craft/technical direction.
  • Ideally, you have training and experience in shunting, combined with driving accompaniment in accordance with the BAV ID card VTE A40 direct. Alternatively, you are an Ai40 shunting attendant indirectly and are willing to take part in VTE A40 training.
  • You have good written and oral language skills in German (at least level B2). ## Your salary and your benefits. We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information about the wage system at SBB can be viewed transparently. This position is at requirement level E, regional allowance level 0.
