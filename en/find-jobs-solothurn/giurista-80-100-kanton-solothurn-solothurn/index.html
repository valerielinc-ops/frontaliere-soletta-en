Full description

The Office for Spatial Planning is committed to ensuring that the canton of Solothurn and its regions and municipalities remain an attractive living space in the future. For the Services department, we are looking for a lawyer, 80-100%, as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement at the Solothurn location.

Your responsibility

  • They support the administration and the specialist departments in planning and building law issues.
  • One focus is on construction outside the construction zone and the cantonal building authority.
  • In the event of conflicts of use, you determine the respective interests and weigh them carefully against each other.
  • They ensure quality assurance in selected co-reports, in answering parliamentary proposals, in departmental orders and government approval decisions.
  • With the Legal Service of the Department of Construction and Justice you maintain the professional exchange.

Your profile

  • For this key function, you bring a completed legal degree (MLaw/lic.iur.), several years of professional experience and interest in questions in construction and planning law.
  • A lawyer's patent and/or additional training in planning and building law are advantageous.
  • In everyday work, you demonstrate negotiating skills, assertiveness and political flair.
  • You are able to identify relevant questions at an early stage and bring your thoughts to the point in stylish German.
  • Rapid comprehension, resilience and an affinity for digitalization processes round off your profile.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
