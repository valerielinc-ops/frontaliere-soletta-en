SBB | SBB Cargo International: Specialist:in Management Systems (m/f/d)

SBB Cargo International is the market leader in European rail freight transport on the north-south axis. Thanks to our more than 1,000 employees in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Italy and France, we travel around 550 trains a week from the North Sea ports to Italy and back. In doing so, we are shifting thousands of net tonnes from road to rail and making an active contribution to more climate protection. To complement our team, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement for the area of Safety & Quality Management in Olten: Specialist: in Management Systems (m/f/d)

Your tasks

  • Together with those responsible for processes, processes, risks and topics, you ensure the continuous improvement of our management systems and the implementation of new requirements.
  • You ensure the certification and audit capability of our management systems.
  • You are responsible for selected parts of the management systems, such as audit and action management.
  • You advise other organizational units and company units on topics related to management systems and also take on tasks in the specialist management.
  • You assume the function of data protection consultant in accordance with the Swiss Data Protection Act (DSG) and advise the relevant authorities on data protection issues.[LS1.1]
  • As a super user, you manage our IT tool for process and document management.
  • You take over program and project management.
  • You conduct internal audits.

Your profile

  • Several years of experience with management systems, especially in quality management according to ISO 9001.
  • Qualification and practical experience as an auditor according to ISO 19011.
  • Qualification and/or experience in the training of employees as well as in project and competence management.
  • Ideally, knowledge and/or qualification in environmental management according to ISO 14001
  • Additional knowledge in the fields of data protection or information security is beneficial.
  • Secure handling of common IT applications and Microsoft 365
  • In-depth knowledge or qualifications are welcome.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Knowledge of Italian, Dutch or French is an advantage.
  • An independent, structured and solution-oriented working method as well as an open and proactive communication.
  • Conceptual thinking, a quick apprehension and a high willingness to learn.
  • It is a pleasure to win people over to topics and drive change forward together.
  • Willingness to travel occasionally.

Our offer

  • Responsible work in international freight transport.
  • Design leeway and the opportunity to actively develop our management systems.
  • Cooperation in an interdisciplinary and international environment.
  • A strong team and modern workplaces in Olten.
  • Language courses and training opportunities.
  • Employee Perks and GA 1st Class.

Get on board and keep Europe’s freight traffic moving with us!
