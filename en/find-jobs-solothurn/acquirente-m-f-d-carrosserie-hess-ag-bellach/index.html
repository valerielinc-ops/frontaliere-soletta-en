Strategic buyer SCM (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 500 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company.

In the supply chain management team in our area SCM, we are looking for an experienced and qualified strategic buyer SCM. Together with us, shape the future of mobility. In this versatile function, you will be responsible for strategic procurement projects, develop international supplier partnerships and drive cost, quality and process optimization. With your commitment and expertise, you strengthen the performance of our supply chain and create sustainable added value for the HESS Group.

Their tasks

  • Technical responsibility for the assigned product groups
  • Independent management and implementation of procurement and supply chain projects
  • Planning and realization of relocations of procurement sources to Western Europe
  • Strategic negotiations with existing and new key suppliers
  • Development, negotiation and implementation of supplier contracts with focus on strategic and congestion suppliers
  • Execution of supplier audits and support of First Article Inspections (FAI)
  • Active involvement of suppliers in product development and project processes (early supplier involvement)
  • Design and implementation of cost optimization and quality improvement measures
  • Close cross-divisional cooperation with engineering, quality, logistics, production and internal procurement

Our requirements

  • Completed technical or commercial training with further training in purchasing or supply chain management
  • Sound knowledge of supply chain management and project management
  • Several years of professional experience in strategic purchasing and international sourcing
  • Demonstrable negotiation successes with strategically relevant suppliers
  • Resilient, communication-strong, solution-oriented and implementation-strong personality
  • Readiness to travel within Europe, travel activity > 40 %
  • Strength of enforcement, negotiating skills and intercultural competence
  • German and English negotiable, ideally Portuguese

We offer them

  • Responsible work in an exciting and innovative environment
  • Interesting and challenging task area in a versatile company
  • Great design possibilities and a lot of personal responsibility
  • Participation in the entire group
  • Contemporary remuneration and work model
  • Long-term perspectives
