Full description

Bachtelen is involved in the canton of Solothurn with a wide range of offers for the promotion, support and integration of young people. This includes special schools, various housing offers, individually coordinated therapy offers as well as consultations and day care centers. Bachtelen employs around 550 people. The school and vocational training sector offers resource-oriented teaching with individualised support. Our students: inside bring a special educational need. Individual education, personal development and preparation for the profession are the focus. In the focus on basic cognition we are looking for a school at Breitengasse 11 in Grenchen as of 01.01.2027:n School:n curative education:in (cycle 3) 45 % 13 lessons, working days Monday and Tuesday

Your work includes

  • You lead a high school class in cooperation with the class teacher and design an everyday lesson in which young people discover their strengths and gain independence.
  • You plan and check the individual support and make learning and development progress visible in school reports.
  • You maintain an open exchange with the parents and search for suitable learning paths together with the interdisciplinary team.
  • You coordinate the deployment of your class team in everyday school life and work in partnership with your teammates.
  • You bring your ideas to school and class projects and develop our school together with the team.

This is what you bring with you

  • A master’s degree in curative curative education or at least. an EDK-approved teaching diploma at the appropriate level.
  • Interest in the lives of young people and joy in accompanying young people with special educational needs on their way.
  • A friendly way, clear communication and enjoyment of working with parents and professionals.
  • A sense of responsibility, an independent working method and flexibility if a school day goes differently than planned.
  • Curiosity and desire to reflect on your work, to educate yourself and to contribute your own ideas.

This is what we offer you

  • An attractive workplace with plenty of room for initiative and participation
  • Flat hierarchies, short decision paths and an open, appreciative cooperation
  • Contemporary employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • A meaningful work environment where your work makes a real difference
  • Generous support (money and/or time) for further education
  • Various employee events
  • Daily two paid short breaks – including free coffee
