Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient. As a project manager, you analyze and design. What counts: mindset, communication and organizational sense.

Mansioni

  • Managing projects that drive personnel development, organizational development or digitalization
  • Analysis of HR issues, development of solutions and development of decision bases
  • Design of HR tools and measures to improve the candidate, employee and manager experience along the entire life cycle
  • Promoting process optimisation
  • Collaboration with professionals and managers – competent, clear and at eye level
  • Active networking in the HR team, with experts within the Migros Group and with internal customers across all eight locations
  • Comprehensible and target group-oriented communication in German and French

Requirements

  • 3-5 years of experience: HR, consulting or digital environment (IT, AI, software)
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): HR, project management or similar
  • Cross-enterprises with a suitable experience profile are welcome
  • German (very good knowledge) French (good knowledge): French: actively used in everyday work
  • Experience in managing projects and designing structures, training and decision-making bases, as well as creating and presenting analyses, recommendations and inputs using a wide range of tools and methods
  • Experience working with different stakeholders – from executives to operational teams, with different languages and backgrounds
  • Enjoy complex issues, new challenges and continuous learning as well as advanced technical skills in dealing with Microsoft 365, Jira and various AI tools
  • Addressable communication and the ability to make complex things understandable
  • Structured thinking, conceptual strength and overview also on a wide range of topics
  • Independent, initiative working methods, personal responsibility and a pragmatic approach to challenges
  • Pragmatic approach to challenges, continuous willingness to learn and the ability to successfully advance topics
