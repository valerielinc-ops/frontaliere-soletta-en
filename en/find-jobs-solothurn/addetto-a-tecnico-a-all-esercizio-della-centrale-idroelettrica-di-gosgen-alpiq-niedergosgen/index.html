About the Role

Employment type: Permanent ¦ Location: Niedergösgen

Our Company

Alpiq is a leading Swiss electricity producer and energy service provider with a European presence. With a strong portfolio of hydropower, nuclear energy and renewable energies we make an active contribution to climate protection and supply security.

Our employees from various nations work together towards one goal: Together for a better climate and an improved security of supply.

Mission

Shape the future of energy with us! Alpiq Hydro Aare AG ensures sustainable electricity production with its three run‑of‑river power plants. For our facilities in Niedergösgen we are looking for an engaged personality who enjoys technology and wants to take on responsibility.

Your main responsibilities

  • Support the leadership and organization of the operations team to ensure safe, economical and legally compliant operation.
  • Independent inspection, maintenance and repairs on the plants; execution of switching orders; analysis and resolution of faults.
  • Safe and considered action during operational disturbances as well as execution of minor repairs.
  • Conduct electrical, mechanical and hydraulic inspections as well as maintenance work, external work on the spillway, boat transfers, small surrounding tasks.
  • Lead small teams, supervise fitters and technicians, ensure occupational safety.
  • Maintain inspection and maintenance plans, documentation, handling of small projects and optimisations.

Your Impact

The extensive, modern power‑plant facilities offer you exciting and challenging tasks, where after thorough onboarding you can actively and hands‑on contribute. As a plant specialist you will be supported by a well‑coordinated and highly experienced operations team and a collegial working atmosphere in a forward‑looking, local and meaningful energy production.

Your profile

  • Basic training in the electrical or mechanical field with technical further education (e.g. Technician HF).
  • Professional experience in the power‑plant sector or comparable industry with practical maintenance experience is an advantage.
  • Willingness for on‑call duty every 5‑6 weeks (Niedergösgen and Winznau should be reachable within 30 minutes from your home).
  • Expertise in mechanics, hydraulics, electrical engineering, automation; organization and documentation of processes.
  • Self‑responsible, resilient, team‑oriented and flexible.
  • Fluent German, English and French are a plus.
  • Driver’s licence category B.
