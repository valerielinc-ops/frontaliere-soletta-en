Ready for your first senior medical position in Switzerland – with exciting risk birth support and an experienced team at your side? Here you will grow into your new role with sound training and without time pressure – Irène Grüninger accompanies you personally when you move from Germany. The women’s clinic of a center hospital in German-speaking Switzerland offers a broad spectrum in gynecology and obstetrics. In addition to the conservative and surgical treatment of gynecological diseases, the care of high-risk pregnancies and premature births are part of everyday clinical life. This allows you to find a versatile environment in which you can contribute your previous experience and expand your skills in a targeted manner. For your entry, a very experienced medical team is at your side. You will get to know the processes and the special features of Swiss hospital life carefully and gradually grow into your responsibility. Your further professional development is also supported – with personal support and opportunities for further education and training.

Their tasks

  • Care of gynecological and obstetric patients in the ambulatory and inpatient area
  • Accompanying at-risk pregnancies and births as well as assessing complex obstetric situations in a team
  • Performing gynecological operations according to your level of training and gradually expanding your operational experience
  • Participation in the supra-medical service after careful training
  • Guidance and accompaniment of assistant doctors in everyday clinical practice
  • Close cooperation with midwives, care and other disciplines

Your Competences

  • Completed specialist training in gynecology and obstetrics, for example in Germany
  • In-depth clinical training, ideally at a university hospital or a medium-sized hospital with a broad range of care
  • Experience in obstetrics, including the care of high-risk pregnancies
  • Interested in a versatile senior doctor work in gynecology and obstetrics
  • Responsible work and willingness to continuously develop your skills
  • Enjoyment of professional exchange and trusting cooperation in the team

Their advantages

  • Accompanied entry into the role as a senior physician: You are also welcome as a newly qualified specialist or newly qualified specialist
  • Sound training without time pressure: You get time to get to know the processes and gain security in your new function
  • Very experienced team: professional support and exchange in challenging decisions
  • Starting salary from CHF 150,000 gross per year for a full-time workload
  • Broad clinical spectrum: Classical gynecology, surgery and risk obstetrics
  • Supported further development: Temporal and/or financial support for further education and training according to company agreement and a free internal course offer
  • Family and work: 80-100% employment and company childcare
  • Assistance when moving to Switzerland: Medimeo supports Recognizing your degrees and the organizational issues surrounding your new start
