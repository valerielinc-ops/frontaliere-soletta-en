Full description

Are you looking for a new professional challenge in the open air? At BKW Power Grid, you work together with your team on the power grid and ensure that the electricity arrives reliably where it is needed.

Your field of activity

  • Together with your colleagues: you build, operate and maintain the distribution network of BKW
  • You will take over assembly, maintenance and troubleshooting work on our plants in the low- and medium-voltage distribution network with focus on 132–0.4 kV as well as on long-range and regulated overhead lines
  • You lay cable lines, maintain overhead lines and repair damage as part of the Picetting and Disturbance Service

You bring that with you

  • An apprenticeship as a grid electrician:in EFZ, electrical installer:in EFZ or assembly electrician:in EFZ or a craftsman background and the willingness to familiarize yourself with grid construction
  • Experience in electrical installations or network construction
  • An independent, reliable working method and enjoyment of working together in a team
  • Category B or C driving licence
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
