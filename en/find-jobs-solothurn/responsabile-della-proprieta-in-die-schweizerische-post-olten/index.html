Full description

You are responsible for one of the most exciting commercial real estate portfolios in Switzerland. With your expertise, you actively shape the further development of Post’s business and operating properties in the Basel / Bern region. You have several years of experience in the management of commercial properties and appreciate a versatile, demanding environment? With us, you take responsibility for an attractive portfolio and contribute your well-founded expertise. Apply now, because with you our properties are in good hands.

You can do that.

  • You independently manage a varied real estate portfolio with commercial, operational and selected residential properties of Swiss Post in the Basel/Berne region.
  • You ensure the highest possible rental rate, develop marketing measures and take care of active vacancy management.
  • As a contact person for ownership, you maintain customer relationships, advise on management issues and work closely with tenants, home maintenance, authorities, planners and external partners.
  • You regularly carry out real estate inspections, assess the condition of the properties and take over the acceptance and handover of rental properties for first rentals and tenant changes.
  • The management of floor and co-ownership properties as well as participation in owner meetings and meetings are also part of your area of responsibility.
  • You ensure the maintenance of the buildings, recognize opportunities for optimization and participate in renovation, conversion and development projects.

You bring that with you

  • You have an oath. Professional certificate in real estate management or a comparable further education and brings in-depth expertise in real estate management.
  • Several years of experience in the management of commercial and business properties enables you to work independently and reliably.
  • You approach change openly and use digital tools and systems safely in your everyday work.
  • A solution-oriented attitude and a structured and responsible way of working distinguish you.
  • With your category B driving license, you can flexibly reach our different locations.
  • Thanks to your very good knowledge of German and your open nature, it is easy for you to interact successfully with different stakeholders.
