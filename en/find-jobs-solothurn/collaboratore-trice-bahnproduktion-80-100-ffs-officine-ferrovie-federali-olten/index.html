Full description

Employees: in railway production 80-100 % - - - - The SBB Historic Heritage Foundation (SBB Historic) cultivates the rich and successful history of the Swiss railway state. One of our tasks is to collect, preserve, preserve, document and archive eyewitnesses of Swiss railway history. On the other hand, we make these valuable witnesses to Swiss cultural and economic history accessible to the public – among other things, by offering adventure rides and rail adventures with our extensive historical vehicle fleet. Employees: in railway production 80-100 % Olten, as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement

Their tasks

  • Support and processing of the customer charter business including coordination with the maintenance of the rolling stock
  • Creation of offers for special and charter trips
  • Ensuring customer contact
  • Planning and preparation of voyages
  • Planning of train and locomotive staff and service staff for the journeys
  • Participation in the project Further development of railway production (SMS, RU)
  • Organization of events / catering
  • Support for railway production management

Your profile

  • Commercial training
  • Education/experience in tourism and/or railway experience (disposition, switch, public transport)
  • Good knowledge of German, French, English word and writing
  • Good IT user knowledge MS Office
  • Readiness for Switzerland-wide missions, if necessary also on weekends

Our offer

  • Success-oriented and helpful team
  • Training opportunities
