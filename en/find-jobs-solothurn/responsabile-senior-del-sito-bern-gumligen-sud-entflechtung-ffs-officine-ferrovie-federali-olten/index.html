  • As a superstructure manager, you are SBB’s client representative in the execution phase. You lead the project on behalf of the overall project management and work closely with internal and external specialist areas in the project planning and implementation in order to lead them to success.
  • Along the SIA project planning phases, you are responsible for construction phase planning, interval planning (track blocking), logistics planning and the submission of third-party and own services.
  • In the SIA implementation phases, you take over as the main responsible:r contractual partner: the management of the execution until commissioning. You keep a consistent eye on the project goals in the dimensions of safety, costs, deadlines and quality and ensure their achievement.
  • Together with our business partners, you are responsible for monitoring and auditing contracts as well as negotiating supplements.
  • Proactive communication with internal clients, authorities, offices, residents and other stakeholders is part of your everyday life – binding, solution-oriented and on an equal footing.
  • You drive innovation and optimize processes in your area of responsibility to further increase efficiency, safety and quality.

You bring that with you.

  • Project work in railway infrastructure construction gives you pleasure in all project phases, with the main focus on submission and realization.
  • With high resilience and quick comprehension, you win internal and external stakeholders for your concerns and make the right tone in contact with authorities, offices, residents and contractual partners.
  • You like to get out of the comfort zone, tackle topics proactively and lead projects independently and confidently to success.
  • A degree in civil engineering (ETH/FH), training as a construction manager: Technician: in HF or training Baumeister: in you have completed and preferably bring several years of professional experience, mainly in the realization phase.
  • In German you express yourself excellently, both verbally and in writing (min. C1). We require very good IT user knowledge.
