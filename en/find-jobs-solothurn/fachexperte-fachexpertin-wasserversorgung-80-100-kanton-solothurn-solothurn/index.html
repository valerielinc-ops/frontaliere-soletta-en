Full description

The Office for the Environment ensures a sustainable approach to the environment. The Water Department is responsible for municipal water management and water protection. For the water department, we are looking for a specialist expert for water supply, 80-100%, as of 01.12.2026 or by agreement at the Solothurn site.

Your responsibility

  • You are technically responsible for General Water Supply Planning (GWP).
  • You independently initiate projects for the implementation of the Solothurn Water Network (SWAN).
  • They advise and accompany municipalities and associations on issues of municipal water management, including general drainage planning (GEP).
  • Your tasks include checking and approving various plans as well as the associated construction projects.
  • Working in interdisciplinary working groups and in the cantonal claims service panel is also one of your tasks.

Your profile

  • You have completed a university degree in engineering (construction/environment) or in the environmental sector or a comparable higher technical training in construction or environmental engineering with several years of experience in municipal water management and/or water protection.
  • Your in-depth expertise as well as your experience in project management and project management enable you to work in trust with partners and stakeholders.
  • Ideally, you will have several years of practice in engineering offices or public administration.
  • They are characterised by strong negotiating skills, team spirit and enthusiasm.
  • Interdisciplinary work and networked thinking are a matter of course for you, and you are open to new tasks and challenges.
