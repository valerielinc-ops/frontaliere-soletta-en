Are you a pediatrician or pediatrician and looking for a job in Switzerland? This group practice has been established for many years, pediatrics is established and demand is high. An experienced pediatrician works you in, and you start with a fixed wage plus turnover share, on request also part-time. The long-established group practice belongs to a practice group of basic care that is broadly networked in German-speaking Switzerland. Pediatrics and adolescent medicine has been firmly established and well utilized for years. We are looking for reinforcement, not rebuilding, because there is a lack of paediatricians in the region. A very experienced pediatrician is already working at the site, with whom you will carry the pediatrics for two in the future. The practice group takes over the entire administration, so that you can concentrate on your patients. The practice is located in the lower Aaretal in the canton of Aargau, near the water castle, where the rivers Aare, Reuss and Limmat flow together. The Aargau is located between Zurich and Basel and combines rural quality of life with short distances to the centers. Baden can be reached by public transport in about half an hour, Zurich in just under an hour, and the regional trains run to Waldshut, short distances to family and friends in Germany. In leisure, hiking and cycling trails lure along the Aare and on the Siggenberg, the thermal baths in Baden and the nearby Black Forest.

Their tasks

  • Comprehensive outpatient care for children and adolescents, around 20 to 25 patients: insiders per day
  • Advice and guidance for parents and families
  • Close cooperation with your pediatric colleague and the family medical team
  • Participation in practical development
  • Participation in quality programmes

Your Competences

  • Specialist title in paediatric medicine, acquired in Switzerland or Germany
  • Professional experience as a specialist, from the hospital or from practice
  • Joy in working with children and families, empathetic appearance
  • Team spirit, commitment and a very good knowledge of German
  • A MEBEKO recognition is not a prerequisite: Medimeo accompanies you through the procedure and supports you in an advisory and financial way.

Their advantages

  • Structured training by a very experienced paediatrician on site
  • Fixum of CHF 170,000 plus revenue share
  • Pension fund including super-compulsory: Also the wage share above the legal minimum is insured, the employer pays with. This means a significantly higher pension than with the basic protection.
  • Continued pay in the event of illness 90% instead of the usual 80% in Switzerland
  • Congress fees for further training are covered
  • FMH membership and professional liability insurance paid by the employer
  • No administration: Wage, patient and insurance bills are handled by the practice group
