You can look forward to that.

SBB’s rail, telecom and energy network comprises a huge number of facilities and objects. To ensure that up-to-date geographic inventory data is always available for planning, expansion and maintenance work, SBB records and manages its investment objects in the spatial information system (RIS). As a representative: in the Department of Data Management you are on the road in several multidisciplinary projects at the same time. You ensure operational data management in the infrastructure projects. In the projects that are implemented with the BIM methodology, you take over the coordination of the existing and specialist models.   • In the construction projects of the SBB Infrastructure division, you take over the operational data management.
