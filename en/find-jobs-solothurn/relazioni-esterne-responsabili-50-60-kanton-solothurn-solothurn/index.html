Full description

The State Chancellery is the staff office of the Government Council and supports it in the performance of its government and leadership tasks. It ensures the coordination of government activities, provides central services for the cantonal administration and ensures the connection to the Cantonal Council. It also supports the Government Council in the performance of foreign relations and the representation of the interests of the canton. For the areas of foreign relations, advocacy and political monitoring, we are looking for a responsible foreign relations at the Solothurn location as of 01.02.2027 or by agreement, 50-60%.

Your responsibility

  • They observe and assess relevant political developments and business at federal level as well as in the intercantonal environment, including important consultation templates.
  • You will identify the need for action and positioning at an early stage and create situation assessments, briefings and decision-making bases.
  • Her tasks include handling business in the areas of foreign relations, political monitoring and advocacy, drafting the relevant decisions of the Government Council and participating in relevant projects and working groups.
  • They support the State Clerk and the Government Council in foreign relations, prepare dossiers for conferences, committees and delegation visits and prepare speeches and welcome messages for the State Clerk in particular.
  • As part of the advocacy group, you maintain political and institutional networks and coordinate exchange with the Solothurn members of the Federal Assembly

Your profile

  • They have a university or university of applied sciences degree, preferably in political, legal or political sciences or public management, or comparable education and experience.
  • Experience in the political environment or in public administration as well as very good knowledge of the Swiss political system and its institutional processes are required.
  • Strong analytical, conceptual and editorial skills as well as political flair and strategic, networked thinking distinguish you.
  • With communication strength and network competence, you can move safely at the interfaces between politics and administration and work in a team- and coordination-oriented manner.
  • They work independently, structured, forward-looking and flexible and have high discretion, loyalty and reliability. Very good German and French skills are required
