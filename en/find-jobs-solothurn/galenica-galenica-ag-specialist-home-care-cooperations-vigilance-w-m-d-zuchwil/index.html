Full description

Location: MediService AG, Ausserfeldweg 1, 4528 Zuchwil

Do you want to help shape home care offerings while keeping an eye on quality and safety? In this versatile function, you control cooperation and processes from implementation to quality monitoring. You work closely with internal and external partners and contribute your ideas to the optimization, standardization and digitalization of processes.

You can do that.

  • You control and coordinate home care cooperation and patient support programs and ensure that agreed services, deadlines and quality requirements are implemented reliably.
  • You control and monitor defined vigilance processes, coordinate messages, follow-ups, quality controls and reconciliations and ensure timely processing.
  • You accompany manufacturer audits from preparation to follow-up, coordinate measures from audit findings and escalate key findings and compliance risks to the responsible manager.
  • You keep an eye on quality and performance, monitor KPIs and contractual requirements, prepare reports and evaluations, and coordinate required training and proof.
  • You ensure correct and timely billing of services to manufacturers and medical practices and coordinate the materials required for home care programs.
  • You will continue to develop the operational processes together with the participating departments, bring in optimization, automation and digitalization potential and support the implementation of new and adapted processes.

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed education or study in health care, life sciences, pharmacy, nursing or a comparable specialty as well as several years of professional experience in the health care, pharmaceutical environment or other regulated environment.
  • You can translate contractual and regulatory requirements into workable processes and bring a strong understanding of quality management, compliance, audits and comprehensible documentation.
  • You work independently, structured and reliable, set the right focus even with changing priorities and take responsibility for your tasks.
  • You communicate securely with different internal and external stakeholders and enjoy working across different areas
  • Experience with pharmaceutical companies, home care providers and in process, quality or project management is an advantage.
  • Ideally, you will have knowledge of pharmacovigilance / vigilance as well as regulatory and quality-relevant requirements and are interested in further developing operational processes with digital work equipment.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as good knowledge of English
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage.

We fill this position without external personnel service providers.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Extend competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

Individual pharmaceutical care Mediservice is a company in the Galenica network and supports people with chronic or rare illnesses through specialized pharmaceutical care - at home, but also by phone and video. As employees of Mediservice, we have the task of making the lives of our patients at home easier. Furthermore, we deliver complex and expensive medicines, offer various specialized services and impart health knowledge as well as tips and tricks for everyday use with medicines. Mediservice employs pharmacists, nurses and logistics staff. You make your very personal contribution to our patients to increase their independence and improve their quality of life.
