Full description

Technician: for electrical and telecommunications systems 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Technician: for electrical and telecommunications systems Olten, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You make an important contribution to smooth public transport. In the Telecommunications team, you will work together with experts from different disciplines and drive the digitalization of SBB forward. You get involved in both electrical and telecommunications systems and develop pragmatic solutions together with your teammate. An innovative working environment with a lot of creative leeway and perspectives for further development awaits you. Benefits: Benefits such as a free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. After successful training, there is the prospect of taking over in a permanent employment relationship. You can move that.

  • After various introductory courses, you take over as a technician: in the construction, commissioning and maintenance of electrical and telecommunications systems on the railway infrastructure and thus contribute to availability and safety.
  • You coordinate small orders and conversions independently and ensure the smooth commissioning.
  • In a small team, you ensure the quality of the systems and actively contribute ideas for improvement.
  • In the event of disruptions, you will analyze the cause and correct errors in the electrical environment of the railway infrastructure.

You bring that with you.

  • Your independence and teamwork characterize you and you stay calm even in hectic situations and keep the overview.
  • For the continuous further education, you bring a lot of motivation and willingness to learn and are happy to perform sporadically pikettdienst, night work and weekend work.
  • You are attentive, focused and physically resilient to successfully accomplish this demanding work. You also like to work outside in any weather.
  • You have completed basic training as an electric installer: in EFZ, assembly electrician: in EFZ or similar and have a driving licence of category B. Sound telecommunications skills are an advantage.

Your reward and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. For this reason, further and detailed information on the wage system can be viewed transparently at SBB. This job is in requirement level E, regional allowance level 0. More about wages and benefits.
