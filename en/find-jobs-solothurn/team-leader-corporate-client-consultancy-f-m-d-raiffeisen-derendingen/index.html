Full description

Raiffeisenbank Wasseramt-Buchsi is a locally anchored, modern and future-oriented bank with a personal corporate culture. Our values – competent, close and personal – shape our daily activities.

In the context of a succession regulation, we are looking for an experienced personality who thinks entrepreneurially, acts in a customer-oriented manner and is responsible for the further development of our corporate customer team.

Tasks

  • Professional and personnel management of the team Corporate Consulting
  • Ensuring holistic, need-oriented advice and support for corporate clients
  • Active management and targeted expansion of the own corporate portfolio
  • Further development of corporate banking business within the framework of the banking strategy
  • Participation in target group-specific market processing, performance of representative tasks and network maintenance in our region

Requirements

  • Banking training with further training in banking, finance or business
  • Multi-annual experience in a bank's corporate business, ideally in the management of SMEs
  • Well-founded knowledge in holistic corporate consulting with a focus on credit and investment
  • Leadership experience in a comparable function or motivation to assume responsibility in a leadership role
  • A strong entrepreneurial thinking and a good sense for customers, market and risks
  • Valuable, clear leadership style and joy in cooperation and further development
  • Regional anchoring is an advantage

We offer: The cooperative values are our cornerstones: together. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.
