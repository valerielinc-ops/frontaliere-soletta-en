Are you shortly before the specialist title or have just acquired it and would like to take your next career step consciously in Switzerland? In a wide-ranging women’s clinic, you will expand your operational skills, gradually take on more responsibility and find a team in which you will arrive quickly. A medium-sized acute hospital of a cantonal hospital group is looking for reinforcements for its women's clinic. The spectrum ranges from benign gynecology to gynecological oncology and urogynecology to obstetrics, with activities in the operating room, on the ward and in the ambulatory. The clinic is recognized as a training center for gynecology and obstetrics, even full for operative gynecology and obstetrics as well as gynecological senology. Of particular note is the stable, well-established team: The cooperation is collegial, the mood is very good and the structures are manageable. A hospital-owned nursery is also available at the site. Medimeo accompanied You personally: Together we clarify which possibilities fit your training status, your professional goals and your ideas, and support you on your way to German-speaking Switzerland.

Their tasks

  • Care of gynecological and obstetric patients in the operating room, on the ward and in the ambulatory
  • Operational activity with the possibility of developing your profile in a targeted manner
  • Services for gynecological and obstetric emergencies
  • Gradual assumption of responsibility, adapted to your training status
  • Participation in the supervision and further training of assistant doctors

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Gynecology and Obstetrics or Advanced Training (mid-to-end of last year)
  • Interest in the full spectrum, from oncology and urogynecology to obstetrics, and pleasure in surgical gynecology
  • Flowing knowledge of German (level C1)
  • Foreign degree? In the case of MEBEKO recognition and vocational training permits, we provide you with advice and financial support.

Their advantages

  • Attractive salary: 13 monthly salaries, benefit bonus and annual salary increase along clear experience levels
  • Recognized further education institution, also for the focus on operative gynecology and obstetrics: ideal for your next career step
  • Five paid training days per year plus a contribution to your training costs
  • Family-friendly: hospital-owned day-care, childcare allowance for children up to 10 years and 16 weeks of maternity leave on full salary
  • Clear employment conditions thanks to collective employment contract and annual working time model
  • Stable team with short distances and very good mood
  • Personal support by Medimeo for orientation, selection and change to Switzerland
