Full description

The Office of Informatics and Organization AIO is the central ICT service provider of the Canton of Solothurn. We are developing our Service Desk and support team in a targeted manner - with a focus on digital customer contact, intelligent self-service offers and the gradual use of automation and AI. We are looking for a team leader/in service desk and support for the Technical Services department on 01.09.2026 or by agreement at the Solothurn site, 80-100%.

Your Responsibility

  • As a team leader, you are responsible for the Service Desk team and support with a clear division of tasks and a sense of priorities and you promote their further development.
  • You can reliably ensure the workplace support and the entire lifecycle of workplace systems for employees of the cantonal administration with your team on site.
  • You design a timely, cross-channel service model
  • from telephone and email to chat and self-service portal
  • and ensure clear processes and a well-maintained knowledge database.
  • You can continuously develop the service offered by Service Desk and Support - close to the clientele's needs and open to future expansions of the services. - You can identify where automation brings real added value, and you can also implement it (e.g. chatbots and AI-supported assistance systems for standard questions), evaluate new tools with a clear focus on efficiency and user-friendliness.

Your profile

  • You have a completed ICT training, ideally with advanced training at the level of HF or FH
  • or comparable professional experience.
  • Perennial practice and leadership experience in ICT support or service management are of benefit.
  • Real interest in new technologies you are distinguished: you have already dealt with AI, chatbots and automation.
  • You have knowledge of ITSM processes
  • ITIL certification is a plus, but not a must.
  • You think structured, act pragmatically and do not lose sight of the user perspective.

We offer

  • promotion of personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • part-time work, also in the cadre
  • possibility of mobile work
  • progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • equal pay between men and women
  • loyalty premiums
  • 16 weeks maternity leave with full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • financial contributions to childcare
  • comprehensive offer of internal and external training
