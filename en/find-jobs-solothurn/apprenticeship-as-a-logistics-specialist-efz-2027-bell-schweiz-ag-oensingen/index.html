Tasks

  • Logistics professionals work in warehouses of industrial, commercial and production plants. They are used wherever goods of any kind are transported, stored, distributed and managed – be they consumer and production goods, letters, parcels or baggage.
  • You receive delivered goods, check and book them in the merchandise management system. With the help of forklifts, drawbars or transverse seat vehicles, you store the goods in a suitable manner. You prepare ordered goods for delivery. You pack and label them, create the delivery documents and then load the vehicles professionally. Your tasks also include regularly checking stockpiles so that there are no excess stockpiles or shortages.
  • Because goods are always moved, you have a good chance on the job market after your successful apprenticeship.
