Tasks

  • You actively shape the strategy as well as the process and technology orientation for OT and IoT at Coop
  • You carry out risk analyses independently and develop safe, practicable and sustainable solutions from them
  • You advise specialist areas on site projects and evaluate supplier solutions based on defined specifications
  • You bring your architecture and security expertise to digitalization projects and develop sustainable, scalable solutions
  • You create and standardize guidelines, standards and instructions for action and strengthen together with IT and specialist areas Security, IT/OT Convergence and Digitalization

Requirements

  • You have a completed degree in computer science, engineering or a comparable education
  • You bring in-depth knowledge of computer science, system architecture, networks and technical solution development
  • You have first practical experience in OT-Security and know the requirements of modern, secure system landscapes
  • You move safely in the interaction of engineering, architecture, operation and security and work structured, self-responsible and with entrepreneurial thinking
  • Ideally, you also have experience in OT, IoT or automation environments as well as know-how in topics such as IEC 62443, edge architectures, device management, monitoring, lifecycle management and complex enterprise networks.
