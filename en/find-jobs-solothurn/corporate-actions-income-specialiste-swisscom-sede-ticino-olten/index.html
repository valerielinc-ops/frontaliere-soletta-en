# Corporate Actions & Income Specialist

Il tuo futuro inizia

Do you want to work close to the pulse of the financial markets? Acquired as Corporate Actions & Income Specialist You play a central role in handling dividends, capital increases, mergers and other securities-related events. You coordinate different interfaces, ensure smooth processes and contribute significantly to customer satisfaction and service quality – a job with responsibility, variety and proximity to financial market events. You bring Your knowledge in the area of corporate actions is targeted and ensures that our customer banks are completely satisfied with the securities processing.

  • Termination, preparation and processing of all corporate actions for our customer banks
  • Identification, removal and monitoring of instructions to international depositories
  • Responsibility for smooth processes in complex and versatile capital measures
  • Varied activities with a lot of personal responsibility and development potential in the exciting environment of the financial markets

Your skills

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship with specialist training
  • 3–5 years of professional experience in banking, ideally in the BPO environment
  • Very good knowledge of banking processes (payment transactions or securities) as well as common banking applications
  • Analytical and holistic thinking, communication strength, problem-solving competence and high professionalism
  • In-depth know-how in the field of corporate actions & income
  • Structured, independent working method and team spirit
  • High Process and Quality Orientation (Outsourcing Partner)
  • Very good German and good French / English

In our varied day-to-day business, we work together with colleagues from other teams across departments and in partnership – always with the aim of achieving the best solutions together: for our customer banks, their customers and for ourselves. Collegial, open interaction is a matter of course for us – both in our daily cooperation and in personal exchange with each other. With us you have the opportunity to work in the office in Olten or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
