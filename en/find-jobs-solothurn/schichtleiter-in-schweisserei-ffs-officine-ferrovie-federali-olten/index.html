Shift manager: in welding 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Shift conductor: in welding Olten, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

A varied and exciting day-to-day work awaits you and together with a motivated team you make an important contribution to public transport. We support further development requests and have a variety of internal further education opportunities for your personal career at SBB. Attractive working conditions are important to us, for example a free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad – also for the family. On our website you will find more exciting information about our benefits. You can move that.

  • As a shift manager: in welding, you ensure our welding technology manufacturer quality by professionally and standardized execution of welding and metal work on our modern rail vehicles and components. This ensures that our customers travel with the highest possible comfort.
  • As part of your welding work, you read drawings and comply with the welding instructions. You also have the technical responsibility in your shift about the work of your colleagues:innen.
  • In addition, you apply the various methods of non-destructive testing and take the required welding tests at the given interval.
  • If necessary, you support your colleagues in the upstream and downstream departments.
  • With your initiative and independent personality, you will find innovative solutions for new resources.

You bring that with you.

  • You are able to perform quality welds on steel materials in the processes MAG (135). Ideally, you already bring welding qualifications according to ISO 9606-1 for throat or stump seams in the FM1 area or are ready to achieve them. Knowledge of the standard 15085 as well as and welding plan level C are advantageous.
  • Further welding knowledge in the field of MIG welding (131) & TIG welding (141) of steel or aluminum material & chromium steel is a plus. The additional qualifications in the ZfP test procedure visual inspection (VT) according to EN ISO 9712 is also an advantage.
  • A completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ as plant and apparatus manufacturer:in, metal manufacturer:in or vehicle maker:in form the basic basis of your new activity. In addition, you bring at least 3 years of professional experience in a similar function and have necessarily completed the further education IWS Schwelsfachmann:frau. Leadership experience is desirable.
  • Since many instructions and documentation are electronic, you are not afraid of IT programs and tools. The reading and understanding of drawings are desired & required. In addition, you can easily communicate in German and understand challenging dialogues (B2-C1). Every other language is a plus.
  • You have the willingness to do 2-shift work in order to take the sweat supervision on a late shift and to support the employees of the shift and to take it further. Due to our safety precautions, it is important that you have good health with full hearing and vision.
