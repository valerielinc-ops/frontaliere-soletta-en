Future employer Since 2009, yellowshark AG has been committed to matching qualified specialists with suitable companies according to their wishes. Our client has been on the market for over 50 years, offers multifunctional event infrastructures, and is looking for a motivated Product Owner ERP / Project Manager to start immediately or by agreement.

Your responsibilities

  • Taking on project and sub-project management (traditional, hybrid, agile) for ICT and business projects
  • Driving the harmonization of digital ERP business processes with the sister companies and Solution Management
  • Definition of new requirements / User Stories relating to the central ERP components
  • Refining and prioritizing the requirements / User Stories and the project / product definition
  • Coordination point between the client, other Product Owners / Application Managers, ICT Operations, as well as internal and external partners and developers
  • Release management, 3rd-level support, and testing of all central ERP components

  • Bistro or cafeteria
  • Bonus model
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • Modern workplace

Your profile

  • Higher education in computer science (e.g. business informatics FA / HF / FH) preferred
  • You have experience in implementing and maintaining ERP systems and digitizing business processes
  • Several years of extensive practical experience in project management, ideally using agile and traditional methodologies; current certifications such as PMP, SCRUM, or Black Belt are an advantage
  • Good understanding of systems and architecture and experience in interface design, experience in migration
  • Very good spoken and written German and English skills
