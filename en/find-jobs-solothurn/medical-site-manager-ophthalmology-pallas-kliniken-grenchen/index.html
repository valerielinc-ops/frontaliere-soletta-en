Full description

With more than 350 employees at 17 locations, Pallas Kliniken is one of the leading providers of ophthalmology and aesthetic medicine in Switzerland. We stand for the highest medical quality, personal care and a strong cooperation. Our dedicated specialist staff plays a central role in the individual care of our patients. Through close interdisciplinary cooperation, we guarantee holistic support at the highest level – characterized by expertise, empathy and innovation.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a motivated, independent and numerical personality as Medical/r site manager/in ophthalmology 80-100 %

Responsibility

  • · Medical, professional and organisational management of the site in the field of ophthalmology.
  • · Ensure high-quality, evidence-based and patient-focused ophthalmological care.
  • · Professional and disciplinary guidance of doctors and professional guidance of medical specialists.
  • · Further development and implementation of modern diagnostic and therapeutic procedures as well as continuous optimization of medical processes and processes.
  • · Ensure and continuously develop medical quality and treatment standards.

Requirements

  • · Recognized specialist title for ophthalmology.
  • · Several years of clinical professional experience in the field of ophthalmology.
  • · Leadership experience is beneficial.
  • · Good business knowledge.
  • · In-depth knowledge of modern diagnostic and therapeutic procedures as well as medical quality standards.
  • · Good IT user knowledge as well as experience with electronic medical histories and practice information systems.
  • · Very good knowledge of German (at least level C1); Further language skills are advantageous.

Benefits

You benefit for your work from our modern infrastructure, in which you can treat your patients professionally. We support you in your work optimally and in the best possible way. In addition to our modern infrastructure, we also offer you internal and external training opportunities. Excellent earning opportunities are a matter of course for us.

Are you looking for a versatile and exciting task in a family group of companies? We look forward to your application.
