Would you like to further develop your career in Switzerland and take on more responsibility? In a certified breast center, you bring your experience in senology, remain active in risk obstetrics and from 2027 onwards you will be involved in a brand new hospital building. A job with a long-term perspective. The women’s clinic of a large center hospital in German-speaking Switzerland combines modern obstetrics, surgical gynecology and specialized breast medicine under one roof. Every year, her certified breast center looks after around 120 patients with newly diagnosed breast cancer and accompanies them throughout the entire course of treatment. At the beginning of 2027, the hospital moves into a modern new building that combines the clinics under one roof and creates the best conditions for daily work with short distances and optimized processes. Regular tumor boards and coordinated treatment concepts characterize cooperation. Specialised Breast & Cancer Care Nurses continuously accompany patients and take on important coordinative tasks. In obstetrics, the clinic takes care of high-risk pregnancies and premature births, with a children's hospital including neonatology at the same location. The hospital is only a ten-minute walk from the train station, Basel, Bern and Zurich can be reached by car in less than an hour. So the place can be connected just as well with a place of residence in one of the big cities as with a quieter life in the green.

Their tasks

  • Collaboration in the interdisciplinary breast center with surgical and conservative care of patients with benign and malignant breast diseases
  • Breast surgery and participation in tumor boards and senological treatment pathways
  • Care of gynecological and obstetric patients, including high-risk pregnancies
  • Participation in the senior medical service of the Frauenklinik
  • Participation in the further training of assistant doctors

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Gynecology and Obstetrics
  • Sound experience in senology and breast surgery, mandatory prerequisite for this position
  • Experience in risk obstetrics, ideally in recent years at a perinatal center Level 1 or a comparable center of maximum care
  • Flowing knowledge of German (level C1) and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Specialist title from Germany or another EU country? In the case of MEBEKO recognition and vocational training permits, we provide you with advice and financial support.

Their advantages

  • A brand new hospital building from 2027 with short distances and state-of-the-art infrastructure as well as a certified breast center with around 120 primary carcinomas per year
  • A broad spectrum of senology, gynecology and risk obstetrics, with neonatology in the home
  • A salary that is transparently based on professional experience, training and function, based on a collective employment contract
  • Pension provision via a pension foundation especially for assistant and senior physicians, in addition to the state AHV
  • Financial and time support for your further education and a free internal training program
  • Family-friendly: hospital-owned nursery for children from 0 to 12 years, 16 weeks of maternity leave with full salary and support for part-time models
  • Personal support by Medimeo for relocation and MEBEKO recognition
