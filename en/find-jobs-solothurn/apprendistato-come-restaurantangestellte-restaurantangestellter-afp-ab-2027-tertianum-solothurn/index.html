About the apprenticeship

Get your EBA through training in the catering industry. With us you will start a varied and meaningful apprenticeship with a future, because as a restaurant employee you can expect a supportive working environment from day one. During your training you will be accompanied and supported by our experienced vocational trainers.

We are looking for you for our Sphinxmatte residence in Solothurn from August 1, 2027

Apprenticeship as a restaurant employee / restaurant employee EBA

You'll learn that with us

  • Looking after and entertaining our guests with a professional service
  • Presentation of the food and drink offerings
  • Conduct sales discussions
  • Impeccable appearance as a hostess

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed school education
  • Great willingness to learn, sense of responsibility as well as service and hygiene awareness
  • Helpful, outgoing and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • Well-groomed appearance and good manners
  • Willingness to work irregular hours (we are open every day)
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy diverse challenges and practical work
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German

What benefits do we offer you?

You are just as important to us as your training is to you! That's why we offer you the following advantages:

  • You get your birthday off
  • Free SBB half-fare subscription
  • Each lunch for CHF 5
  • Discounted mobile phone subscriptions with Sunrise
  • Discounts and offers relating to fitness, leisure and your wellbeing
  • Up to 10% discount on your supplementary health insurance
  • And much more...

You start straight away with an exciting introductory day and halfway through your training we invite you to our summer camp for a whole week. Of course, your training completion must be celebrated properly, so we organize an unforgettable graduation party for all graduates. And maybe this is just the beginning of your career with us! We offer numerous opportunities to work within our company immediately after completing your apprenticeship.

You can find more benefits and insights on our website.
