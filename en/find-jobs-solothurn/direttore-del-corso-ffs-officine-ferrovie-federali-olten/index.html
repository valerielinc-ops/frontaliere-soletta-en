Course leader | login vocational training - - - - - - - - Your position in the world of mobility As an educational partner of the SBB, BLS, RhB, the Public Transport Association (VöV) and over 80 other mobility and mobility-related companies, login Berufsbildung AG organizes market-oriented vocational apprenticeships, internships and further training for around 2,100 trainees in over 35 different professions. We are looking for someone for the organizational unit training in the planning area of login Berufsbildung AG in Olten as of January 1, 2027 or by arrangement Course leader: in 50%

This is what you can move

  • Hierarchical and technical leadership of course leaders for inter-company courses (üK) as well as the chief experts for the qualification procedures (QV) for the professions public transport specialists, public transport retail specialists, rail transport specialists, network electricians and track builders
  • Budgeting of the responsible ÜKs, as well as accounting for subsidies
  • Ensuring the planning specifications are provided to the planning team for the course organization
  • Carrying out visitations in üKs and QVs to ensure the methodological/didactic quality of education
  • Carrying out qualifications for course instructors together with those developing teaching materials
  • Responsibility for the planning and implementation of the QV in the public transport sector competence center, in collaboration with the chief experts
  • Ensuring the flow of information to industry management, product management and HR (industry topics, developments in professions, requirements planning, deployment planning)

This is what you bring with you

  • EFZ professional qualification as well as further training at HF/FH or federal level. Specialist certificate
  • Methodological-didactic further training (certificate level trainer, adult educator HF, at least SVEB 1)
  • At least 5 years of professional experience, ideally in the educational sector, e.g. in learning support, course management, ideally in adult education and/or leadership experience
  • Communicative personality with persuasiveness, assertiveness and moderation experience
  • Very reliable way of working, organizational talent and experienced MS Office knowledge
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of French (level B2)

What you can look forward to

A versatile and demanding job in a dynamic environment with scope for creativity. Attractive employment conditions such as hybrid working, a free GA and support for further training round off our offer. This function is in wage band D.
