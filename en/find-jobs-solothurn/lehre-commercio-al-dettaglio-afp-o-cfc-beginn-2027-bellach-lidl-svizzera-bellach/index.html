Full description

Already have ideas for the time after finishing school? Start your future with us and make use of the development opportunities at Lidl Schweiz. The stores are at the center of everything we do. As a retail apprentice (specializing in creating shopping experiences), whether EBA or EFZ, you contribute to the success of your Lidl store. Start/end EBA: August 1, 2027 - July 31, 2029 Start/end EFZ: August 1, 2027 - July 31, 2030 Your tasks

  • Operating cash registers and preparing cash reports
  • Ensuring freshness, quality and cleanliness
  • Stocking goods and presenting merchandise
  • Checking incoming goods in the warehouse and supporting your team with inventory counts
  • Supporting the planning and implementation of promotions and seasonal displays
  • Determining merchandise requirements and ordering goods in the 3rd year of training (EFZ)
  • Taking on initial shift management responsibilities in the 3rd year of training (EFZ)

Your profile

  • An average to very good upper secondary school leaving certificate
  • A1 in a foreign language for EBA and at least A2 for EFZ
  • Enjoyment of interacting with people
  • Independent working style and a quick grasp of things
  • Teamwork skills, reliability and a sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to learn and commitment
  • Physical stamina
  • Very good command of German
