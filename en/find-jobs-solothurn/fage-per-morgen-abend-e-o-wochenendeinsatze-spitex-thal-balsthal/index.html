Full description

We support people in independent living and contribute to their quality of life. Spitex Thal is a non-profit organization and provides professional and comprehensive care and care at home on behalf of the 8 resident communities in the Thal. Our 100 employees provide high-quality services every day with commitment and skill. The future in healthcare is “ambulatory” and thus in Spitex! We are looking for you as FaGe for morning, evening and/or weekend assignments You bring with you

  • Completed vocational training as a specialist / health professional EFZ
  • Openness towards new
  • You are practical, responsible, communicative and flexible
  • Joy about independent work as well as in the team
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Your age doesn't matter

Then you are right with us! Our offer

  • A meaningful and independent activity with a lot of scope for action and design
  • An employee- and family-friendly company in the organization of working hours
  • A strong and diverse team at the back
  • An appreciative leadership culture with a flat hierarchy
  • A field of work in the most beautiful rural environment
  • A careful introduction as well as practical training opportunities
  • A learning and future-oriented Spitex organization with a performance mandate
