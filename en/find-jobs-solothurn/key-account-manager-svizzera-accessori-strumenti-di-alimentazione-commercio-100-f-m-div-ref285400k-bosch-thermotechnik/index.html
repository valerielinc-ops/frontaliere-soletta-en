Your wich

To strengthen our sales team in Switzerland, we are looking for an experienced and entrepreneurial personality as a key account manager for power tool accessories in specialist retail. You are a sales pro with proven successes, who loves not only to recognize potential but also to realize it quickly with strategic skill and power? Then we offer you the opportunity to further develop our most important trading partners and actively shape growth.

Your main tasks

  • Customer development & support: You develop and strengthen sustainable business relationships with defined key customers by regular visits to their central offices and the external locations. The strategic acquisition and the establishment of selected new customers complete your area of responsibility.
  • Overall responsibility: You bear the full responsibility for sales, costs and margins of the entire accessory and hand tool portfolio with your customers and actively control the product life cycle.
  • Negotiation management: You conduct annual discussions and negotiations at decision-making level.

The design and maintenance of the corresponding contracts is also part of your area of responsibility. - Market analysis & strategy: Through precise market and customer analyses you identify the relevant growth segments. You balance them with customer needs in order to derive and implement strategies that are appropriate to potential. - Customer activities & POS: You plan and implement customer-specific activities, from product range design to advertising appearances, trainings to effective point-of-sale measures and marketing campaigns.

  • Product launches: You ensure the successful introduction of new products and campaigns and position our innovative accessory ranger, the riv in the market. - Interface Management: You act as a central interface and ensure a close, coordinated collaboration with the Power Tools Sales Team, the End User Team, local marketing and the global business unit.
  • Project responsibility: You take over the management for further customer projects in areas such as electronic process connection (EDI), logistics optimization, master data management or Category Management.

Your profile

  • Training: You have a solid basic commercial or technical training which you have supplemented with a higher level of further education (e.g. HF/FH in business administration, sales management).A university degree is a plus, but not a requirement. - Experience & Know-How: You have 5-10 years experience in sales outside service (as a key account or area sales manager), ideally in an industry-related environment. The experienced handling of common IT applications, especially MS Excel and PowerPoint, is a matter of course for you.
  • Personality and working methods: You are a convincing, winning personality with entrepreneurial and proactive action. You can identify growth opportunities, build strong relationships and take responsibility for results from the first day in order to translate impulses into measurable successes. - Languages: Swiss German or German you control in the first half of the year.
