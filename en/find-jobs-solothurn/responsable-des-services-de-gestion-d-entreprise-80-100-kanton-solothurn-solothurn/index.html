Full description

The official clerks of the Canton of Solothurn are the regional contact points for a wide range of public services: they combine the functions of a notary, the land register, inheritance, operation and bankruptcy office as well as the commercial register office and are thus available to private individuals, companies and authorities as competent contacts. For the business services department, we are looking for a manager of business services, 80-100%, at the Solothurn location as of 01.04.2027 or by agreement.

Your responsibility

  • In this role, with the support of a small team, you will take over the management of the finance and accounting of the office clerks.
  • They prepare the various documents for planning, reporting and controlling in accordance with the principles of impact-oriented management.
  • For the office clerks, you operate the management information system and support the departments in business issues.
  • You work in computer science and organizational projects.

Your profile

  • You are a responsible and entrepreneurial personality who has a higher specialist training in accounting / controlling (expert in finance and accounting with Federal FA or equivalent training).
  • You have project management experience as well as good computer science skills and are ready to work in or lead different projects.
  • Ideally, you already have leadership experience.
  • With your strong social skills, you can quickly gain the trust of your employees.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
