SBB infrastructure builds, operates and maintains the busiest railway network in Europe. With your commitment, you bring the energy safely to the trains and make a direct contribution to punctual and reliable rail traffic. You work closely with the construction management and construction management team in the Mitte region and assume responsibility from planning to acceptance. You are on the road on the construction site and in the office and use modern working methods where the activity allows. Development and further learning are encouraged and lived in everyday life.   • You plan, organize, control and lead traction current construction sites with internal construction teams and thus contribute to a secure energy supply for rail transport.
  • You monitor the execution work and ensure compliance with quality, costs and deadlines.
  • You define personnel and vehicle resources and ensure timely orders in your specialist area.
  • During the acceptance of the created facilities, you participate and accompany the documentation.
  • To a limited extent, you take on construction management tasks in projects with external partners.

You bring that with you.

  • Structured and efficient working methods as well as strong organisational talent, even if several construction sites run in parallel.
  • With an independent and solution-oriented approach, you make well-founded decisions and keep track even under time pressure.
  • Practical experience in the construction or work management of traction current construction sites.
  • Several years of professional experience in catenary construction is an advantage.
  • Completed technical vocational training, ideally complemented by further training as chief fitter:in, construction site manager:in, construction manager:in or a comparable qualification, or willingness to continue training.
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing, knowledge of another national language is a plus.
