Bus whisperer as service technician (m/f/d |80% - 100%) HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 500 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company. To complement our global life cycle management team in the Bus & Trolley division, we are looking for a qualified and dynamic bus whisperer as a service technician. You do not want to simply repair – but understand, improve and move? Then become our next bus whisperer! Their tasks

  • Independent processing and handling of customer inquiries
  • Troubleshooting and diagnosis of traction components and other systems of electric and trolley buses at the customer
  • Carrying out repair work on the entire vehicle
  • Accompanying supplier visits to the customer
  • Diagnosis and repair of traction batteries
  • Maintenance of modifications/modifications from the aftersales
  • Internal communication for the continuous improvement process Our requirements
  • Basic technical training as automotive mechatronics technician Commercial vehicles, agricultural or construction machinery mechanic or similar basic training
  • Vehicle knowledge on buses/commercial vehicles from Vortel
  • Professional experience of several years
  • Flexibility and independent work is expected and offered
  • Willingness to travel at home and abroad
  • Language skills in French and English are beneficial
  • Advanced PC knowledge We offer them
  • Exciting and varied activity with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Flexible annual working hours
  • Opportunity to work part-time
  • Working in a collegial team with very good cohesion
  • Modern electric vehicle as a business car If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including letters of motivation via link (Apply now).
