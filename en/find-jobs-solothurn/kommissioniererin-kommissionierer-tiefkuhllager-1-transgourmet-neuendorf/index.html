Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services.

Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You pick customer orders on time, error-free and top-quality – in the area of frozen products at approximately -20°C
  • You keep setup orders and load the transport containers correctly.
  • You assign orders to the right tour and place them in the appropriate delivery space.
  • You trigger supplies when articles run out.
  • You support with inventories and dispose of waste properly.
  • You work system-managed and comply with all order picking, QS and ASIB guidelines.

Requirements

  • You have experience in goods receipt and picking.
  • You have a valid SUVA-approved truck ID.
  • You work goal-oriented, structured and with high initiative.
  • You are physically resilient and made for cold rooms.
  • You are familiar with warehouse management systems.
  • You speak good German and are reliable.
