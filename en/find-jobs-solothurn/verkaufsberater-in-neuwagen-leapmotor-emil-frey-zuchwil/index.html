Full description

What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey AG Zuchwil represents the model palettes of Toyota, Ford and Leapmotor. The company also includes the brand workshop, a carrosserie department and a spare parts warehouse. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a sales consultant for the Leapmotor brand immediately or by agreement. Their tasks

  • Competent advice and support of our customers when purchasing vehicles
  • Sale of new vehicles of the Leapmotor brand, including additional services (financing, leasing, insurance)
  • Building and maintaining long-term customer relationships
  • Active acquisition of new customers (showroom, telephone, online inquiries)
  • Preparation of offers and handling of sales contracts
  • Organisation and conduct of test runs
  • Maintenance and presentation of the vehicle stock in the showroom
  • Participation in brand-specific training and courses Your profile
