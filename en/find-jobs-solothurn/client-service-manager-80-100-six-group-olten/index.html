Client Service Manager (80 - 100%)

Olten | Up to 40% home office possible | Reference 8210

Would you like to kick-start your career in the international securities business?

Our Client Desk Team – a motivated mix of young and experienced employees with a strong customer focus – is looking forward to a communicative, team-oriented person.

These are your responsibilities

  • Processing and coordinating inquiries from national and international banks in the area of Mandatory Cash Related Corporate Actions (dividends, interest payments and repayments) across all relevant markets
  • Clarification of entitlement issues as well as booking and payment discrepancies
  • Clarifications with banks, Custodians, CSDs, Paying Agents and other market participants to ensure correct and timely processing
  • Research using internal and external systems, data sources and market materials
  • Collaboration with internal departments
  • Handling customer escalations and contributing to the optimization of processes and service quality

This is what you bring

  • Commercial training, banking apprenticeship or comparable qualification
  • Ideally, experience in Asset Servicing, Custody, Corporate Actions or Securities Operations
  • Excellent communication skills, both spoken and written, in German and English (min. B2)
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working with analytical thinking
  • Strong customer and service orientation as well as a professional demeanour
