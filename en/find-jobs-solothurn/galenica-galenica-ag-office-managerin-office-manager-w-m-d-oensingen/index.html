Full description

Location: Medifilm AG, Ostringstrasse 10, 4702 Oensingen

Medifilm is growing and with us our structures and tasks are also growing. This is why we are creating a new position as Office Manager. As a central organizational hub, you support the CEO and management team, ensure smooth processes and bring structure to a wide range of administrative and organizational topics. You are actively involved in shaping the cooperation at Medifilm and creating freedom for our managers.

You can do that.

  • You support the CEO and leadership team in the planning and organization of meetings, workshops, exams, management updates and other management vessels.
  • You coordinate dates and documents, document resolutions and responsibilities and keep an eye on open pending and deadlines.
  • You ensure a professional office day and organize visits, events, meeting rooms and other administrative processes.
  • You support internal communication and ensure structured filing and maintenance of organizational, management and contract documents.
  • You coordinate administrative topics around entry and exit, onboarding, recruitment dates, jobs, accesses and internal organizational information.
  • You act as a central interface to internal and external contacts and bring structure, transparency and commitment into administrative processes.

You bring that with you

  • You have a commercial apprenticeship EFZ or a comparable apprenticeship and bring experience as an office manager, executive assistant or executive management assistant.
  • You’re very adept at using Microsoft 365, especially Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and you’re confident in digital work environments.
  • You like to organize, think ahead and can independently structure, coordinate and prioritize several parallel topics.
  • You work reliably, accurately and discreetly and confidently with confidential information.
  • You convince with a friendly and professional appearance, pronounced service orientation as well as communication strength and sensitivity.
  • You have very good German and good English skills
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Expand competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-defining healthcare provider in our country. The Galenica network offers a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
