Full description

Location: Medifilm AG, Ostringstrasse 10, 4702 Oensingen

Do you want to actively advance projects and shape the future of pharmaceutical production? At Medifilm AG in Oensingen, you support the optimization of processes, accompany technical and organizational changes and gradually take on more responsibility in exciting operations projects. You work closely with different specialist areas and can experience the impact of your work directly in everyday life.

You can do that.

  • You plan and realize smaller projects as well as sub-projects in the area of operations and support their successful implementation.
  • You will accompany the introduction of new plants, technologies and workflows and contribute to efficient processes.
  • You will analyze existing production processes and develop sustainable improvement proposals together with the specialist areas.
  • You coordinate project tasks between production, de-listing, technology, quality management and external partners.
  • You create and maintain project plans, schedules and action lists and ensure transparency about the project progress.
  • You support with project documentation, change control procedures and the preparation of key figures and decision bases for the head of operations.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed technical, pharmaceutical or commercial training and ideally advanced training in project or process management.
  • You have already gained initial experience in project work or in a production environment.
  • You are interested in technical processes and the continuous improvement of processes.
  • You work in a structured, reliable and independent manner and also keep track of several topics.
  • You appreciate cooperation with different specialist areas and communicate appropriately.
  • You master MS Office, especially Excel, and have good knowledge of German and English for cooperation with international partners; GMP knowledge is an advantage.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Extend competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-shaping healthcare provider in our country. The Galenica network offers a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
