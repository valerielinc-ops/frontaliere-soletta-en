Introduction

With a modern branch network, Lidl Switzerland has an increasingly dense presence in the Swiss market. Expansion in Switzerland has been continued since the market entry in 2009. At Lidl Switzerland, exciting tasks and versatile career opportunities await you in the dynamic environment of the retail trade. You will work in the new goods distribution centre in Derendingen. Your tasks

  • You work in the non-food sector
  • Transfer of the goods to the associated storage places
  • Proper and timely picking based on branch orders
  • Transport securing of the picked goods
  • Control and storage of incoming returns from branches
  • Support for stock inventories
  • Co-responsibility in ensuring cleanliness in the camp

Your profile

  • First experience in the logistics industry
  • Reliable, independent and punctual personality
  • High operational readiness and resilience
  • Team, cooperation and communication skills
  • Readiness for shift work
  • Forklift certificate of advantage
  • Good knowledge of German, French and/or English
