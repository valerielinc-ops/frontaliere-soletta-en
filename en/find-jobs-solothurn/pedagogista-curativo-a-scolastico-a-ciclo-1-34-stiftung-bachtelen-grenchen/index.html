Full description

Bachtelen is involved in the canton of Solothurn with a wide range of offers for the promotion, support and integration of young people. This includes special schools, various housing offers, individually coordinated therapy offers as well as consultations and day care centers. Bachtelen employs around 550 people. The school and vocational training sector offers resource-oriented teaching with individualised support. Our students: inside bring a special educational need. Individual education, personal development and preparation for the profession are the focus. In the focus on basic cognition we are looking for a school at Breitengasse 11 in Grenchen as of 01.02.2027:n School:n curative education:in (cycle 1) 34 % 10 lessons, working days Monday morning and Friday

Your work includes

  • In cooperation with the class teacher, you lead a basic level class and create a playful learning day in which children can discover, try out and develop at their pace.
  • You plan and check the individual support and record small and large development steps in school reports.
  • You build a trusting relationship with the parents and shape the promotion together with the interdisciplinary team.
  • You coordinate your class team and accompany the employees with clear guidance and an open ear.
  • You bring your ideas to school and class projects and develop our school together with the team.

This is what you bring with you

  • A master’s degree in curative curative education or at least. an EDK-approved teaching diploma at the appropriate level.
  • A sense for younger children with special educational needs and joy in taking up their curiosity and accompanying their development.
  • A friendly way, clear communication and enjoyment of working with parents and professionals.
  • Patience, responsibility and the flexibility to engage with different needs and situations.
  • Curiosity and desire to reflect on your work, to educate yourself and to contribute your own ideas.

This is what we offer you

  • An attractive workplace with plenty of room for initiative and participation
  • Flat hierarchies, short decision paths and an open, appreciative cooperation
  • Contemporary employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • A meaningful work environment where your work makes a real difference
  • Generous support (money and/or time) for further education
  • Various employee events
  • Daily two paid short breaks – including free coffee

You have questions? Seraina Zaugg can provide you with information on 032 531 08 88 or seraina.zaugg@bachtelen.ch. We look forward to your application!
