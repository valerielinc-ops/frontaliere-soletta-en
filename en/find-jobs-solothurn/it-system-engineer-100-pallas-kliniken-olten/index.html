Full description

The Pallas Clinics specialize in the fields of ophthalmology, dermatology and aesthetic medicine. With more than 350 employees at 17 locations, the clinic group is one of the leading and independent private clinics in German-speaking Switzerland. Excellent medicine and human treatment is the guiding principle. The headquarters in Olten, with around 50 employees, takes over the operational management.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed person as

IT System Engineer 100%

Responsibility

  • System Management: Establishment, integration and operation of new systems and interfaces
  • Server management: Management and further development of our server landscape (physical and virtual) including archiving and IAM processes
  • Workplace Management: Setup and maintenance of workstations, both physical and virtual
  • Monitoring: System monitoring of the entire IT infrastructure (PRTG)
  • Virtual Desktop Infrastructure: Managing and Updating the VDI Environment
  • Operating system automation: further development and support of automated processes for patch management as well as the maintenance and updating of operating systems.
  • Application Management: Implementation and integration of software solutions into existing and new infrastructures (On-Premise & Cloud)
  • Network Management: Configuring and optimizing network solutions at all locations and building new networks
  • Cloud management: administration of cloud services and resources, migration of services to the cloud and optimization of cloud infrastructure
  • Backup management: management and assurance of regular backups and verification of recoverability as part of emergency strategies

Requirements

  • A completed degree in computer science or a comparable qualification is advantageous, but not mandatory. However, training in computer science is absolutely necessary
  • At least 3-5 years of work experience in the management of ICT systems, including network, server and application management including experience in the management of virtual workplaces and operating systems
  • Competences in scripting and programming, as well as the ability to analyze complex systems and make data-driven decisions
  • Experience with VMware, Hyper-V, HPE/Aruba, Barracuda, Exchange, SCCM, and PRTG desired
  • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work independently and team-oriented
  • Excellent communication skills in German and ideally good knowledge of English
  • Commitment and motivation to address healthcare challenges

Benefits

  • Exciting and varied activities in a diverse team
  • Flexible working hours and home office options to promote a work-life balance
  • Training and career opportunities in the future-oriented health sector
  • A working atmosphere that focuses on team spirit, joy of work and a positive culture of error

Sounds exciting? Then we look forward to your application and to getting to know you personally!
