Full description

The Pallas clinics are specialized in ophthalmology and aesthetics. With over 350 employees at 17 locations, we are one of the leading providers in Switzerland.

Our Service Center is the central point of contact for our patients. The team coordinates appointments, processes telephone and written inquiries and provides competent and reliable support with a high level of service orientation.

In order to strengthen our service center, we are looking for a communicative, service-oriented and reliable personality as soon as possible or by agreement.

Personnel/in Service Center 100%

Responsibility

  • Demanding telephone appointment bookings and appointment postponements according to the internal standards for all our locations
  • Telephone support and advice for our patients
  • Processing of written requests by e-mail
  • Professional and targeted reconnection of external calls
  • Acquisition and mutation of patient data
  • Processing of incoming and outgoing mail
  • Other administrative activities

Requirements

  • Completed basic training in the medical sector (MPA, optometrist, pharmaceutical assistant, dental assistant or similar) or a customer- and service-related basic training such as retail trade
  • Communicative and service-oriented personality
  • Independent, team-capable and resilient personality, which keeps calm even in hectic times
  • Good PC knowledge
  • Very good oral and written knowledge in German. Oral English skills are beneficial
  • Readiness to perform a Saturday service each month in the morning from 8-12 o’clock

Benefits

  • A young, dynamic team with an appreciative and uncomplicated cooperation
  • Regulated working hours that enable good planning of your everyday life
  • Attractive employee discounts on our services in the areas of ophthalmology and aesthetics
  • A modern workplace in a central location directly at Olten station with optimal public transport connections
  • A varied area of responsibility with a lot of contact with our patients
