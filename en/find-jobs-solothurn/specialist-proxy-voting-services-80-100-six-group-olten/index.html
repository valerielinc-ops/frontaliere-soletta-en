Specialist Proxy Voting Services (80-100%)

Olten | Up to 40% home office possible | Reference 8235

Are you looking for an exciting challenge in a dynamic working environment? Do you want to take responsibility for the coordination and handling of worldwide proxy voting?

Then our team Proxy Voting International is happy to hear from you!

These are your tasks

  • Processing of worldwide voting rights orders and GV tickets
  • Monitoring deadlines, exceptions and special cases such as late voting
  • Answering customer inquiries as well as coordination with custodians, providers and internal interfaces
  • Coordination of disclosure requests under regulatory requirements, e.g. SRD II
  • Participation in process improvements and projects influencing the proxy voting service

You bring that with you

  • Commercial education, banking or similar qualifications
  • Ideally, professional experience in proxy voting, corporate actions, securities operations or in the customer service environment
  • Excellent communication skills in word and writing in German and English (min. B2)
  • Structured, reliable workflow with an eye for risks, deadlines and details
  • Enjoying customer contact, teamwork and the further development of processes
