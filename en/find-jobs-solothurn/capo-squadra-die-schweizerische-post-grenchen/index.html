Introduction

Do you love customer contact and have a flair for sales? Are you ready to take your next step and gain initial experience in a leadership position? Then apply now. With you we are close to the people.

You can do that.

You support team management in management tasks in Team Grenchen-Solothurn, you complement and represent yourself fully.

You bring that with you

In addition, you communicate very well orally and in writing in German, every other national language is an advantage.
