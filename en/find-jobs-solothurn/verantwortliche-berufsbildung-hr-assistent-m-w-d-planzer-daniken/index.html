Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

Tasks

  • You take over the overall coordination for our learners in 3 different professions and you recruit and accompany them until the apprenticeship.
  • The responsibility of vocational training is in your hands – you ensure that all processes run smoothly, advise our vocational and practical trainers and you exchange ideas with parents, vocational colleges, ÜK and authorities
  • In addition, you advise and support our employees and managers in HR-specific topics skilfully and with great passion
  • Our HR systems are carefully maintained and ensure that all relevant information is always at hand
  • You keep an eye on the health of employees and also have discussions in cooperation with the managers
  • You are also actively involved in recruiting, from demand planning to successful onboarding
  • You take over the deputy staff management of the branch with sovereignty, a sense of responsibility and an eye for the essentials

Profile

  • We require at least 3 years of experience in vocational training and you have the vocational trainer card – ideally you are familiar with the teaching professions KV, logistics and road transport
  • You have already worked in human resources and preferably have an appropriate training
  • Ideally, you have already gained experience in our industry or a similar dynamic environment
  • You are used to taking responsibility and at the same time taking action
  • Your empathetic, solution-oriented, enduring and responsible nature sets you apart
  • Even if it gets stressful, you stay cool and keep track.

Benefits

  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you start with 5 weeks of holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Do you love cool events? We can celebrate!

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
