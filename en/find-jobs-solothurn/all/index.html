New fiscal cross-border workers pay Swiss withholding at 80% of the ordinary rate and Italian tax with a credit for Swiss tax. How many days of remote work can I do while keeping cross-border status in Canton Solothurn?

For fiscal cross-border workers covered by the Italy–Switzerland protocol, home telework up to 25% follows the dedicated tax rule.
