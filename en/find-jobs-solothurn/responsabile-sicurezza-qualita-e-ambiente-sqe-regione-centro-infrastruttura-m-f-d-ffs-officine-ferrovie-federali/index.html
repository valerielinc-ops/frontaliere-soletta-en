Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. In this leadership role, you play a key role in shaping safety, quality and the environment (SQE), in collaboration with the operational organizations of the Infrastructure Divisions — in a way that is responsive and practical! Together with the senior management teams, projects and specialized SQE managers, you support the implementation of our safety and quality objectives in the Central Region. You evolve in a varied environment, between operational sites, operational topics, conceptual and strategic projects, and guidelines. What you can move.

  • With your team, you provide advice, training and introduction of safety, quality and environmental guidelines, instruments and processes for the infrastructure sectors (maintenance, projects, facility management, operation) in the Central Region.
  • You are responsible for event analysis, analysis and reporting of security indicators, as well as training and review of security operational functions. You also accompany the line of command during security checks and audits.
  • In close collaboration with the prescriptive bodies and the three other regional units of safety, quality and environment Infrastructure, you will develop regional guidelines and support projects as well as concrete issues related to the security of partners in the region.
  • You use your good contacts with the region's infrastructure division management teams to provide proven solutions and identify opportunities and risks early enough.
  • You develop and implement practical tools, whether they be field security campaigns, environmental checklists or Kaizen approaches.
  • You monitor subcontractors and third party yards. You are also contributing to the development of the regional QES unit, in accordance with the principles of enterprise management.

What you bring.

  • Thanks to your excellent communication capacity, your great empathy, your ability to take different points of view and your assurance, you involve different interest groups in a way tailored to their needs.
  • You have first experience in results-based driving and development in a technical environment, or in specialized driving in the areas of security.
  • In your previous duties, you have gained experience in the design and facilitation of training and workshops, as well as in the introduction of standards and advice.
  • You have a very good knowledge of safety standards and standards in the rail sector, in particular the safety of construction sites and workplaces (TEN 20100).
  • You have a university degree or a high school specializing in business or technical management, or equivalent training, and practical experience in managing processes and projects.
  • For this function, an oral and written mastery of German corresponding to the C1 level at least is required. Knowledge of other national languages is an asset.
