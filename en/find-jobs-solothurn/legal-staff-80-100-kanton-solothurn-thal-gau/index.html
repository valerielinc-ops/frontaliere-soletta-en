Full description

The Cantonal Bankruptcy Office in Oensingen performs the tasks of bankruptcy law enforcement in the Canton of Solothurn. For the Cantonal Bankruptcy Office, we are looking for a legal employee, 80-100%, at the Oensingen location.

Your responsibility

  • Independently, you carry out bankruptcy proceedings with a high degree of difficulty on natural and legal persons as well as on rejected inheritances.
  • They assume responsibility for all the tasks of bankruptcy proceedings.
  • Supervisors and employees support you with challenging procedural and legal issues.
  • They make investigations into bankruptcy offences and write criminal complaints to the competent authorities.
  • In your function, you are in close and active contact with customers and authorities.

Your profile

  • They have a completed law degree and good IT knowledge.
  • Ideally, you have already proven your expertise in practice and can demonstrate experience in debt recovery and bankruptcy law.
  • They have a secure appearance, can negotiate skillfully and prevail if necessary.
  • Flexibility, balance and an eye for priorities set you apart in a lively work environment.
  • They like to work independently and enjoy taking on responsibility for a demanding, interesting and extremely versatile area of responsibility.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity for mobile work
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
