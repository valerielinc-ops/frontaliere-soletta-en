Carrosserie painter buses & commercial vehicles (m/f/d)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. We also offer a comprehensive range of repairs and services for vehicles of all kinds (buses, commercial vehicles, passenger cars, campers, etc.). To strengthen our paint centre in the area of services, we are looking for a qualified carrosserie painter.

Do you have an apprenticeship as a carrosserie painter and do you have a good sense of color? As an employee in our dynamic team, you can prove your sense of presentation and impact as well as your strong craftsmanship. In this varied job you paint the most different vehicles and industrial parts - you will never get bored with us! Their tasks

  • Independent processing of paint jobs on buses & commercial vehicles
  • Independent processing of paint jobs for new bus production (small and add-on parts) and industrial painting

Our requirements

  • Training degree as carrosserie painter EFZ or industrial painter EFZ
  • Professional experience
  • High level of readiness
  • Independence, reliability and teamwork

We offer them

  • Interesting, varied and challenging activity
  • Moving working time
  • Conditions of performance
  • Opportunity for further training
