Role overview

Tasks

  • Care of patients at the Centre for Integrated Prevention and Rehabilitation (iPR) in the fields of oncology, pulmonology, metabolism and cardiology
  • Management and implementation of various therapy and exercise services such as strength training (MTT), ergometer, water, outdoor and relaxation therapy
  • Implementation of sports cardiac performance diagnostics (spiroergometry) as well as planning of individual therapy goals with motivational advice for lifestyle change
  • Documentation of treatments, drafting of reports and information and advice for patients on the ward
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation in the medical-therapeutic team for the holistic treatment of patients

Profile

  • Master’s degree in sports sciences (focus on prevention and rehabilitation) or Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in physiotherapy with SRK recognition
  • Experience in the care of patients with oncological and pneumological diseases and ideally further training in sports therapy for cancers
  • Knowledge of performance diagnostics, in particular spiroergometry and ECG interpretation
  • Strong teamwork, enjoyment of interdisciplinary collaboration and the ability to motivate and guide patients
