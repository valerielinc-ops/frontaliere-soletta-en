Automatician:in/Electronician:in for component processing in the field of electrical engineering Olten, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You are part of a small, very experienced and collegial team and make an important contribution to the smooth functioning of the SBB fleet. You get a structured introduction, mostly directly «on-the-job» and thus increase the flexibility of your everyday life. So that you can get to know the different types of vehicles, you will get to know different components and thus the diversity of our fleet. Attractive working conditions are important to us. Our benefits such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • In this exciting activity, you will independently carry out repair and refurbishment work on electronic components such as customer information systems and network technology, which will then be installed in passenger cars.
  • As an Automatic Engineer or Electronic Engineer, you play an important role in ensuring the smooth operation and thus the availability of the electronic components of our fleet.
  • The needs of our customers are constantly changing and you are an important part that implements these needs in the form of modernization and thus significantly contributes to the comfort of our customers.
  • In this role, you are responsible for compliance with safety, quality and execution standards.
  • You report the executed work and ensure the timeliness and quality of the documentation.

You bring that with you.

  • A high sense of responsibility and security as well as a high degree of resilience are among your great strengths.
  • You are a technically affine personality with an independent and flexible way of working. We rely on personalities capable of teamwork, as you do nothing alone at SBB and always work in a team.
  • To keep you up-to-date with the latest technology, you are open to various training and further education (internal/external).
  • You have completed basic electrotechnical training EFZ (automatician: in, electronics technician: in or in a comparable profession).
  • In order to understand orders and instructions correctly, you can communicate in German (verbal and written), knowledge of a second national language (e.g. French) is a plus.

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This post is at requirement level F, regional allowance level 0. More about wages and benefits.

If you still want to tell us your motivation, you still have the opportunity to do so in our application tool. Making big is easier with you. This starts with your application.
