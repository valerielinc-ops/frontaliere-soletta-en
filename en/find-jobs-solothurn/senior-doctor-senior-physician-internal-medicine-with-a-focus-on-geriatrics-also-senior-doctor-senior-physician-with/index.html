How about having time for your patients again in geriatrics – and still making a career? As a senior physician or senior physician, respectively, Senior physician Internal Medicine with destination LA, you lead your own department with permanent assistance in a manageable rehabilitation clinic, set up a consultation hour and usually have work at 5 pm. Her new place of work is an established rehabilitation clinic in the canton of Aargau, which has consciously remained manageable with around 400 employees. It is small enough that you know each other quickly, and large enough for a wide range of geriatric, musculoskeletal and internistic-oncological rehabilitation. X-ray, laboratory, an in-house pharmacy, psychology and nutritional advice are in the house. The clinic is a SIWF-certified training institution and focuses on short decision-making processes instead of hierarchical thinking. Because the stays are planned and last several weeks, there is time for medicine instead of throughput. You really get to know your patients and accompany them back to an independent life. The clinic is close to nature on the Aare, in the middle of a local recreation area, and yet central. From the nearest regional centre, Interregio trains travel half-hourly to Zurich and Basel. Who comes from southern Germany, has a short wire to the old homeland. The job is suitable for everyone who wants to progress professionally without having to live in the big city.

Their tasks

  • They lead a geriatric rehabilitation department with around 40 patients, supported by a permanent assistant doctor. Share the station collegially with an orthopaedic cadre physician
  • Represented as senior physician or senior physician You are the chief physician geriatrician and take over deputies in the geriatrics at the second clinic location
  • You lead interdisciplinary reports with therapy, care, social counseling and psychology
  • After your training, you will build up your own outpatient office hours if you are interested and develop them further
  • You support assistant doctors in further training, participate in medical risk management and take on background services on a regular basis

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Internal Medicine. A focus or an additional designation geriatrics is a big plus and opens the door as a senior doctor or senior physician
  • Experience in geriatrics - in the acute hospital or in rehabilitation
  • German skills at a high level
  • Your first step into Switzerland? On the way there, we support you with advice and financial support in obtaining your permits (MEBEKO)
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and leading young colleagues

Their advantages

  • Annual salary from about CHF 170,000 as a senior physician or from about CHF 180,000 as a senior physician with a focus on geriatrics
  • Share in sales at your own office hours possible
  • Permanently assigned assistant doctor and a colleague at eye level on the same station so that you are not alone when moving to Switzerland
  • Structured familiarisation with the Swiss health system, with social counselling, care, psychology and pharmacy. You will not start the consultation until you have arrived and are interested in it from your side.
  • Schedulable working day, usually from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., with regular background service
  • 27 days of leave (from 60 years of age 32 days) and 10 paid training days per year
  • Strong pension provision: The employer pays 60% of the contributions to the pension fund (company pension provision), and the savings contributions are well above the legal minimum
