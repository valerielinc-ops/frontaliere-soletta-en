Pemsa is a leading Swiss recruitment agency with over 30 years of industry expertise. We specialize in recruiting and providing qualified personnel in building, industry, electricity, plant and engineering. Thanks to our capillary presence and our extensive network, we guarantee close proximity to the customer and tailor-made staff solutions, regardless of the location of the yards or workplaces in Switzerland. Our expertise and professional service are the basis of long-term collaborations with our customers and candidates.

Mansions

  • Customer advice and assistance in the slaughterhouse department, taking into account individual requests and food requirements
  • Art display and attractive presentation of fresh meat, meats and other butcher’s products
  • Sale, portioning and packaging of meat and meat according to Swiss standards of hygiene and quality
  • Guarantee of availability of the goods through careful management of the warehouse and control of deliveries
  • Use of cash systems and scales in strict compliance with business guidelines
  • Collaboration with respect to hygiene standards, safety at work and cleaning at work
  • Support for marketing campaigns and seasonal promotions in the sales area
  • Autonomous organization of daily activities in the sales department, including reorders and inventory
  • Responsibility for the presentation and care of the sales area, as well as for its fleet vehicles for deliveries and supplies

Profile required

  • Basic vocational training completed in the field of slaughterhouses, retail trade or equivalent qualification
  • At least several years of working experience in selling butcher shop in Switzerland with a solid knowledge of Swiss standards of quality and hygiene
  • Excellent German mastery at native level to ensure professional advice and communication with customers
  • Autonomy and reliability in work, as well as a strong customer orientation and talent in sales
  • Driving licence and vehicle to carry out autonomous deliveries of goods and transport tasks
  • Good mastery of cash systems and scales, ideally experience with industry-specific software
  • High resistance to stress and flexibility, particularly with regard to working hours in the retail sector
  • Ability to work on team, well-kept appearance and hygienic behavior
  • Additional benefit: residence at an acceptable distance from the workplace to ensure optimum operational availability
