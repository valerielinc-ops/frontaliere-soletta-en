Full description

Bachtelen is a leading competence centre in the Canton of Solothurn for children and young people. With a broad-based offer in schools, living, therapy, counselling and family-supplemented care, we are committed to the best possible promotion and integration of children and young people. More than 550 dedicated employees jointly shape a culture of openness, expertise and appreciation.

As a business manager, you take on the responsibility to further develop this meaningful task with foresight, empathy and leadership.

Their tasks

  • Responsibility for the operational management of the Competence Center and the implementation of the strategic orientation in cooperation with the Board of Management of the Bachtelen Association
  • Further develop offerings in collaboration with senior management, extended management and their dedicated teams
  • Representation of the institution vis-à-vis authorities, politicians and the public
  • Cultivating a value-oriented, open and innovative organisational culture

Your profile

  • University degree in pedagogy, social pedagogy or psychology
  • Sound management training and several years of management experience
  • Strategic, networked and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Integrative, communicative and empathetic leader

What we offer

  • Appreciative corporate culture and a competent, motivated team
  • Annual working hours and possibility to home office
  • Generous support for individual further education
  • Working conditions that fit your life: flexible, fair, self-responsible
  • Attractive pay and very good social benefits
