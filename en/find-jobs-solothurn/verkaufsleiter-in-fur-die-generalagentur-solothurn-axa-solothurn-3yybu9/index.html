100%, Location: Solothurn

Shape your future – together with us!

As AXA, we see ourselves as a partner who stands by your side and believes in you. That's why we encourage and challenge you and proactively show you possibilities for your future. We encourage you and offer you freedom so that you can connect the things that are important to you. Together with your commitment and your individual contribution, this becomes a forward-looking partnership. For you, for us and for our customers. And not just for the moment, but for the long term.

Start your career in an agency at AXA, the No. 1! We are at our customers' side as a competent partner – in every situation. Are you too?

What to expect

  • Personnel and technical management as well as training of a team of insurance advisors
  • Determination of sales targets in consultation with the general agent
  • Deputy General Agent
  • Planning and implementation of market development measures
  • Support and expansion of our own customer portfolio
  • Needs-oriented 360° customer advice in all insurance matters
  • Establishment and support of an intermediary network

What you bring to the table

  • Joy and talent to motivate and lead a team of insurance advisors
  • Proof of success in the financial services sector or in the insurance industry with a focus on the non-life sector
  • Passing the intermediary examination (VBV) is required
  • Leadership experience desired
  • Good knowledge of written and spoken German, other foreign languages are an advantage
  • Good networking and anchoring in the Solothurn region is essential
  • Independent and goal-oriented way of working as well as a high level of commitment and initiative
  • Assertiveness, communication skills and negotiation skills
  • Further training in the areas of insurance, sales and management desired

What we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you the space to develop and fully exploit your potential. As a leading Swiss insurer, we offer ambitious people attractive career opportunities and entrepreneurial freedom.

  • Discount of up to 30% on AXA products
  • 5 weeks vacation
  • Fleet discount/refund of Half-Fare travelcard/GA travelcard (pro rata)
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Scope for independent action
  • Innovative structures, processes and modern tools
  • Well-founded, professional training and further education opportunities
