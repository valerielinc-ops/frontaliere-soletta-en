Full description

Build logistics from the beginning! For our new location in Oberbuchsiten, we are looking for logistics specialists who independently understand, safely execute and take responsibility for processes in a food business. In this way, you actively shape structures, standards and processes right from the start.

Luogo di lavoro: Lüchinger+Schmid, Halmackerstrasse 351, 4625 Oberbuchsiten

Important information

We only consider complete applications with CV, all employment certificates and EFZ certificate. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned. An application letter or video is desired for this position.

Mansioni

  • You pick and palletize finished products according to the order and ensure a timely delivery.
  • You prepare freight forwarding orders, handle them cleanly and ensure correct documentation.
  • You organize packing and packaging materials and ensure timely availability in daily business.
  • You process returns, register them in the lock warehouse and coordinate the further steps with the quality assurance.
  • You take over the collection and transfer of goods and materials with chauffeurs and ensure a smooth process.
  • You consistently comply with hygiene, quality and biosafety requirements and actively participate in the ongoing improvement of logistics processes at the new location.
  • You operate forklifts such as cross-seat and drawbar trucks safely in daily use.

Requirements

  • You have experience in a storage, picking or production logistics environment, ideally in the food industry. You understand logistical processes and their connections, from incoming goods to picking and packaging to transport and returns.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): You have completed vocational training in the logistics or warehouse sector or have several years of practical experience in this environment.
  • You have good to very good knowledge of German in word and writing, at least at level B2.
  • You implement work instructions and process specifications independently, correctly and reliably.
  • You have a valid forklift for cross-seat and/or drawbar trucks or are ready to purchase it at short notice.
  • You work reliably, carefully and responsibly and convince through teamwork and flexibility.
  • You are ready to work in two shift operation (early and late shift) as well as in the refrigerator at 4 degrees and at normal room temperature.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Reference information: When obtaining references, we ask for impressions, strengths and weaknesses again and have them verified by former supervisors or employees. References can be obtained after the first or second round and are only made with consent. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Final discussion before issuing the contract: Before the contract is issued, we hold a final discussion in order to clarify open questions of both sides and to regulate contractual details. If all points are clarified, the written offer follows. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
