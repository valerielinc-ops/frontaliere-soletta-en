Transport Coordinator: in short-term planning 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Transport Coordinator: in short-term planning Olten, 01.02.2027 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

In the Transport Plan team of SBB Cargo with 16 employees, you plan and coordinate the needs-based and efficient handling of freight transports and ensure a reliable transport chain. You work without shifts during office hours and with your contribution you noticeably contribute to the performance of freight transport. In a dedicated team, you develop pragmatic solutions, coordinate them with internal and external partners and drive improvements in our planning processes. You benefit from a wide range of development opportunities in logistics planning and from cross-divisional cooperation that helps you to advance professionally and personally. You can move that.

  • As Transport Coordinator, you take orders for freight transport, analyze the requirements and accompany the transport chain until the delivery to the dispatch.
  • With your team, you coordinate the use of rolling stock and personnel, check solution options and control efficient, cost-optimized resource use.
  • In close cooperation with internal and external partners, you ensure a coordinated transport chain and continuously coordinate the next steps.
  • You develop pragmatic, operationally feasible solutions, make the necessary decisions and prepare the transfer to the operational services.
  • You actively question existing processes and drive forward the continuous development and optimization of our planning processes.

You bring that with you.

  • You like to critically question existing processes, identify optimization potential and develop processes further. Your pronounced process understanding helps you.
  • You work independently and remain resilient even in demanding situations. You think networked and act solution-oriented.
  • Customer orientation and a safe eye for the optimal use of resources.
  • Several years of experience in planning or scheduling in rail transport is a mandatory requirement.
  • A completed basic vocational training EFZ and a tertiary further education in logistics, transport, business administration or process management.
  • Very good knowledge of German (at least C1) and ideally knowledge of French or Italian at least A2.
