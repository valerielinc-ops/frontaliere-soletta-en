What you achieve

  • You actively support the field sales force throughout the entire sales process and thus contribute to our shared success.
  • As a central contact person, you answer professional and technical questions in the areas of private non-life insurance, corporate non-life insurance, individual life, accident, health, and occupational pension plans (BVG).

  • With a sense for individual needs, you develop tailor-made concepts and solutions.
  • Together with the team, you actively shape market development and sales promotion.
  • You accompany our apprentices in their daily work and specifically promote their professional and personal development.

What you bring

  • Pleasure in direct customer contact and a pronounced service orientation
  • Sales-oriented thinking and enthusiasm for developing individual customer solutions
  • Efficient, solution-oriented collaboration with the field service and relevant specialist departments
