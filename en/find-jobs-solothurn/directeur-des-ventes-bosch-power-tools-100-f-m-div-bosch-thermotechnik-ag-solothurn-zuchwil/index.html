Full description

##

  • Your tasks

As Area Sales Manager, you will take responsibility for the expansion and further development of our Swiss business in the area of ​​power tools and measurement technology. You will be responsible for supporting and developing our specialist dealers and selected major customers, sustainably increasing sales and profits and implementing effective sales measures. In close collaboration with our cross-functional teams, you will ensure that our products are the first choice at the point of sale and for professional users. Your main tasks:

  • Managing the business: You have full sales and profit responsibility for your assigned Swiss specialist retailers and look after selected major customers. In doing so, you will sustainably expand existing customer relationships and business volumes.
  • Implement measures: You develop tailor-made customer concepts and implement effective sell-in and sell-out activities in a targeted manner.
  • Leverage potential: You carry out in-depth product and customer analyzes to identify and realize unused market and sales potential.
  • Keep an eye on the market: You closely monitor the market and competitive activities in the region and proactively derive measures.
  • Win together: You will work closely with our cross-functional teams to ensure sustainable overall success in the Swiss market.
  • Your profile

Training: sound commercial or technical-technical basic training with targeted further training in the areas of sales, distribution or marketing. Working style & personality: proactive thinking and acting, strong “can-do” mentality, happy to take on entrepreneurial responsibility, communication talent at all hierarchical levels, needs-oriented. Experience & know-how: well-founded experience and established network in the Swiss construction or ancillary construction industry and/or in the power tool trade, well-founded technical sales and consulting expertise Languages: fluent Swiss German (or business-fluent German), knowledge of English and/or French is a plus. Place of work: “Home office” with travel around 80%
