We are the bank within the Helvetia Baloise Group – that’s for sure. We combine professionalism, short decision-making paths and a culture in which people can take responsibility.
<br/ Payment transactions are one of the core services of a bank: it must function reliably at all times and is at the same time in constant change. As part of the payment team, you and your team ensure smooth operation. A task with responsibility, room for manoeuvre and a lot of impact. Tasks: • You process and control payment orders (domestic and foreign, SIC, SWIFT, LSV, standing orders and netting) efficiently and on time. • You advise customer consultants and end customers competently in specialist and support inquiries. • You will carry out complex investigations, research and recovery in a targeted and solution-oriented manner. • You are responsible for the first cut in the compliance filter and thus guarantee a secure, compliant processing. • You validate technical adjustments for system releases and interface developments. • You represent the department in projects and actively promote the continuous optimization of our operational processes. Requirements • Several years of professional experience in national and international payment transactions coupled with a sound process understanding • Precise, structured and reliable working methods with analytical foresight in complex situations • Team-oriented and communicative personality who values cross-divisional collaboration • High motivation to question the existing, to contribute your own ideas and to actively shape changes and automation • Solid user experience in Microsoft 365; Experience with Avaloq, Confluence and Jira is a big plus • stylish knowledge of German; Knowledge of French, Italian or English is an advantage We believe in cooperation at eye level, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
