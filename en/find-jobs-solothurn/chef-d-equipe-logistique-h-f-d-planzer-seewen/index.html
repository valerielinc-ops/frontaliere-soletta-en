Teamleiter Logistik (m/w/d) — Planzer

Full description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Senn.

Anchored in the Schwyz talc boiler for 100 years now, we move goods and ensure that they are delivered on time. With a direct rail connection and a strong connection with the Planzer network, we make logistics efficient and ecological. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

Tasks

  • You lead a team of up to 20 employees in the morning shift from about 4 a.m. to 1 p.m.
  • Together with your team and your manager, you ensure smooth and efficient shift operation
  • The proper goods inspection from goods receipt to goods exit is a matter of course for you
  • You plan the storage and handling area that is available to you
  • Help with loading and unloading is a matter of course for you, because you like to take part and check whether everything is correct
  • Occupational safety is your highest priority - you actively exemplify it and consistently implement it in your area of responsibility. You also attach great importance to order and discipline
  • Cases of damage and reservations in the case of non-compliant deliveries of goods you continue to report

Profile

  • You have in-depth professional and leadership experience in logistics or production
  • You are already a proud owner of a forklift card
  • You are familiar with the PC and are routine in dealing with the common hardware and software
  • Oral and written you can express yourself very well and stylishly in German language
  • Your efficient, independent and reliable way of working distinguishes you and you are happy to take on responsibility
  • You have flexibility, quality awareness and teamwork, you are a true organizational talent

Benefits

  • With us you start with 5 weeks of holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • With our annual map you can use the Rothenfluh- and Mythosbahn for free!

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
