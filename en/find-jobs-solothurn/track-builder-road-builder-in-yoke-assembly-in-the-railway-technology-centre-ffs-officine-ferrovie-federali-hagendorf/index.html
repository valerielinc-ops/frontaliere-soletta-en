You can look forward to that.

Welcome to the Bahntechnik Center (BTC) Hägendorf. On the assembly site, you put track yokes together, which are later installed directly on the railway line. With your daily work, you ensure that central elements of the railway infrastructure are precisely manufactured and function reliably. In this way, you make a direct and visible contribution to the safety and reliability of rail transport. You can expect a job with clear processes, responsibility for quality and safety as well as a team that lives cooperation and mutual support. At the same time, you have the opportunity to further develop your expertise and develop yourself. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for family - can be found on our website under this link. You can move that.

  • As a member of the team, you are actively involved in the implementation of technical work in the railway infrastructure sector and thus contribute to a safe and reliable railway operation.
  • You carry out mechanical assembly work professionally and safely handle tools and machines.
  • Your work is done in compliance with clear guidelines on quality, deadlines and safety.
  • You carefully check the completed work and ensure a clean execution.
  • In addition, you are involved in the team and support a smooth process in everyday work.

You bring that with you.

  • High security awareness and consistent work according to applicable security regulations are a matter of course for you.
  • You bring a high willingness to learn, initiative, a sense of responsibility and good communication skills. Teamwork is important to you.
  • You are physically resilient and enjoy using your craftsmanship every day.
  • We presuppose a completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ in the mechanical or technical area. If you already have experience in railway infrastructure or track construction, this is a big advantage.
