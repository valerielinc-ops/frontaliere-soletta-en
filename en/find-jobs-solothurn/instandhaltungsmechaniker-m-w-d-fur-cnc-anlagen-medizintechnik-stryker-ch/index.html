Full description

Work Flexibility: Onsite

We are looking for a maintenance mechanic (m/f/d) for CNC equipment and conventional equipment at the earliest possible time, who will support our team in the E xFix department at our location in Selzach (CH). In your new role, you will be mainly responsible for the maintenance of various investment systems, thus guaranteeing the production of high-quality and impeccable medical implants in accordance with customer requirements and taking into account current regulations regarding quality and environmental protection.

This post shall be filled indefinitely.

Our offer to you

  • Ensure the availability of production facilities in compliance with Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) requirements.
  • Perform fault analyses and correct machine faults independently or in cooperation with external service technicians.
  • Plan, organize and document preventive maintenance measures according to the maintenance plan.
  • Analyze plant and process data as well as implement measures to increase plant availability and machine performance.
  • Supporting environmental, health and safety (EHS) inquiries and clarifications.
  • Managing the spare parts inventory and supporting machine procurement and internal machine relocations.
  • Create work instructions and provide technical training for employees.
  • Design and development of devices and equipment for production plants.

That sets them apart. Required qualifications

  • Completed technical vocational training as a polymechanic, industrial mechanic, industrial electrician or comparable qualification.
  • Minimum 5 years of professional experience in a production environment.
  • Experience in maintenance, machine maintenance or service technology.
  • Experience in a regulated environment, such as medical technology or GMP-regulated production.
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Outlook, Excel and Word.
  • Very good knowledge of German and basic English.

Preferred qualifications

  • Swiss Federal Certificate and Connection Permit pursuant to Article 15 of the Low Voltage Installation Ordinance (NIV).
  • Knowledge of French.
