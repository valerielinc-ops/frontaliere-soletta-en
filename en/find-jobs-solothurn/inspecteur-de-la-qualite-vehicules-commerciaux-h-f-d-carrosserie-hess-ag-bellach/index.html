Quality inspector commercial vehicles (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to an extensive bus model range, HESS designs, builds and sells commercial vehicle bodies and conversions of all kinds.

To complement our team in the commercial vehicles division, we are looking for a motivated and experienced quality inspector at the production site in Subingen (SO). Are you a reliable personality who impresses with precision, a sense of responsibility and organizational talent. In this versatile role, you will ensure that our vehicles meet the highest quality standards when delivered to customers. Your tasks

  • Receipt of newly delivered vehicles as well as comprehensive and precise documentation of the respective vehicle condition
  • Creation and management of the quality documents (Q documents) of the vehicles and ensuring the required standards in all work steps
  • Carrying out quality controls on the assembled vehicles
  • Creation of vehicle dossiers for motor vehicle inspection (MFK) and maintenance of vehicle data in the internal work list
  • Seamless storage of vehicle data including comprehensive photo documentation
  • Provision of vehicles for delivery and preparation of the necessary documents
  • Support the production manager and the workshop manager with administrative and operational tasks

Our requirements

  • Completed training or relevant professional experience (ideally in automotive or technical areas)
  • Passion for vehicles and a good eye for details
  • Confident use of common office software (MS Office) and willingness to familiarize yourself with new systems
  • Structured, independent and extremely conscientious way of working
  • Communicative and helpful personality who enjoys working in a team
  • Valid driving license Cat. B

We offer you

  • Responsible and varied permanent position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • Interesting, varied and challenging tasks
  • Modern working environment
  • Sliding, flexible working hours
  • Performance-based employment conditions
  • Support with further training and development
