Calculator: in building and infrastructure construction 80-100 % Your contribution with us

  • You create calculations for challenging projects in building and infrastructure construction
  • You analyze tender documents and prepare submissions and offers
  • You determine quantities, calculate prices and develop service specifications
  • You collect offers from suppliers and subcontractors and purchase them
