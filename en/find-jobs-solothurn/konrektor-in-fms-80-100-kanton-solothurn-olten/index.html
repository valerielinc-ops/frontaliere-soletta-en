Full description

The Cantonal School Olten teaches about 1100 pupils at the Gymnasium, at the School of Applied Sciences (FMS), at the secondary level P and in the preliminary course PH and employs about 175 employees. For the school management of the Cantonal School Olten we are looking for a /-n Conrektor /-in FMS, 80-100% by 01.02.2027 or by agreement at the location Olten.

Your responsibility

  • You are a member of the school management.
  • You are responsible for the pedagogical and organizational management of the departments of technical school, specialist maturity health and social work as well as the preliminary course PH.
  • You take over the leadership of the assigned teachers and employees, implement the decisions of the school management as well as the cantonal requirements and actively shape the school and quality development.
  • Teaching is also part of your workload.

Your profile

  • They have completed higher education, a teaching diploma (Sec II) with several years of professional experience as a secondary school teacher, as well as education in school management or the willingness to catch up.
  • We expect a convincing leader with a strong will to shape and a high level of performance competence.
  • Familiarity with questions in the field of FMS/FM/pre-course PH and an awareness of cooperation with both donor and receiving institutions are among your strengths as well as the recognition and use of scope for action and an idea regarding the development of FMS.
  • Thanks to your strong communication skills, commitment and resilience, you and your team contribute to a flawless functioning school operation.
