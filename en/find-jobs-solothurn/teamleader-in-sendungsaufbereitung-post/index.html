This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

With you, we gain a motivated leader who acts as a role model and actively participates. Apply now and take responsibility!

You can do that.

  • Together with your deputy, you are responsible for the professional and personnel management of about 30 employees.
  • As a strong leadership duo, they work hand in hand, promote team development and maintain communication.
  • You create the personnel planning and are responsible for the shipment processing (working hours Monday to Friday between 16:00 and 01:15 and about 2x a month on Sunday between 17:00 and 22:00).
  • You integrate new employees into the team and support them in the introductory phase.
  • In order to fulfill your role model function, you actively participate in day-to-day business.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed basic training EFZ preferably in logistics.
  • The role as a role model and coach gives you pleasure and you are happy to take on management tasks. In addition, you are ready to train yourself in the field of leadership and to contribute what you have learned in practice.
  • You are an organizational talent and see change as an opportunity.
  • You communicate accurately and maintain a fair, appreciative approach to the team.
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German as well as a safe handling of MS-Office round off your profile.

With you, flexibility becomes everyday life. We promote progressive teamwork and value a balanced working life. This position can also be taken over by two persons in "co-management". You can apply directly as a duo or we support you in the search for a suitable person.
