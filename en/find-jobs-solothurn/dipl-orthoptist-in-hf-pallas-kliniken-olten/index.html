Full description

The Pallas Clinics are specialized in ophthalmology and aesthetics. With over 350 employees and 17 locations, we are one of the leading providers in Switzerland.

Our medical staff plays an indispensable role in the customer care process. They act as direct contacts and provide professional and individual support. The focus is always on the well-being of our customers. Through close cooperation with our specialists, we guarantee a holistic supply. Our team is characterized by high commitment, regular training and the pleasure of the interdisciplinary cooperation with our customers.

Do you love to provide orthopaedic care in a dedicated, established team? Our modern and generous eye practices are looking for you at our locations in the region of Aargau, Solothurn and Bern.

Our modern and generous eye practices look for you as

Dipl. Orthoptist/in HF

Responsibility

  • Independent examination, treatment, care and electronic documentation of strabological and neuroophthalmological patients
  • Care and treatment of small amblyopia patients
  • Care and examination of pre- and postoperative strabismus patients
  • On request also preoperative examination of refractive surgical patients
  • Perform kinetic perimetry (gold man or electronic kinetic program). No care of static perimetry
  • Implementation and interpretation of Hess (at best also Harmswand)
  • In close cooperation with the responsible ophthalmologists, the C-Skias and the ophthalmological examinations are carried out Independent examination, treatment, care and electronic documentation of strabological and neuroophthalmological patients
  • In close cooperation with the competent ophthalmologists, the C-Skias and the ophthalmological examinations are carried out

Requirements

  • You have completed training as a Dipl. Orthoptist/-in HF (for a foreign diploma, SRC recognition is necessary)
  • Your exact working method is reflected in the efficient examination and documentation
  • Empathic appearance, understandable education and resilience are also among your strengths. In addition, you are considered a responsible contact person to patients and doctors
  • Experience in ski ascopy and spectacles regulation is advantageous

Benefits

  • Our orthoptists enjoy a generous patient examination time
  • Due to our size, you can focus on the pure Orthoptik consultation time. No appointments, preliminary or diagnostic measurements and further work on the reception
  • Due to a great flexibility in the planning of holiday and consultation hours, family and leisure time are ideally compatible with the Orthoptik consultation hour.
  • In close cooperation with ophthalmologists during the consultation hours, you will experience a culture at eye level
  • For your daily work, standardized, versatile orthoptic work/examination equipment is available at the locations.
  • If interested, participation in the monthly internal colloquium
  • The visit of external further education and training is important to us and is financially supported
  • In addition, our locations are modern, rely on digital work and can be easily reached due to the proximity to the station.

We are happy to arrange the workload, working days and times flexibly, so that it is also feasible for you. If you like working with children but also adults, we should get to know each other.
