Stv. Head of Logistics (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company.

To complement our team in the area of operations, we are looking for a motivated and qualified personality for the warehouse/shipping department as Stv. Head of Logistics.

You appreciate the varied environment in logistics and you are an organizational talent. If you would also like to make a difference and contribute your many ideas to the ongoing projects, we have the ideal position for you! As Stv. Head of Logistics with around 45 employees in the fields of goods incoming, warehouse logistics and forwarding, you have a significant influence on our logistics processes and are an important position in the rapidly growing environment. Help with your expertise to perfect the logistics around the production of our high-quality electric buses!

Their tasks

  • Ensuring and monitoring day-to-day business in all operational processes as well as active assistance in day-to-day business
  • Introducing and implementing process optimizations
  • Support in physical warehouse optimization
  • Monitoring of missing parts and, if necessary, taking action
  • Organizational and personnel management of around 45 employees (in the absence of the Head of Logistics)
  • Active support for inventory work
  • Close cooperation with internal departments to ensure optimal material distribution for spare parts, production and assembly
  • Prepare data from the ERP in the EXCEL and format for further processing

Our requirements

  • Basic technical training in an industrial establishment
  • Further training in logistics
  • Many years of professional experience in the logistics environment
  • Experienced MS Office knowledge (especially advanced user knowledge in Excel)
  • Very good user experience in an ERP system
  • Demonstrated leadership experience and strong organisational skills
  • Independent operation and high load capacity
  • High level of teamwork and a strong sense of duty
  • Very good knowledge of German (English of advantage)

We offer them

  • Interesting and varied work in a successful traditional company
  • Challenging Leadership
  • Moving working time
  • Conditions of performance
  • Opportunity for further training and development
