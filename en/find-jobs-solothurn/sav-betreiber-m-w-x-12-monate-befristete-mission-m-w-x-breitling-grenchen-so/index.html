Full description

We are a committed Squad whose mission is to redefine chronometrics, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision, we create watches that combine a neo-retro design with casual, accessible and durable luxury — designed and designed by passionate teams around the world. Join our #Squad in Granges as Operator, VAS Operator on temporary mission and be part of this extraordinary mission! We are a team of 18 employees and are currently looking for our next, next colleague to complete our #Squad! You will be attached to the site of Granges . It's more than a VAS Operator position, it's the position in which you contribute directly to the quality of customer experience and the loyalty of our customers to the brand. In Breitling SA's SAV team, you play an essential role in the service process by carrying out technical operations on our watch products. You contribute directly to the quality and reliability of each watch entrusted to you, while ensuring that the Breitling standards are met in terms of quality, cost and time.

Your contribution

  • You adhere to all Breitling policies and procedures.
  • You read, review and apply the worksheets accurately to ensure that customer requirements are met.
  • You ensure the dismantling, winding and sealing control of the watch boxes during the various maintenance services.
  • You untie, refit, put up the dial and hands, test the performance of the movement and perform quality control on all Breitling products for which you have been trained.
  • You ensure that all documents related to the service are properly completed, attaching any required test documentation, before transferring the work to the next steps.
  • You contribute to the productivity of your unit, while respecting Breitling's quality, timeliness and cost standards.
  • You meet the agreed weekly production levels, according to the standard operating times defined by Breitling.
  • You perform any other task or respond to any other reasonable request required by the workshop manager. You think there's even more to find out about this job? You're right! Apply today to learn more! You will integrate perfectly with our team if:
  • You have a good experience as an operator in watchmaking (AFP Diploma, an additional asset).
  • You have a good level of concentration and a good understanding of customer expectations and requirements.
  • You are able to manage goals and meet deadlines.You like to work as a team, are proactive in nature and are focused on problem solving.
  • You have great versatility and good adaptability.
  • You have a training in assembly of watch cases and bracelets, which is an advantage.
  • You have training in dial and needle adjustment, which is an advantage.
  • You have a knowledge of Breitling products, which is a plus.
  • You have proven experience working on quality watches.
  • You speak French fluently. We invite you to apply even if you do not meet all of these criteria.
